India vs Pakistan hockey head-to-head

The rivalry between India and Pakistan stretches back decades. The teams have met five times in Men's Hockey World Cup history before the 2026 edition. India entered the latest World Cup meeting with a 3-2 advantage in those previous World Cup encounters.

Pakistan won the first World Cup meeting in 1971, while India defeated Pakistan in 1973 and in the 1975 World Cup final. Pakistan won the 1986 encounter, and India won the most recent World Cup meeting in 2010.

India vs Pakistan recent hockey form

The rivalry has remained competitive in recent international hockey, although India has enjoyed strong recent results against Pakistan. In the 2026 FIH Pro League, India defeated Pakistan 4-3 and later recorded a 7-1 victory in another meeting.

Those results give India a psychological advantage heading into the World Cup clash, although World Cup matches can be very different because qualification pressure is much higher.
