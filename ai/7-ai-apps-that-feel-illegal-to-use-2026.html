7 AI Apps That Feel Illegal to Use in 2026

AI tools are becoming unbelievably powerful. Some apps can write, design, code, edit videos, summarize books, and even replace entire teams of workers. In this article, we explore 7 AI apps that feel almost illegal because of how useful they are.

Live Updates: This list was updated in May 2026 with the latest AI tools and download links.

1. ChatGPT

ChatGPT is one of the most advanced AI assistants available today. It can answer questions, write articles, generate code, help students study, create business ideas, and even summarize long PDFs in seconds.

Students use it for homework help while creators use it for content generation and scripting.

Official Website: https://openai.com

How to Download: Visit the official website or download the mobile app from the Play Store or App Store.

2. Gemini AI

Gemini by Google is a powerful AI system that integrates deeply with Android, Gmail, Docs, and YouTube. It can summarize emails, create travel plans, and generate content instantly.

Latest updates include real-time internet browsing and image generation tools.

Official Website: https://gemini.google.com

3. Claude AI

Claude AI is becoming famous for its extremely human-like writing quality. Many bloggers and professionals now use it to create articles, emails, scripts, and research summaries.

It also supports long documents better than many competitors.

Official Website: https://www.anthropic.com

4. Canva AI

Canva AI can generate presentations, thumbnails, social media posts, logos, and posters in seconds. Many YouTubers use it daily to create professional content without hiring designers.

It is especially popular among students and small business owners.

Official Website: https://www.canva.com

5. Notion AI

Notion AI can summarize notes, organize projects, generate ideas, and automate productivity tasks. It is widely used by startups and students.

Recent updates added AI meeting summaries and smart task generation.

Official Website: https://www.notion.so/product/ai

6. Runway ML

Runway ML is one of the most advanced AI video tools on the internet. It can remove backgrounds, generate cinematic videos, and create animations using only text prompts.

Content creators use it to save hours of editing work.

Official Website: https://runwayml.com

7. Perplexity AI

Perplexity AI combines search engines with AI answers. Instead of giving only links, it directly answers questions with summarized information and references.

Many users now prefer it over traditional search engines for research purposes.

Official Website: https://www.perplexity.ai

Latest AI Trends in 2026

AI apps are rapidly replacing traditional software. New updates now include voice assistants, AI video generation, automatic coding tools, and real-time translation features.

Experts believe AI productivity tools will continue growing throughout 2026 and beyond.
