IAS Divya Mittal Resigns After 13 Years: Facts and Figures

IAS officer Divya Mittal has resigned from the Indian Administrative Service after 13 years of administrative service. Mittal, a 2013-batch Indian Administrative Service officer of the Uttar Pradesh cadre, announced her resignation on August 30, 2026, through a social media post. Multiple reports confirmed that she voluntarily decided to end her IAS career. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

At the time of her resignation, Divya Mittal was serving as Special Secretary in the Revenue Department of the Uttar Pradesh government. Before moving to the Revenue Department, she served as District Magistrate of Deoria. She had also served as District Magistrate of Mirzapur and Sant Kabir Nagar during her administrative career. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
