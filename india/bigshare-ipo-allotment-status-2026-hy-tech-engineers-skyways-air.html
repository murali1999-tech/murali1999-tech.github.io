Bigshare IPO Allotment Status 2026: Check Latest Allotment Details Online

Bigshare Services Private Limited is the registrar for several IPOs in the Indian primary market, and investors can use its official allotment portal to check whether shares have been allotted to their applications. As of August 29, 2026, two recently active IPOs handled by Bigshare are Hy-Tech Engineers and Skyways Air Services. Both had their basis of allotment scheduled for August 28, 2026. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

For Hy-Tech Engineers, the IPO received extremely strong demand before closing. The final reported subscription was 244.41 times overall. The Qualified Institutional Buyer category was subscribed 255.77 times, the Non-Institutional Investor category was subscribed 402.29 times and the Retail Individual Investor category was subscribed 170.58 times. The issue offered 2,56,10,205 shares across the relevant investor categories. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

The Hy-Tech Engineers IPO was open from August 24 to August 27, 2026, with a price band of ₹50 to ₹53 per share. The basis of allotment was scheduled for August 28, refunds were scheduled to begin on August 31, shares were expected to be credited to successful applicants on August 31 and the shares were scheduled to list on September 1, 2026. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Investors who applied for Hy-Tech Engineers can check their allotment through Bigshare Services, as well as through the stock exchanges. The registrar's system can be used to search using details such as PAN, application number or DP/Client ID. The result displayed by the registrar is the official application-level allotment information. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Skyways Air Services is another recent IPO for which Bigshare Services is the registrar. Its IPO was open from August 24 to August 27, 2026, with a price band of ₹131 to ₹138 per share. The issue size was ₹582.80 crore and the final reported overall subscription was 71.25 times. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Skyways Air Services recorded subscription of 139.69 times from QIB investors, 87.24 times from NIIs and 25.40 times from retail investors. The IPO offered 4,22,31,600 shares. The allotment date was August 28, while refunds and demat credit were scheduled for August 31. The listing was scheduled for September 1, 2026, on NSE and BSE. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

To check a Bigshare IPO allotment, investors should use the official Bigshare Services IPO status facility and select the relevant company. Depending on the IPO, the portal can ask for PAN, application number or DP/Client ID details. Investors should carefully verify the company name before submitting their information because Bigshare handles allotments for multiple issues. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

If the allotment status shows that shares have been allotted, the next step is normally the credit of those shares into the investor's demat account according to the IPO timetable. If no shares are allotted, the blocked application amount is scheduled to be released according to the applicable refund timeline. Investors should check their bank and demat accounts if the registrar status and account status appear to differ temporarily.

The very high subscription for Hy-Tech Engineers means retail applicants faced significant competition for the available shares. A high subscription multiple does not mean every valid application receives shares. In heavily oversubscribed IPOs, the retail allotment process can result in many applicants receiving no shares even when their applications are valid.

Investors should also be careful with grey market premium figures. GMP is unofficial and is not the same as the actual listing price. The final listing price is determined by market trading after the shares begin trading on the exchange. Therefore, investors should not consider GMP a guaranteed return or a confirmed listing value.

The most reliable way to know whether an IPO application was successful is to check the official registrar or exchange application-status facility. Investors should not share their UPI PIN, trading password, demat password or banking credentials with anyone claiming to provide an IPO allotment service.

As of August 29, 2026, the key Bigshare IPO allotment dates are therefore August 28 for the basis of allotment of Hy-Tech Engineers and Skyways Air Services, August 31 for scheduled refunds and demat credit, and September 1 for the scheduled listing of both issues.
