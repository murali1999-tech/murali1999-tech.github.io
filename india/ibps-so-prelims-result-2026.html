IBPS SO Prelims Result 2026 Out: Check Result Link, Scorecard and Mains Exam Details

Read more updates here

October 15, 2026

The IBPS SO prelims result 2026 has been announced, bringing an important update for candidates who appeared for the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection Specialist Officer examination. Candidates can now visit the official IBPS website and check their qualifying status for the next stage of the recruitment process.

The result relates to the Common Recruitment Process for Specialist Officers, CRP SPL-XVI. The preliminary examination was conducted on August 29, 2026. Candidates who qualify in the prelims can move forward to the mains examination, subject to the official eligibility and selection rules.

Applicants should use the official IBPS website rather than relying on unofficial result pages or social media messages. The official result announcement is available at IBPS SO XVI preliminary result status. Candidates can also visit the IBPS official website for recruitment notices and updates.

Candidates should keep their examination details ready before checking the result.

To check the IBPS SO prelims result, open the official IBPS website and look for the result status notice for CRP SPL-XVI. Follow the result link provided there. You will generally need your registration number or roll number and the password or date of birth requested on the login page. Enter the details carefully and complete any security verification shown on the screen.

After logging in, check whether your status shows that you have qualified for the mains examination. Read the information displayed on the page and save a copy of the result or scorecard if a download option is available. It is sensible to keep a digital copy and, if needed, print it for future reference.

If the website is slow or does not open, wait for some time and try again. A large number of candidates may visit the portal after a result announcement. Check that you have entered the correct registration details and date of birth. Do not share your password, date of birth or other personal login information with unknown websites or people claiming to provide results.

The Specialist Officer recruitment process is intended for several specialist roles in participating banks. Depending on the post, these can include IT Officer, Agricultural Field Officer, Rajbhasha Adhikari, Law Officer, HR or Personnel Officer and Marketing Officer. Candidates should refer to the official notification for the specific requirements and selection stages for the post they applied for.

Qualifying in the prelims is an important step, but it does not mean that the final appointment has been confirmed. Candidates who qualify must prepare for the next stage and follow the instructions issued by IBPS. The mains examination and any subsequent interview or selection procedures applicable to the post will determine further progress in the recruitment process.

Candidates who qualify should continue preparing for the next examination stage.

The IBPS SO mains examination is scheduled for November 1, 2026, according to the published recruitment schedule. Candidates should check their official notices and call letters for the confirmed reporting time, examination centre, permitted documents and other instructions. It is best not to depend on dates shared only through unofficial messages.

Those who have qualified should begin revising the syllabus for their particular Specialist Officer post. The subjects and professional knowledge requirements differ by role, so candidates should follow the relevant official syllabus and examination pattern. Practising previous questions, revising important concepts and taking timed mock tests can help candidates identify topics that need more attention.

Candidates who do not qualify in this attempt can still use the experience to improve their preparation for future banking examinations. Reviewing time management, accuracy and weaker subjects may help with the next attempt. Keep checking official recruitment notices for future vacancies, application dates and examination schedules.

Be careful with claims about cut-off marks and scorecards. The cut-off can vary according to the post, category and recruitment rules. Candidates should not assume that a score shared in a coaching group or social media post is official. Check the scorecard and any cut-off information published by IBPS itself before drawing conclusions about your performance.

For the latest and most reliable information, visit www.ibps.in. Use the result link published by IBPS and follow the instructions on the official login page. Keep your registration number, roll number and other examination details safe until the recruitment process is complete.

In summary, the IBPS SO prelims result 2026 was announced on October 15. Candidates who appeared for CRP SPL-XVI can check their qualifying status through the official IBPS result page. Those who qualify should prepare for the mains examination scheduled for November 1 and continue monitoring official announcements for scorecard, cut-off and further recruitment details.
