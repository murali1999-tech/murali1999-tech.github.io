Friday, October 16, 2026, brings a fresh collection of movies and shows to streaming platforms, giving viewers plenty of options for their weekend watchlist. The reported OTT releases include Masterplan, DC, Tenzing, Aasha, Money Trap: The Karun Treasure and Sacrifice. These titles cover crime, comedy, psychological thriller, adventure and biographical drama, with releases associated with services such as Amazon Prime Video, SonyLIV, Apple TV, ZEE5, Netflix and Lionsgate Play. Streaming availability can vary by region, so viewers should check the official platform listing before watching.

For people searching for October 16 OTT releases, Friday OTT release movies or new movies to watch this weekend, this guide covers the main reported titles, their stories and where to look for them. The lineup includes international productions as well as regional Indian cinema, making it a varied week for streaming audiences.

Masterplan is one of the notable films arriving on Amazon Prime Video. The crime comedy stars Stanley Tucci as Roger, a retired master thief who plans to steal the Mona Lisa. He recruits an Italian hacker played by Simona Tabasco and a French explosives expert played by Victor Belmondo. The heist becomes more complicated when Roger reveals a surprising family connection to his recruits. The combination of a high-profile art theft, unusual partnerships and family secrets makes Masterplan a title to consider for viewers who enjoy crime comedies.

DC is a Tamil crime thriller reported for SonyLIV. The film features Lokesh Kanagaraj as Devadas, an outlaw whose life changes after a robbery goes wrong. While trying to escape the police, he forms an alliance with Chandra, played by Wamiqa Gabbi. Their partnership takes them deeper into a dangerous criminal world. The film is described as a fresh reimagining inspired by Sarat Chandra Chattopadhyay's classic Devdas, presenting a darker story built around crime, survival and complicated relationships. Viewers interested in Tamil films and dubbed regional releases should check SonyLIV for the available language options.

Tenzing is a biographical drama associated with Apple TV, arriving for streaming on October 16. The film follows Tenzing Norgay, the Sherpa mountaineer who reached the summit of Mount Everest with Sir Edmund Hillary in 1953. Genden Phuntsok plays Tenzing, while Tom Hiddleston portrays Hillary. The story explores the historic expedition as well as Tenzing's personal challenges and his struggle for recognition. It may appeal to audiences who enjoy true stories, mountaineering history and films about extraordinary human achievements.

Aasha is a Malayalam psychological thriller reported for ZEE5. Urvashi plays the central character, a widow whose life takes a disturbing turn after a minor road accident leads to a confrontation with a volatile man named Jeemon, played by Joju George. What begins as an ordinary dispute develops into a tense personal conflict. The film's premise focuses on fear, determination and the consequences of a confrontation that refuses to end. Reports list additional dubbed-language options, but viewers should confirm the current audio availability on ZEE5.

Money Trap: The Karun Treasure is another reported international release associated with Netflix. The Turkish crime comedy follows scam artist Asım Noyan, who reunites with his former gang after obtaining a map linked to the legendary Treasure of Karun. Their attempt to carry out one final scheme becomes complicated when rival gangs and other treasure hunters enter the picture. The film blends a treasure hunt with deception, criminal plans and unexpected twists. Netflix's regional catalogue should be checked to confirm availability in India.

Sacrifice is a satirical thriller featuring Chris Evans, Anya Taylor-Joy, Salma Hayek Pinault and Vincent Cassel. Its story centres on a charity gala on a Greek island that is taken over by a radical group. The wealthy guests become hostages as the group's leader attempts to carry out a dangerous plan connected to a global crisis. The film combines suspense, dark humour and social commentary. Reports differ over its streaming platform, with some listings naming Lionsgate Play and others listing Netflix. Check the service's official catalogue before assuming it is available on a particular platform in India.

Alongside these films, viewers may also be interested in Roadies Rebirth, the reported new season of the reality competition franchise. The programme is associated with JioHotstar, with reports also mentioning Netflix and television availability. The season brings contestants into a competition built around challenges, team dynamics and strategy. Fans should check the official streaming service for the premiere time and episode schedule.

Another title appearing in some release calendars is Final Destination Bloodlines, the supernatural horror film. Its reported October 16 streaming date and Indian Netflix availability should be verified directly, as international release schedules and regional catalogues can differ. Horror fans should check the Netflix app or official title page for confirmation before planning a watch.
