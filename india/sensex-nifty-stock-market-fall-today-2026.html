Sensex Nifty Today: Why Is the Stock Market Down?

Read more updates

Indian stock markets came under pressure on September 11, 2026, with the Sensex and Nifty 50 falling sharply during early trading. The benchmarks later recovered some losses but still finished lower.

The Sensex closed at 74,781.76, down 120.83 points, while the Nifty 50 ended at 23,398.10, down 79.70 points. Rising crude oil prices and geopolitical tensions were among the main concerns for investors.

Brent crude moved above $108 a barrel, raising concerns about inflation and India's import costs. Weak global markets, higher bond yields and foreign investor selling also added pressure to sentiment.
