Leylah Fernandez: Why She Is in the News Today After Singapore Title and Wuhan Open Win

Canadian tennis player Leylah Fernandez is in the news after an impressive run of results on the Asian tennis swing. She won the Singapore Tennis Open in September and has continued competing at the Wuhan Open in China. Her latest victory came on October 14, when she defeated Chinese qualifier Han Shi 6-3, 6-4 to reach the next round of the WTA 1000 tournament. The result has brought renewed attention to her form and her efforts to finish the season strongly. Read the match report from Tennis Canada.

The biggest reason Fernandez attracted attention recently was her Singapore Tennis Open title on September 27, 2026. She defeated Australia's Talia Gibson 7-5, 6-0 in the final, winning the last nine games of the match. The victory gave Fernandez her first WTA singles title of the season and the sixth title of her professional career. It was also her second WTA 500-level singles title. The official WTA report has the final result and tournament details.

Her performance in Singapore was especially notable because she had to overcome difficult opponents on the way to the trophy. In the quarterfinals, Fernandez defeated top seed Mirra Andreeva 6-2, 7-5. She then recovered after losing the first set against Maja Chwalinska in the semifinals, winning 1-6, 6-4, 6-2. Fernandez carried that momentum into the final, where she took control after a close opening set against Gibson.

The Singapore title was important for more than the trophy itself. It gave Fernandez a confidence boost after a challenging season and showed that she could compete with highly ranked opponents. Her win over Andreeva was another significant result against a top-10 player. Fernandez has built a reputation for fighting hard in long rallies and finding ways to change the direction of a match even when she is under pressure.

Her latest news comes from Wuhan, where she has been playing in the WTA 1000 event. Fernandez began her campaign with a three-set victory over Ekaterina Alexandrova on October 12. She followed that up with a straight-sets win over Han Shi on October 14. Fernandez won the match 6-3, 6-4, showing patience in the rallies and improving her attacking play as the match progressed. The victory kept her campaign alive at one of the major tournaments of the Asian swing.

Fernandez had to work for the Wuhan victory. Han Shi had already produced a notable result by defeating Alexandra Eala in the previous round, so the Canadian could not take the match lightly. Fernandez converted important break points and handled the pressure when the score became tight. Although the match was competitive, she won in two sets and advanced without needing a deciding set.

The Wuhan tournament matters because it is part of the WTA 1000 level, one of the most important categories of events outside the Grand Slams. Players can earn valuable ranking points and face strong opposition. A good run at this level can help improve a player's position in the rankings and build momentum before the end of the season.

Fernandez was also in the headlines earlier in the Asian swing because of her results in Beijing. After winning in Singapore, she travelled to China for the China Open but lost in the second round to Kamilla Rakhimova on October 3. She then moved on to Wuhan and returned to winning form. This sequence shows why tennis news can change quickly: one tournament may end in disappointment, while the next offers another opportunity.

Born in Canada, Leylah Annie Fernandez is known internationally for reaching the women's singles final of the 2021 US Open. She made an outstanding run through that tournament, defeating several highly ranked opponents before finishing as runner-up. Although she did not win the final, that performance established her as one of the most exciting young players on the women's tour.

Fernandez is a left-handed player whose game is built around movement, sharp angles and aggressive groundstrokes. She is often comfortable in extended rallies and can redirect the ball to open up the court. Her determination is another part of her playing style. The Singapore semifinal was a good example: after a difficult first set, she adjusted her approach and won the next two sets to reach the final.

Her career has also included success in doubles and team competitions. Fernandez helped Canada win the Billie Jean King Cup in 2023, an important achievement for Canadian women's tennis. She has continued to compete across the tour and has won singles titles in different countries. Her Singapore victory added another trophy to that record and continued her strong connection with tournaments in Asia.

Fans following Fernandez should keep an eye on her upcoming Wuhan matches, as each round brings a tougher challenge. Her recent results suggest she has found useful rhythm, but success in a WTA 1000 event requires consistent performance against a deep field. Serve accuracy, return pressure and the ability to manage long rallies will all matter as the tournament progresses.

For now, Leylah Fernandez is in the news for two clear reasons: her breakthrough Singapore title in September and her recent progress at the Wuhan Open in October. The Singapore trophy gave her a sixth career WTA singles title, while her win over Han Shi continued her run in China. Tennis fans will be watching to see whether she can carry this momentum into the later rounds and end the season with more strong results.
