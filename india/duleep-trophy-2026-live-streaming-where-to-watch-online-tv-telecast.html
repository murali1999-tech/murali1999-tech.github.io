Duleep Trophy 2026 Opening Matches

The Duleep Trophy begins on Sunday, August 23, with two quarter-final matches. Both fixtures are scheduled to begin at 9:30 a.m. Indian Standard Time.

The opening fixtures are important because the winners will progress to the semi-finals. Central Zone and South Zone have already qualified directly for the semi-final stage based on their previous tournament status.

Six Teams in Duleep Trophy 2026

The 2026 Duleep Trophy features six regional teams: Central Zone, South Zone, East Zone, West Zone, North Zone and North East Zone.

The tournament follows a knockout-style structure. Four teams compete in the quarter-finals, while Central Zone and South Zone enter directly at the semi-final stage.

The quarter-final winners will join the two directly qualified teams in the last four. The semi-finals are scheduled to take place from August 30 to September 2.

Key Players to Watch

The tournament features several players who could attract significant attention from cricket fans and selectors. East Zone includes Ishan Kishan, Mohammed Shami, Mukesh Kumar and young batter Vaibhav Sooryavanshi.

West Zone is led by Ruturaj Gaikwad and includes players such as Prithvi Shaw and Shardul Thakur. North Zone also features several players with experience in Indian domestic and international cricket.

Central Zone includes Rajat Patidar and Rinku Singh, while South Zone has Tilak Varma, Karun Nair and Shreyas Gopal among its prominent names.

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi in Focus

One of the biggest attractions of the opening round is Vaibhav Sooryavanshi. The young batter is part of the East Zone squad and enters the Duleep Trophy with considerable attention surrounding his development in red-ball cricket.

His performances will be closely followed by fans because the Duleep Trophy provides an important platform for young players to demonstrate their ability in first-class cricket.

Duleep Trophy Semi-Finals

The semi-finals are scheduled from August 30 to September 2. Central Zone and South Zone are already placed in the semi-final stage, while the remaining two places will be decided by the quarter-final results.

Both semi-finals are scheduled to be played at the BCCI Centre of Excellence in Bengaluru. Match timings are scheduled for 9:30 a.m. IST.

Duleep Trophy 2026 Final

The Duleep Trophy final is scheduled from September 6 to September 10. The final has been moved from Bengaluru to the M.A. Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai.

The venue change was made to allow spectators to attend the marquee domestic fixture. The move also gives cricket fans an opportunity to watch the tournament decider inside a major cricket stadium.

The final is scheduled to begin at 9:30 a.m. IST and will be one of the key televised matches of the competition.
