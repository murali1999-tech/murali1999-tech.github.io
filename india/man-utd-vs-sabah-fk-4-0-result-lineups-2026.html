Man Utd vs Sabah: 4-0 Result, Lineups and Latest News

Read more updates

Manchester United made a strong return to the Champions League with a 4-0 victory over Sabah at Old Trafford on September 10, 2026. The result gave United an impressive start in the league phase, while Sabah faced a difficult night against the English club.

Matheus Cunha opened the scoring in the 27th minute before Bruno Fernandes doubled the advantage in the 42nd minute. Benjamin Sesko added another goal just before half-time, giving Manchester United a comfortable 3-0 lead. Lisandro Martinez completed the scoring in the second half.

Manchester United started with Senne Lammens, Noussair Mazraoui, Leny Yoro, Lisandro Martinez, Patrick Dorgu, Youri Tielemans, Kobbie Mainoo, Bryan Mbeumo, Bruno Fernandes, Matheus Cunha and Benjamin Sesko.

Sabah included Stas Pokatilov, Akim Zedadka, Rahman Dashdamirov, Aden McCarthy, Tymoteusz Puchacz, Umarali Rakhmonaliev, Ivan Lepinjica, Kaheem Parris, Veljko Simic, Christian Nwachukwu and Joy-Lance Mickels.
