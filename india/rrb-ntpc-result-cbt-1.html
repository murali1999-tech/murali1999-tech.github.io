Beware of Fake RRB NTPC Result Websites

During major examination results, many unofficial websites publish result-related articles. Candidates should be careful before entering registration numbers, passwords, dates of birth or other personal information. The official Railway Recruitment Board websites should be preferred whenever a result is announced.

Candidates should also avoid websites or individuals asking for money in exchange for releasing a result, changing marks or guaranteeing selection. Railway recruitment results are published through official channels, and candidates should follow the instructions contained in the official notification.
