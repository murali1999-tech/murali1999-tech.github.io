Jailer 2 Review: Rajinikanth Movie Rating, Release Date, Hindi Version and Box Office

Jailer 2 has finally arrived in theatres, bringing Rajinikanth back as the much-loved Tiger Muthuvel Pandian. The sequel to the 2023 blockbuster Jailer was released on October 15, 2026. Directed by Nelson Dilipkumar, the film has attracted considerable attention from fans eager to see whether the superstar can recreate the success of the original. Early reviews are mixed to positive, with praise for Rajinikanth's screen presence, Anirudh Ravichander's music and the film's action sequences, although some reviewers have raised concerns about the pacing.

The biggest attraction is undoubtedly Rajinikanth. His familiar style, powerful dialogue delivery and dramatic entrances remain central to the film. The director understands that audiences come to a Rajinikanth movie for entertainment, memorable moments and the excitement of watching the superstar on the big screen. Several early reviews suggest that the sequel delivers these elements, particularly during its major action sequences and interval block.

Nelson Dilipkumar returns as writer and director after the success of the first Jailer. The sequel continues the story of retired jailer Tiger Muthuvel Pandian, whose peaceful life is disturbed by a new threat from the criminal underworld. The situation forces him to return to action to protect his family and legacy. The premise provides room for suspense, comedy, dramatic confrontations and the larger-than-life moments that fans expect from this franchise.

One of the film's strongest talking points is its supporting cast. Vidya Balan joins the franchise, while S. J. Suryah and Mithun Chakraborty add new characters to the story. Ramya Krishnan returns as Ashtalakshmi, and the wider cast includes familiar faces from the original film and other Indian film industries. The appearances of actors such as Mohanlal and Shiva Rajkumar have also generated excitement among fans who enjoy seeing popular stars share the screen.

Anirudh Ravichander's music is another major reason audiences are interested in Jailer 2. His background scores have played an important role in the success of several Rajinikanth films, building excitement around entrances, confrontations and action scenes. Early reactions have praised the energetic score and its contribution to the theatrical experience. For many fans, the music and sound design are important parts of what makes a mass entertainer enjoyable.

The film's comedy is also receiving attention. Nelson is known for combining dark humour with action and unusual character interactions. Early audience reactions suggest that some comic sequences work particularly well, helping balance the intense scenes. However, not every part of the narrative has received the same response, with some viewers feeling that the story slows down at certain points.

Published reviews offer different ratings. Pinkvilla awarded the film 4 out of 5, praising Rajinikanth's screen presence, surprise appearances and Anirudh's score. India Today gave it 3 out of 5, highlighting the superstar's style and Nelson's humour. Bollywood Hungama also rated it 3 out of 5, describing it as a commercial entertainer. Telugu360 gave the movie 2.75 out of 5. These ratings show that critics broadly recognise its entertainment value while differing on how effectively the story holds together.

Audience reactions on opening day have also been enthusiastic in several theatres, particularly during the major action moments and interval sequence. Some viewers have described the film as a celebration of Rajinikanth's screen presence, while others have expressed disappointment with the pace of the second half. Social media reactions are subjective and may change as more people watch the film, so it is worth considering full reviews rather than relying on a few viral posts.

For viewers wondering whether Jailer 2 is worth watching, the answer depends on what they expect from the film. Fans who enjoy Rajinikanth's signature style, dramatic elevations, action, comedy and star appearances may find it entertaining. Those who prefer tightly paced storytelling and a more restrained approach may be less satisfied with certain sections. The film's nearly three-hour theatrical experience also means that pacing may be an important factor for some viewers.

The theatrical release date is October 15, 2026. The film is directed by Nelson Dilipkumar and produced by Sun Pictures under Kalanithi Maran. It was released with a U/A 16+ certificate, and listings have advertised a runtime of approximately 2 hours and 45 minutes to 2 hours and 55 minutes, depending on the listing. Moviegoers should check the duration and language shown by their local cinema before booking.

The Hindi version has been a topic of discussion ahead of release. Reports indicated that the Hindi-dubbed film would not be screened by major chains such as PVR INOX and Cinepolis because of a disagreement over the proposed streaming release window. Availability can vary by cinema and location, so Hindi-speaking viewers should check their local listings rather than assume that every theatre will offer the Hindi version. Early fan enthusiasm is encouraging, but it should not be confused with confirmed box office revenue.

Jailer 2 is one of the major Tamil cinema releases of October 2026, with Rajinikanth's return and Anirudh's music providing much of its appeal. The early critical response suggests a commercial entertainer with memorable star moments, humour and action, but some uneven pacing. For existing fans of the first Jailer, the sequel offers another opportunity to watch Tiger Muthuvel Pandian in action. For other viewers, the mix of reviews provides a useful guide to what to expect before purchasing a ticket.

Overall, Jailer 2 looks most suited to audiences who enjoy larger-than-life Indian action films and Rajinikanth's distinctive screen persona. Its final box office performance and longer-term audience verdict will become clearer over the coming days. For now, moviegoers should check current showtimes, language availability and the latest reviews before deciding whether to watch it in theatres.
