Dussehra, also called Vijayadashami, is being celebrated across much of India on Tuesday, October 20, 2026. The festival represents the victory of good over evil and is associated with Lord Rama's victory over Ravana as well as Goddess Durga's victory over Mahishasura. Families observe prayers, visit temples, take part in community celebrations and attend events featuring the symbolic burning of Ravana effigies.

One of the most searched questions is the Dussehra puja muhurat for 2026. According to the Drik Panchang listing for Proddatur, Andhra Pradesh, Vijay Muhurat is approximately 1:58 pm to 2:45 pm on October 20. The precise time can vary by location, so people should check a local panchang for their town before planning a ritual. The Dashami tithi continues into October 21 in many calculations, and some regional observances follow different dates. In particular, Bengal observes Vijayadashami on October 21 in the cited calendar.

Ravan Dahan usually takes place in the evening during local celebrations, often after sunset. The exact event time depends on the organisers and the city.
