BPSC TRE 4.0: 32,388 Teacher Vacancies

The Bihar Public Service Commission has announced the fourth phase of its School Teacher Recruitment Examination, known as TRE 4.0. According to reports on the recruitment notification, a total of 32,388 teacher posts are available under Advertisement No. 14/2026.

BPSC TRE 4.0 key update: Online applications are scheduled to begin on September 1, 2026. Candidates should carefully check the official notification for eligibility, subject requirements, application fees and other conditions before applying.

The recruitment is being conducted for teaching positions under Bihar's Education Department. It is expected to attract a large number of candidates because government teaching jobs remain highly sought after across the state.

Reports indicate that the application window will open on September 1. The deadline for fee payment is September 29, while the application submission deadline is September 30, 2026.

Niu Lai Chinese Movie Box Office

Another unusual story gaining international attention is the Chinese animated movie Niu Lai, also known as The Ox Comes or The Cow is Coming.

The film has attracted attention because of its extremely unusual journey from an obscure low-budget production to a viral box-office success. The animation has been widely criticised online for its crude visual style and unconventional presentation. Instead of destroying interest in the film, however, the criticism helped generate curiosity among Chinese audiences.

According to recent reports, the movie was created by a mother-and-son team over several years with an extremely small budget. Its initial theatrical performance was tiny, with only a few thousand yuan in early earnings.

The situation changed after clips and discussions about the film spread across Chinese social media. Viewers began purchasing tickets partly because they wanted to see the movie that had become an internet sensation.

Why Niu Lai became viral: Negative online reactions, memes, curiosity and social-media discussions unexpectedly turned the low-budget animation into a box-office phenomenon.

Recent reports put its earnings at more than 14.9 million yuan, although box-office figures can continue changing as the theatrical run develops. Other reports have cited different totals at different stages of its release.

The Niu Lai story is an interesting example of how internet culture can influence movie audiences. Traditionally, expensive advertising campaigns and large production budgets are considered important for a successful theatrical release. Niu Lai demonstrates that viral attention can sometimes create an entirely different route to commercial success.

Why Niu Lai Is Being Compared With Big Movies

The unusual success of Niu Lai has received even more attention because it has appeared in the Chinese box-office conversation alongside much larger international productions.

The contrast is striking. A film produced by only two people with a tiny budget has managed to attract audiences while major Hollywood productions have been competing for Chinese viewers.

The film's success also raises an interesting question about modern entertainment: can audiences turn a movie's weaknesses into its biggest marketing advantage? In the case of Niu Lai, online criticism appears to have generated curiosity rather than simply discouraging viewers.

Skoda Slavia Facelift Launch in India

Automobile enthusiasts in India are also watching August 18 closely because Skoda is launching the updated Slavia facelift.

The facelift brings styling and equipment changes to Skoda's popular midsize sedan. The company is expected to use the update to make the Slavia more competitive against models such as the Volkswagen Virtus, Hyundai Verna and Honda City.

The refreshed model is expected to receive revised exterior styling, including changes to the grille, lighting elements and bumpers. Updated alloy wheels and other visual changes are also part of the facelift's expected package.

New Automatic Gearbox

One of the most significant mechanical changes expected for the Slavia facelift is a new 8-speed automatic transmission for the 1.0-litre TSI petrol engine. The existing 1.0 TSI engine produces around 116hp and 178Nm and is expected to continue.

The more powerful 1.5-litre TSI petrol engine is also expected to remain available, producing around 150hp and 250Nm. The 1.5 TSI is paired with manual and dual-clutch automatic transmission options.

Slavia Interior and Features

The cabin is expected to retain several features already associated with the Slavia, including a 10.25-inch touchscreen infotainment system, wireless smartphone charging, automatic climate control with rear vents, a single-pane sunroof and an eight-speaker audio system.

Some versions may receive updated upholstery and additional convenience features. A larger digital driver's display and front parking sensors have also been reported as possible additions.

Pricing is an important factor for buyers. The outgoing Slavia was priced from around ₹10 lakh to ₹18.19 lakh ex-showroom before the facelift, although the updated model's final variant-wise prices should be checked against Skoda's official announcement.
