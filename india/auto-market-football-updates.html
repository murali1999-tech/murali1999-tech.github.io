Mahindra has introduced the XUV 3XO ZNG, bringing a factory-fitted CNG option to its compact SUV range. The new model was announced in India on October 15, 2026, with an introductory ex-showroom price starting at ₹9.99 lakh. The company has also announced a claimed fuel efficiency of 24.7 km/kg, making it an option worth considering for buyers who want SUV styling with lower fuel expenses. Bookings and deliveries are scheduled to begin on October 20, 2026. Read the official Mahindra announcement.

For buyers comparing petrol and CNG vehicles, the important points are the actual mileage in everyday traffic, CNG availability in their area, boot space, service costs and the price difference between variants. The claimed mileage may differ from real-world results depending on driving style, traffic and road conditions. Buyers should also confirm the latest variant-wise prices and equipment with an authorised Mahindra dealer before booking.

Yes Bank shares came under significant pressure following a major legal development on October 15, 2026. Reports said the share price fell sharply following the ruling, with the stock closing at ₹18.15 on the day. The decision has implications for the bank's capital position, financial reporting and investor sentiment.

Anyone tracking Yes Bank shares should pay attention to official stock exchange filings, the bank's statements, future financial results and any disclosures explaining the impact of the judgment.
