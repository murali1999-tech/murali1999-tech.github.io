The name Saha is commonly associated with former India wicketkeeper-batter Wriddhiman Saha. Saha was one of India's most dependable wicketkeepers during his international career and played an important role during several Test campaigns.

Saha's career included 40 Test matches for India, in which he scored more than 1,300 runs and made numerous dismissals behind the stumps. He was particularly respected for his wicketkeeping technique and ability to perform in difficult conditions.

He announced his retirement from all forms of cricket in February 2025 after finishing his professional career with Bengal. His retirement brought an end to a long domestic and international career.
