Sydney Thunder vs Sydney Sixers is one of the most anticipated rivalries in Australia's Big Bash League. Known as the Sydney Smash, the contest brings together two teams representing the same city, with local pride and important competition points at stake. The matches regularly attract cricket fans who enjoy aggressive batting, fast bowling and exciting finishes.

Both sides have their own identity and supporters. Sydney Thunder are known for their green colours, while Sydney Sixers traditionally wear magenta. Whenever the teams meet, fans expect an entertaining T20 contest in which a few overs can completely change the result.
