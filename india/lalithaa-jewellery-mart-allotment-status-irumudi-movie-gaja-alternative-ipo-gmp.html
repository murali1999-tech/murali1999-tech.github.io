Grey Market Premium, commonly known as GMP, is an unofficial market indicator that investors sometimes use to understand market sentiment toward an upcoming IPO. GMP is not an official exchange price and is not guaranteed to translate into the actual listing price.

Investors searching for Gaja Alternative IPO GMP today should treat any reported premium as indicative rather than certain. GMP can change several times before listing depending on demand, market conditions, subscription trends and broader investor sentiment.

GMP warning: Grey market transactions are unofficial. GMP figures can be speculative and should not be treated as a guarantee of listing gains.

How IPO GMP Is Used

For example, if an IPO has an issue price of ₹100 and the reported GMP is ₹20, some market observers may estimate an indicative listing price of approximately ₹120. This is only a calculation based on the reported premium and does not guarantee the actual market price.

Investors should consider the company's financial performance, valuation, subscription numbers, risk factors and market conditions rather than relying only on GMP.
