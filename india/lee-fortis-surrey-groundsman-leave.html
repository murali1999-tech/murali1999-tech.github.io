Lee Fortis: Surrey Groundsman Placed on Leave After ECB Employee Row

Published: August 28, 2026 | Cricket News

Surrey head groundsman Lee Fortis has been placed on leave following a reported altercation with an England and Wales Cricket Board employee at The Oval. The incident has attracted attention because Fortis is one of the most recognised groundsmen in English cricket and previously made headlines following his confrontation with India head coach Gautam Gambhir in 2025.

The latest incident reportedly took place on July 28, 2026, while preparations were being made for a Hundred match at The Oval. MI London and London Spirit were due to play at the venue the following day, and members of the ground staff were preparing the playing surface and surrounding areas.

According to reports, Fortis became involved in a heated argument with an ECB employee who was helping with preparations for the match. The employee was reportedly left in tears following the exchange. Surrey chief executive Steve Elworthy was subsequently seen speaking with Fortis on the field.

Fortis then left the playing area and has not been seen at The Oval since the incident. Reports say he has been placed on leave while the matter is dealt with internally. It remains unclear when, or whether, he will return to his position.

Surrey have not publicly provided detailed information about the incident. A Surrey spokesperson said matters involving the club and its employees are private and confidential and therefore the club would not comment.

Lee Fortis has been Surrey's head groundsman at The Oval since 2012. He has built a strong reputation for preparing high-quality cricket pitches for county cricket, international matches and other major competitions.

Fortis has also received significant recognition for his work. He won the ECB Grounds Manager of the Year award for multi-day pitches three consecutive times from 2022 through 2024. His work has included preparing surfaces for important Test matches and Surrey County Championship fixtures.

The latest controversy has also brought back memories of Fortis's confrontation with Gautam Gambhir during India's 2025 tour of England. Gambhir and Fortis were involved in a heated disagreement at The Oval over access to the pitch during India's preparations for a Test match.

The 2025 incident attracted considerable attention on social media and among cricket fans. Gambhir was reported to have objected to restrictions around the pitch, while Fortis was responsible for the preparation and protection of the playing surface. The confrontation became one of the talking points of India's tour.

India's batting coach Sitanshu Kotak also criticised Fortis after the earlier disagreement, adding to the public discussion surrounding the groundsman. However, the current leave decision is connected to the more recent incident involving an ECB employee and is separate from the 2025 dispute with Gambhir.

Fortis's position is particularly important because The Oval is one of England's major international cricket venues. The ground hosts Surrey matches as well as international fixtures and major events. Preparing a cricket pitch involves extensive work and coordination between grounds staff, teams, match officials and governing bodies.

The timing of the latest development is also significant for Surrey. The Oval is scheduled to host major international cricket in 2027, including the fifth Ashes Test and the World Test Championship final. Surrey therefore faces an important period ahead as it manages its ground staff and preparations for the upcoming season.

Fortis's absence does not mean cricket operations at The Oval will stop. Ground staff teams work collectively on pitch preparation, maintenance and match-day operations. However, the head groundsman has a major responsibility for coordinating the work and maintaining standards across the season.

For cricket fans, the story is also a reminder of how important grounds teams are to professional cricket. Players and coaches receive most of the attention, but groundsmen work behind the scenes to prepare pitches that can significantly influence matches.

The latest reports do not establish that Fortis has permanently left Surrey. He has been reported as being on leave, while the circumstances surrounding his future remain unclear. Until Surrey makes an official announcement, claims that he has been permanently dismissed should be treated cautiously.

Further information could emerge if Surrey or the relevant cricket authorities issue a formal statement. Until then, the details of the internal matter remain limited because the club has said that employee matters are confidential.

Lee Fortis has been a prominent figure at The Oval for more than a decade. His record of award-winning pitches has established him as one of the leading groundsmen in English cricket, making the latest development a significant story for Surrey and the wider cricket community.
