Celina Jaitly: Bollywood Actress, Career and Latest News

Read more updates

Celina Jaitly is an Indian actress, model and former beauty queen who became well known in Bollywood during the 2000s. She won the Femina Miss India Universe title in 2001 and represented India at the Miss Universe pageant the same year.

Jaitly made her acting debut with the Hindi film Janasheen in 2003. She later appeared in films including No Entry, Apna Sapna Money Money, Golmaal Returns and Thank You. Her performances and glamorous screen presence helped her become a familiar name among Hindi film audiences.

Along with her film career, Celina Jaitly has remained in the public eye through interviews, social media appearances and discussions about her personal experiences. She has also been associated with humanitarian and social causes.
