Pasindu Sooriyabandara is a Sri Lankan right-handed batter who has attracted major attention after being called into Sri Lanka's playing XI as the concussion replacement for experienced batter Dinesh Chandimal during the first Test against India in Galle on August 18, 2026.

Latest update: Sri Lanka Cricket confirmed that Sooriyabandara was approved as Chandimal's concussion replacement after Chandimal was ruled out following an injury sustained while fielding on Day 4 of the Test.

Who is Pasindu Sooriyabandara?

Pasindu Sooriyabandara, whose full name is Don Pasindu Sanjula Sooriyabandara, was born on October 19, 1999, in Colombo, Sri Lanka. He is a right-handed batter and has also bowled right-arm off-break.

He developed through Sri Lanka's domestic cricket system and represented Sri Lanka at Under-19 level. His domestic career includes appearances for Sinhalese Sports Club and Moors Sports Club, while he has also represented Galle in Sri Lankan domestic competitions.

Sooriyabandara has built a strong reputation in first-class cricket, where his ability to produce substantial scores has made him one of the notable batters in Sri Lanka's domestic system.

Why is Pasindu Sooriyabandara trending?

Sooriyabandara became a major talking point on August 18, 2026, during the India tour of Sri Lanka. Dinesh Chandimal suffered an injury while fielding during the fourth day of the first Test at Galle.

Chandimal attempted to stop a shot from India's KL Rahul near the boundary. He landed heavily after diving to save runs and was subsequently assessed by Sri Lanka's medical team.

Sri Lanka Cricket stated that Chandimal's precautionary scan showed no internal injury, but he was later assessed for concussion and ruled out of the remainder of the match. Match officials approved Sooriyabandara as the concussion replacement.

This gave Sooriyabandara an unexpected opportunity to feature in the Test against India.

His first appearance against India

Sooriyabandara had been sitting in the Sri Lankan dugout before being called onto the field as Chandimal's replacement. His arrival immediately became one of the major stories from Day 4 of the Galle Test.

However, his first innings in the match ended almost immediately. He was dismissed for a first-ball duck, making his unexpected Test opportunity a dramatic and difficult introduction to international cricket.

Despite the disappointing start with the bat, his selection as a concussion replacement represents an important moment in his career. His domestic performances had already brought him into consideration for higher-level cricket, and the Test opportunity puts him firmly in the spotlight.

Domestic cricket record

Available career records show that Sooriyabandara has been particularly productive in first-class cricket. CricketArchive lists him with more than 4,900 first-class runs, including 14 centuries and 23 half-centuries, with a highest score of 242.

A promising domestic batter

Sooriyabandara's first-class numbers demonstrate why he has been regarded as a promising Sri Lankan batter. His ability to score centuries and convert starts into substantial innings has been an important part of his domestic reputation.

He also represented Sri Lanka A and was selected to lead Sri Lanka A in the four-day matches against New Zealand A in 2026. That leadership opportunity was another indication of the confidence placed in his batting ability.

His career has therefore followed a gradual pathway from junior cricket to domestic first-class cricket, Sri Lanka A cricket and now an unexpected opportunity at Test level.

Pasindu Sooriyabandara and Sri Lanka cricket

Sri Lanka has traditionally produced technically strong batters through its domestic cricket structure. Players who consistently perform in first-class competitions can earn opportunities with Sri Lanka A before being considered for the senior national team.

Sooriyabandara's domestic record has helped him move through that pathway. His selection as a concussion replacement against India is another significant step, although one unusual feature of the situation is that the opportunity came because of a medical replacement rather than through the original Test XI selection.

What happens next?

The biggest question surrounding Sooriyabandara is whether this unexpected international opportunity can help him earn a permanent place in Sri Lanka's future plans.

A first-ball dismissal is obviously not the performance a batter wants, but one innings does not define a player's career. His domestic record, leadership experience with Sri Lanka A and previous performances remain important indicators of his potential.

If he continues producing strong performances in domestic and A-team cricket, he could remain in contention for future international selections.

Conclusion

Pasindu Sooriyabandara has suddenly become one of the most searched Sri Lankan cricket names after entering the India vs Sri Lanka Test as Dinesh Chandimal's concussion replacement on August 18, 2026.

The 26-year-old batter has already built an impressive domestic record, including multiple first-class centuries and a highest score of 242. His opportunity against India arrived unexpectedly, and his first innings ended with a first-ball duck.

Nevertheless, his appearance represents an important milestone. With continued performances in domestic cricket and Sri Lanka A cricket, Sooriyabandara has an opportunity to establish himself as a future option for Sri Lanka's senior team.
