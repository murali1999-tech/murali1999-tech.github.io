📌 SSC Phase 14 Recruitment Overview

  • Organization: Staff Selection Commission
  • Exam Name: Selection Post Phase 14
  • Total Vacancies: 3000+ Posts
  • Application Mode: Online
  • Last Date: May 4, 2026

🎓 Eligibility Criteria

SSC Phase 14 recruitment is open to candidates with different educational qualifications:

  • Matriculation (10th Pass)
  • Higher Secondary (12th Pass)
  • Graduation (Bachelor’s Degree)

This makes it one of the most accessible government job opportunities in India.

🧪 Selection Process

  • Computer-Based Examination (CBT)
  • Skill Test (if required)
  • Document Verification

The exam is objective in nature and includes sections like General Intelligence, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, and English Language.

💰 Salary Structure

The salary depends on the post and level:

  • Level 1 to Level 7 Pay Scale
  • Approx Salary: ₹18,000 to ₹81,000
  • Additional allowances like HRA, DA

📝 How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official SSC website
  2. Complete One-Time Registration
  3. Fill the application form
  4. Upload documents and photo
  5. Pay application fee
  6. Submit and download confirmation
