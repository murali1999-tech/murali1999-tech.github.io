The Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) has officially released the CRPF Constable Recruitment 2026 notification, offering a massive 9,195 vacancies across India. This is one of the biggest government job opportunities for candidates who have completed 10th or 12th standard and are looking for a stable and respectable career in the defense sector.

👉 Big opportunity for 10th pass / 12th pass candidates

📌 Overview of CRPF Recruitment 2026

The CRPF is one of India’s largest paramilitary forces responsible for maintaining internal security. Every year, thousands of candidates apply for constable positions due to job security, salary benefits, and career growth.

  • Total Vacancies: 9,195 Posts
  • Department: Central Reserve Police Force
  • Job Type: Central Government Job
  • Application Mode: Online
  • Status: Applications Open

🎯 Eligibility Criteria

To apply for CRPF Constable Recruitment 2026, candidates must meet the following requirements:

  • Must have passed 10th or 12th from a recognized board
  • Age limit typically between 18 to 23 years
  • Must meet physical fitness standards set by CRPF
  • Indian citizenship is mandatory

🏃 Selection Process

The selection process for CRPF Constable includes multiple stages designed to test both physical and academic abilities.

  • Physical Efficiency Test (PET)
  • Written Examination
  • Document Verification
  • Medical Examination

Candidates must clear each stage to move to the next round. Physical fitness plays a crucial role in final selection.

💰 Salary and Benefits

Selected candidates will receive a good salary package along with multiple benefits:

  • Basic Salary: ₹21,700 to ₹69,100 (approx)
  • Allowances: HRA, DA, Travel Allowance
  • Medical facilities
  • Pension benefits

📝 How to Apply Online

Follow these steps to apply for CRPF Constable Recruitment 2026:

  1. Visit the official CRPF recruitment website
  2. Register using your email and mobile number
  3. Fill out the application form carefully
  4. Upload required documents
  5. Pay the application fee
  6. Submit and take a printout
