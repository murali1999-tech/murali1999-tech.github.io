49ers vs Rams: Score, How to Watch, Time, Stats and Melbourne NFL Game

Latest NFL updates

The San Francisco 49ers defeated the Los Angeles Rams 27-7 in the first-ever NFL regular-season game played in Australia. The historic NFC West matchup was held at the Melbourne Cricket Ground on Friday, September 11, 2026.

The game attracted more than 100,000 fans to the MCG and became one of the biggest crowds for an NFL regular-season game. The matchup was also the opening game of the 2026 NFL season for both teams.

The 49ers controlled the game for much of the afternoon, with quarterback Brock Purdy leading an efficient San Francisco offense. Purdy finished with 205 passing yards and three touchdown passes as the 49ers produced a convincing victory.

San Francisco's preparation for the unusual trip to Australia became one of the major stories before kickoff. The 49ers arrived in Melbourne about a week before the game and adjusted their training schedule to deal with the major time-zone difference.

Head coach Kyle Shanahan and his staff prepared players for the early local kickoff through changes to sleep, nutrition and practice routines. The team also spent time becoming familiar with the conditions and surroundings before game day.

The Rams took a very different approach. Los Angeles arrived in Australia shortly before the game and kept its normal preparation routine, hoping to minimize the disruption when the team returned to the United States for Week 2.

That difference in travel preparation became a major talking point after San Francisco's dominant performance. Rams coach Sean McVay acknowledged that his team was outplayed and did not use travel as an excuse for the loss.

The 49ers were particularly effective through the air. Purdy connected with his receivers for three touchdowns, while San Francisco's defense also played a major role in limiting the Rams' offensive production.

The Rams entered the season with enormous expectations. Los Angeles was considered one of the leading Super Bowl contenders and had added star defensive lineman Myles Garrett during the offseason. The team also brought back Aaron Donald after his retirement, although Donald was ruled out for the Melbourne opener.

Garrett did play in the game, but the Rams defense struggled to stop Purdy and the San Francisco offense. The 49ers consistently found ways to move the ball and create scoring opportunities.

Matthew Stafford started at quarterback for Los Angeles, while the Rams relied on their experienced offense to compete with San Francisco. The team could not generate enough points to keep the game close after the 49ers established control.

The final score was 49ers 27, Rams 7. The result gives San Francisco an important divisional victory to begin the season and puts the 49ers at the top of the early NFC West standings.

The game was also historic because it was the NFL's first regular-season contest in Australia. Melbourne had previously hosted American football events, but never a regular-season NFL game involving two teams playing for a league victory.

The Melbourne Cricket Ground provided a unique setting for the game. The famous stadium is primarily associated with cricket and Australian rules football, but more than 100,000 fans filled the venue for the NFL event.

For Australian fans, the game began at 10:35 a.m. AEST on Friday. In the United States, it was Thursday night, beginning at 8:35 p.m. Eastern Time and 5:35 p.m. Pacific Time.

The unusual time difference made the game especially interesting for international viewers. Fans in Melbourne watched a morning NFL game while viewers in the United States watched it as Thursday Night Football.

The game was streamed live on Netflix in the United States and internationally, with NFL+ also providing access for eligible viewers. It was not part of Amazon Prime Video's regular Thursday Night Football package because the international season opener had separate distribution arrangements.

Fans searching for where to watch the 49ers vs Rams game could therefore watch the matchup through Netflix, while local Australian coverage and NFL platforms provided additional options depending on location.

The NFL's decision to stage the game in Melbourne is part of a larger international expansion strategy. The 2026 season features a record nine international games across seven countries, giving the league an opportunity to reach fans outside the United States.

Australia is viewed as an important growth market for American football. The huge attendance in Melbourne demonstrated the level of interest that already exists among Australian fans.

The 49ers also benefited from strong local support. The team spent several days in Melbourne before the game, held fan activities and embraced the opportunity to connect with Australian supporters.

San Francisco's early arrival was designed to reduce the effects of jet lag. The players followed detailed schedules involving sleep, meals, training and recovery as the coaching staff prepared for the unusual kickoff.

The Rams' decision to arrive much later was based on a different theory. The organization wanted players to maintain their familiar routines and avoid spending too much time adjusting to the Australian time zone before immediately flying back to the United States.

The contrasting approaches provided an interesting experiment, although one game cannot prove which travel strategy is better. The 49ers clearly looked more comfortable on the field in Melbourne.

Brock Purdy's performance was one of the biggest positives for San Francisco. The quarterback looked composed and helped the offense produce 27 points against a Rams defense that was expected to be one of the strongest units in the NFL.

Purdy entered the season under significant pressure after the 49ers' disappointing end to the previous campaign. His performance in Melbourne immediately gave San Francisco fans confidence that the offense could compete at a high level again.

Kyle Shanahan also received praise for the team's preparation. The 49ers had to manage a journey of thousands of miles while preparing for a game at an unusual time and in a stadium with a very different playing environment.

The Rams now have to regroup quickly. Their next game is against the New York Giants in Week 2, and the team will want to put the Melbourne defeat behind it as soon as possible.

Los Angeles still has one of the most talented rosters in the NFC. Matthew Stafford remains an experienced quarterback, while the offense includes major weapons such as Davante Adams. The defense also has Myles Garrett and other high-level players.

San Francisco will also need to move forward quickly. The 49ers have another difficult season ahead in a competitive NFC West that includes the Rams, Seattle Seahawks and Arizona Cardinals.

The 49ers and Rams will meet again later in the season. Their second matchup is scheduled for Week 14, giving Los Angeles an opportunity to respond to the Melbourne defeat.

The first meeting nevertheless gives San Francisco an early advantage in the divisional race. A 27-7 win also provides an important confidence boost for a team hoping to make another deep postseason run.

The atmosphere at the MCG was another major part of the event. More than 100,000 spectators attended, creating one of the largest crowds ever seen for an NFL regular-season game.

Entertainment surrounding the game included the Jonas Brothers at halftime, while fans were able to experience NFL activities around the stadium and throughout Melbourne.

The Melbourne game also generated significant attention outside sport. Government officials promoted the event as an important international attraction for Victoria, while the NFL viewed the matchup as an opportunity to expand its Australian fan base.

There was also a strong connection between American and Australian sporting culture. The MCG is one of the world's most famous cricket and Australian rules football venues, making it a fitting location for the NFL's first regular-season game in the country.

For fans asking what time the NFL game was in Melbourne, kickoff was 10:35 a.m. AEST on Friday, September 11. For viewers in Los Angeles, the game started at 5:35 p.m. PT on Thursday, September 10.

For viewers on the U.S. East Coast, kickoff was 8:35 p.m. ET Thursday. That unusual timing allowed the game to be presented as a Thursday night event in the United States while taking place on Friday morning in Australia.

The final 27-7 score was a strong statement from San Francisco. The 49ers defense held the Rams to seven points, while Purdy and the offense consistently produced enough plays to build a comfortable lead.

Los Angeles will have to examine its offensive execution, protection and defensive performance before Week 2. The Rams were expected to challenge for the Super Bowl, so one defeat does not change those expectations completely.

For San Francisco, however, the victory could become one of the most memorable season openers in franchise history. Winning the first NFL regular-season game in Australia and doing so by 20 points gives the 49ers a memorable start to 2026.

The 49ers vs Rams matchup also demonstrated the NFL's growing international reach. Melbourne proved that American football can attract a massive audience in Australia, and the success of the event is likely to influence future international scheduling.

NFL Commissioner Roger Goodell has indicated that Australia will receive another regular-season game under the league's agreement with Victoria. The success of the Melbourne event gives the NFL a strong foundation for future games in the country.

For now, the focus turns back to the regular season. San Francisco leaves Melbourne with a 1-0 record and plenty of confidence, while the Rams return to the United States looking for a response.

The final result remains clear: the San Francisco 49ers defeated the Los Angeles Rams 27-7 in Melbourne. Brock Purdy threw three touchdown passes, the 49ers defense controlled the Rams offense and more than 100,000 fans witnessed an historic NFL event.
