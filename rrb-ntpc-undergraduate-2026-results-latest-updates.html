What is RRB NTPC?

RRB NTPC stands for Railway Recruitment Board Non-Technical Popular Categories. These positions include several clerical and operational roles in Indian Railways. The Undergraduate category is specially designed for candidates who completed their qualification at the intermediate or higher secondary level.

Popular posts under the Undergraduate category include junior clerk, accounts clerk, commercial ticket clerk, and train clerk positions. These jobs are highly popular because they offer salary stability, allowances, job security, and opportunities for promotion within the railway department.

Normalization Process Explained

Since the RRB NTPC examination is conducted in multiple shifts, the Railway Recruitment Board follows a normalization process to ensure fairness. Different shifts may have varying difficulty levels, so normalization helps maintain balance while calculating final scores.

Candidates often notice slight differences between raw marks and normalized marks. This is a standard procedure followed in large-scale competitive examinations conducted in India to maintain equal evaluation standards.
