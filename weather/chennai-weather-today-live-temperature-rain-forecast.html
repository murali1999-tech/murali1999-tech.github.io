Chennai weather today

Chennai, the capital city of Tamil Nadu, has a tropical climate with warm temperatures and significant humidity throughout much of the year. Weather conditions can change quickly, particularly during periods when showers and thunderstorms develop along the Tamil Nadu coast.

For August weather, Chennai can experience a mixture of sunshine, clouds and short-duration showers. Temperatures generally remain warm, while humidity can make the apparent temperature feel considerably higher than the measured air temperature.

Chennai temperature today

Temperature is one of the most searched Chennai weather indicators. During warm periods, afternoon temperatures can rise into the mid-30s Celsius, while nighttime temperatures can remain in the upper 20s Celsius.

The actual temperature at any particular location in Chennai can differ slightly depending on the neighbourhood, proximity to the coast, cloud cover and rainfall. Coastal areas can experience additional humidity and changing winds.

Will it rain in Chennai today?

Rainfall in Chennai can be highly localized. A shower may affect one part of the city while another neighbourhood remains dry. During unstable weather, thunderstorms can also develop quickly during the afternoon or evening.

Chennai humidity and wind

Humidity is an important part of Chennai's weather experience. Even when the air temperature is not exceptionally high, elevated humidity can make conditions feel considerably warmer.

Wind direction and speed also influence how comfortable the weather feels. Sea breezes can affect coastal Chennai, while changing weather systems can produce stronger winds during thunderstorms.

The live weather panel displays wind information alongside temperature and humidity, allowing visitors to understand current conditions rather than looking only at the temperature.

Chennai weather and thunderstorms

Thunderstorms can develop when atmospheric instability combines with sufficient moisture. During these periods, Chennai can experience brief heavy rain, lightning and gusty winds.

People should avoid exposed locations during lightning activity and should follow official warnings issued by the India Meteorological Department and local authorities.

Chennai weather for travel

Visitors travelling to Chennai should consider both temperature and humidity when planning outdoor activities. Lightweight clothing, adequate drinking water and protection from strong sunlight can be useful during hot periods.

If rain is expected, travellers should allow additional time for road journeys. Heavy showers can temporarily slow traffic and cause water accumulation on some roads.

Chennai weather forecast accuracy

Weather forecasts are continuously updated as new observations become available. Small differences between weather services are normal because forecast models, observation stations and update schedules can differ.
