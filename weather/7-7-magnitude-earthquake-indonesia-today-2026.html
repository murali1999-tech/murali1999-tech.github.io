⚠️ Major Earthquake in Indonesia

A powerful magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck eastern Indonesia near the Flores region on August 15, 2026. The earthquake caused severe shaking, building damage and landslides in parts of East Nusa Tenggara. Rescue and assessment operations are continuing.

Authorities initially issued a tsunami warning. The warning was subsequently lifted after monitoring showed no significant continuing sea-level threat. Aftershocks have continued to occur.

🚨 What to Do During an Earthquake

Drop, Cover and Hold On. If shaking begins, drop to the ground, take cover under a sturdy table or desk if available, and hold on until the shaking stops.

Stay away from windows, glass, unstable walls and objects that may fall. Do not use elevators during or immediately after strong shaking.

If you are near the coast and authorities issue a tsunami evacuation warning, move quickly to higher ground or follow the designated evacuation route. Do not wait to watch the sea.

After the main earthquake, remain alert because aftershocks can cause additional damage to already weakened structures.

Why does Indonesia experience so many earthquakes?

Indonesia lies in one of the world's most seismically active regions and forms part of the Pacific Ring of Fire. Several tectonic plates meet around the Indonesian archipelago, producing frequent earthquakes and volcanic activity.
