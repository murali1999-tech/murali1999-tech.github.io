What Is RUHS CUET?

RUHS CUET stands for Rajasthan University of Health Sciences Common Undergraduate Entrance Test. It is conducted for admission into health science and medical related undergraduate programs offered by Rajasthan University of Health Sciences.

The entrance exam evaluates students on different subjects and helps determine eligibility for admission into various academic courses.

How to Download RUHS CUET 2026 Admit Card

  1. Visit the official RUHS website.
  2. Open the admissions or examination section.
  3. Click on the RUHS CUET 2026 admit card link.
  4. Enter your application number and password.
  5. Submit the details.
  6. Your admit card will appear on the screen.
  7. Download and print the hall ticket for future use.
