About Dhar Bhojshala

Dhar Bhojshala is one of the most discussed historical and religious sites in Madhya Pradesh. Located in Dhar district, the site has become the center of legal, archaeological, and religious debates for many years. Hindus consider Bhojshala as a temple dedicated to Goddess Saraswati, while Muslims identify the structure as the Kamal Maula Mosque. Because of this dual claim, the Bhojshala case continues to attract nationwide attention.

The site is believed to have historical links with Raja Bhoj, the famous Paramara king who ruled central India during the 11th century. According to historians, Bhojshala was once a center of Sanskrit learning and education. Over the years, the structure underwent several changes, leading to disputes regarding ownership and usage rights.

Bhojshala Case Latest News

The Bhojshala case has remained in the news because of the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) survey ordered by the court. The survey aimed to examine the historical nature of the structure and collect evidence regarding its origin and architectural features.

Recent reports indicate that archaeological findings, inscriptions, pillars, sculptures, and ancient remains discovered during the survey have intensified public interest in the matter. The survey process was carried out under court supervision with strict security arrangements.

The legal proceedings continue in court as different parties present their claims and evidence regarding the historical identity of Bhojshala.

ASI Survey and Archaeological Findings

The Archaeological Survey of India conducted extensive examinations at the Bhojshala complex. Reports mention discoveries of ancient carvings, pillars, and architectural structures that are being studied by experts.

  • Ancient stone carvings were reportedly identified.
  • Several Sanskrit inscriptions attracted attention.
  • Architectural patterns linked to temple-style construction were discussed.
  • Survey documentation was submitted before the court.

The ASI survey became a major topic of discussion across social media and news channels because of its potential impact on the ongoing legal dispute.

Why Bhojshala Is Important

Bhojshala is considered significant because of its religious, historical, and cultural importance. Supporters of the temple claim it was originally a Saraswati temple and educational institution established during Raja Bhoj’s rule. The structure also holds importance for Muslims who offer prayers at the site on Fridays under existing administrative arrangements.

Due to the emotional and historical connection attached to the structure, the matter remains highly sensitive. Authorities continue to maintain strict law and order arrangements during major hearings and religious events.
