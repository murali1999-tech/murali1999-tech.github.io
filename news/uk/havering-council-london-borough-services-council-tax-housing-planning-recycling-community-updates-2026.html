About Havering Council

Havering Council is the governing authority responsible for local administration within the London Borough of Havering. The borough is located in northeast London and is one of the outer London boroughs.

The council manages various community and civic services including:

  • Housing and homelessness support
  • Council tax collection
  • Waste and recycling services
  • Road maintenance and transport
  • Education and schools
  • Planning permissions
  • Libraries and public facilities
  • Social care services

Havering is known for its suburban communities, parks, shopping centers, and growing residential developments.

Council Tax Services

Council tax is one of the most important local services managed by Havering Council. Residents use council tax payments to help fund local services and infrastructure.

Through the official portal, residents can:

  • Pay council tax online
  • Check balances
  • Apply for discounts
  • Update personal details
  • Request support for financial difficulties

Discounts may be available for single occupants, students, disabled residents, and low-income households.

Housing and Homelessness Support

Havering Council provides housing assistance to residents facing housing problems or homelessness risks. The council also manages housing applications and social housing programs.

Housing services include:

  • Homelessness support
  • Housing applications
  • Emergency accommodation
  • Tenant support services
  • Private housing enforcement

Residents can access housing advice and support through the official council website.

Waste Collection and Recycling

Waste management and recycling collection are among the most used Havering Council services. Residents can check collection schedules and report missed bins online.

The council encourages recycling to reduce environmental impact and improve sustainability across the borough.

  • Household waste collection
  • Garden waste services
  • Recycling programs
  • Bulky waste collection
  • Street cleaning services

Planning and Development

Havering Council manages planning applications for residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects across the borough.

Residents and developers can:

  • Submit planning applications
  • Track application status
  • View planning maps
  • Read public notices
  • Submit objections or comments

Planning services play a major role in managing urban growth and local development in Havering.

Community and Public Services

Havering Council also supports libraries, parks, leisure centers, youth programs, and public health services. Residents can access updates about local events, road works, and emergency notices online.

The borough contains several important areas including:

  • Romford
  • Hornchurch
  • Upminster
  • Rainham
  • Harold Wood

Public transportation connections through London Underground, Elizabeth Line, and National Rail help connect Havering with central London.
