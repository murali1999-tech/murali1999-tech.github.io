Why Petrol Prices Differ Across Cities

Petrol and diesel prices vary from city to city because state governments impose different VAT and local taxes. Transportation costs and dealer commissions also contribute to price differences.

This is why petrol price in Mumbai may differ from Delhi, Bangalore, or Jaipur.
