The Kerala SSLC Result 2026 is one of the most awaited academic announcements for Class 10 students in Kerala. How to Download Kerala SSLC Result 2026

  1. Visit the official website.
  2. Click on “Kerala SSLC Result 2026”.
  3. Enter your registration number.
  4. Submit the details.
  5. Your result will appear on the screen.
  6. Download and print the marks memo for future reference.

Students can also check school wise results using the dedicated school code option available on some portals.

School Wise Kerala SSLC Result 2026

Many students search for school wise Kerala SSLC results to view the performance of their schools. The school wise result option helps schools and students analyze overall pass percentage and toppers.

To check school wise results:

  • Visit the official result portal.
  • Select “School Wise Result”.
  • Enter the school code.
  • View the complete school result list.

FAQ – Kerala SSLC Result 2026

When will Kerala SSLC Result 2026 be released?

The result is expected in May 2026. Official confirmation will be available on Kerala government portals.

Where can I check Kerala SSLC Result 2026?

You can check the result on result.kite.kerala.gov.in, keralaresults.nic.in, and sslcexam.kerala.gov.in.

Can I download the marks memo online?

Yes. Students can download provisional marks memos online after result declaration.

How can I check school wise SSLC result?

Use the school code on the official portal to access school wise results.

Due to heavy traffic, websites may become slow temporarily. Refresh the page after a few minutes or try alternative official portals.
