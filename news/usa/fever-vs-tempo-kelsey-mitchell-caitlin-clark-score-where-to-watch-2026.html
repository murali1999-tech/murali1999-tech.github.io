Indiana Fever vs Toronto Tempo: Kelsey Mitchell, Caitlin Clark, Score and Latest News

Latest update: August 19, 2026
The Indiana Fever defeated the Toronto Tempo 101-95 on August 18. Kelsey Mitchell scored 29 points before leaving the game in the fourth quarter, while Caitlin Clark finished with 24 points and seven assists.

The Indiana Fever and Toronto Tempo played a closely watched WNBA matchup at Scotiabank Arena in Toronto. The Fever came away with a 101-95 victory, extending their winning streak to five games. The game generated significant attention because it marked Caitlin Clark's first professional appearance in Canada and because Kelsey Mitchell left the contest before the fourth quarter was completed.

Fever vs Tempo: What happened?

Indiana controlled much of the game but Toronto continued to fight back. The Fever ultimately held on for a six-point road victory. Mitchell was especially effective early, scoring 16 points in the opening quarter and finishing with 29 points.
