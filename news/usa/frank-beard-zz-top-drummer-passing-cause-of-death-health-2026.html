Who was Frank Beard?

Frank Lee Beard was an American drummer who became internationally famous as a member of ZZ Top. Despite his surname, Beard was famously the only member of the classic trio who did not wear the band's trademark long beard.

Beard was born in Texas and developed an interest in drumming at a young age. His musical career eventually brought him together with Billy Gibbons and Dusty Hill, forming the classic ZZ Top lineup that would remain together for decades.

His drumming style was a major part of ZZ Top's sound. His tight grooves helped combine blues, rock, boogie and Southern influences into the distinctive sound that made the band internationally successful.

ZZ Top members: Billy Gibbons, Dusty Hill and Frank Beard

The classic ZZ Top lineup consisted of guitarist and vocalist Billy Gibbons, bassist and vocalist Dusty Hill, and drummer Frank Beard. The trio became one of the most enduring lineups in rock history.

Dusty Hill died in 2021 at age 72. Following Hill's death, Elwood Francis joined ZZ Top on bass and continued performing with Billy Gibbons and Frank Beard.

With Beard's death in 2026, Billy Gibbons is now the surviving member of ZZ Top's classic trio. Reuters reported that Gibbons said the band intends to continue performing, consistent with Beard's wishes.

ZZ Top's musical legacy

ZZ Top became one of America's best-known rock bands, particularly during the 1970s and 1980s. Albums such as Tres Hombres and Eliminator helped establish the group's identity.

Songs including "Gimme All Your Lovin'," "Sharp Dressed Man" and "Legs" became major international hits. The band's distinctive music videos, sunglasses, guitars, long beards and Texas image made ZZ Top instantly recognizable during the MTV era.

ZZ Top was inducted into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame in 2004. Beard's contribution extended across decades of recordings and live performances, making him a central figure in the band's history.

Will ZZ Top continue after Frank Beard's death?

According to recent reporting, ZZ Top is expected to continue performing. Billy Gibbons and bassist Elwood Francis have continued the band's touring plans with drummer Michael Monahan filling in for Beard.

The decision means that ZZ Top's music will continue on stage even after the deaths of two members of its classic trio. The band has indicated that continuing to perform is consistent with Beard's wishes.
