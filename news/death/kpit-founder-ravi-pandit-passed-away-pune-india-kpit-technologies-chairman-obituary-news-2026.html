KPIT Technologies officially confirmed the passing of its founder and chairman Ravi Pandit through an exchange filing. According to reports, he passed away in Pune on the morning of May 8, 2026.

Ravi Pandit was widely respected in India's IT and automotive engineering industry. His leadership helped transform KPIT Technologies into a globally recognized mobility and automotive software company.

Who Was Ravi Pandit?

Ravi Pandit was an Indian businessman, entrepreneur, and technology leader best known as the co-founder and chairman of KPIT Technologies. He co-founded the company in 1990 along with Kishor Patil.

Born in Pune, Maharashtra, Ravi Pandit completed his education in management from the prestigious MIT Sloan School of Management. He was also a Chartered Accountant before entering the technology industry.

Under his leadership, KPIT evolved from a small IT services company into a major global player focused on automotive software, electric vehicles, mobility solutions, and engineering research.

Journey of KPIT Technologies

KPIT Technologies started as KPIT Infosystems and later became KPIT Cummins after collaboration with Cummins. Over the years, the company expanded rapidly into automotive technology and mobility engineering.

Ravi Pandit played a major role in shifting the company's focus toward future technologies including:

  • Electric vehicle software
  • Autonomous driving technologies
  • Automotive embedded systems
  • Connected mobility solutions
  • Digital vehicle engineering

Today KPIT Technologies serves leading automotive companies across Europe, the USA, Japan, China, and India.

Contributions Beyond Business

Ravi Pandit was not only a successful entrepreneur but also contributed significantly to social and environmental initiatives. He supported sustainable development programs and educational projects.

He was associated with several organizations and policy groups related to technology, sustainability, and urban development.

Many entrepreneurs and professionals considered him an inspiration for building a globally respected company from Pune.

Industry Reactions

Following the news of his passing, tributes poured in from industry leaders, employees, investors, and technology professionals across India.

Social media users described Ravi Pandit as a visionary leader who helped establish Pune as a major technology and automotive software hub.

Several users also praised his ethical leadership, long-term vision, and contribution toward innovation in the mobility sector.
