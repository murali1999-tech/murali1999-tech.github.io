Belgium Sewage Worker Gold Discovery: €9 Million Treasure Found During Sewer Work

Published: August 16, 2026 | Belgium
Breaking discovery: An 18-year-old Belgian student working with a construction team reportedly stumbled upon a huge hidden gold treasure estimated to be worth around €9 million while workers were carrying out sewer-related renovation work in Sint-Gillis-Dendermonde, near Brussels.

What began as an ordinary summer construction job turned into an extraordinary discovery when workers renovating a former brewery site in Belgium uncovered a large collection of gold. The treasure reportedly included gold coins, nuggets and bullion bars hidden inside a bricked-up cellar wall.

The discovery has attracted international attention because of the enormous estimated value of the hoard. Reports put the value at approximately €9 million, equivalent to roughly $10.4 million based on reported conversions. The exact market value, historical significance and ownership of the gold are still being investigated by Belgian authorities.

Who Discovered the Belgian Gold Treasure?

The discovery involved an 18-year-old Belgian student identified in reports as Kobe, who was working during the summer as part of a construction team. He and his colleagues were carrying out excavation and renovation work connected with the installation of a new sewer system.

At first, the objects reportedly did not look particularly unusual to the young worker. Some pieces were initially mistaken for ordinary coins. As more material was uncovered, however, the workers realized that they had encountered something much more valuable.

The team eventually discovered gold coins, nuggets and bullion bars concealed within the old structure. Reports say that dozens of gold bars were among the items recovered.

Where Was the Gold Found?

The discovery occurred in Sint-Gillis-Dendermonde in East Flanders, Belgium. The site was associated with a former brewery and a historic villa belonging to a brewing family. The brewery operation had continued for decades before the property was eventually repurposed.

The gold was reportedly concealed in a cellar wall that had been bricked up. Its location helped explain why the treasure remained hidden for so long despite the property having undergone changes over the years.

Why Was the Gold Hidden?

One of the biggest unanswered questions is why such a large quantity of gold was hidden inside the building. Investigators are examining the history of the property and the former owners in an attempt to determine how the treasure came to be stored there.

Some reports have linked the property to the Van Assche brewing family. However, the precise history of the treasure has not yet been conclusively established. Authorities must determine whether the gold was legitimately accumulated and stored by a previous owner or whether it could have been connected to theft or another criminal activity.

The investigation is therefore not simply about determining who owns the gold today. Authorities also need to establish its provenance and ensure that the treasure is not connected to an unresolved historical crime.

Workers Reported the Discovery

Rather than secretly taking the treasure, the construction team reportedly informed the relevant authorities after realizing the significance of what they had found. Police became involved and the gold was secured.

The decision to report the discovery could become important when Belgian authorities determine what happens to the treasure. The workers have reportedly expressed hope that they could eventually receive some form of reward, but they do not automatically become owners simply because they discovered the gold.

What Happens to the €9 Million Gold?

The ownership question remains unresolved. Belgian authorities are investigating potential claims and the history of the property. Reports indicate that the treasure has been placed in secure custody while the investigation continues.

The legal process could take considerable time because officials need to identify any legitimate owner or heirs and determine how Belgian treasure and property laws apply to the discovery.

The construction workers therefore face an unusual situation: they discovered an enormous fortune but cannot simply take the gold home. Any eventual financial reward will depend on the legal determination of ownership and the applicable Belgian rules.

A Remarkable Construction-Site Discovery

Finding valuable objects during construction work is not completely unheard of in Belgium. Previous cases have involved workers discovering gold coins and even gold bars during renovation or sewer-related work. However, the reported €9 million value of this latest treasure makes the 2026 discovery particularly remarkable.

The story is also notable because the discovery was made by a young student working a summer job. Instead of an experienced archaeologist or professional treasure hunter, it was an ordinary construction worker who happened to be in the right place at the right time.

Police Investigation Continues

Belgian authorities are continuing to investigate the origin of the gold. Officials must establish the identity of the original owner, examine the property's history and determine whether any criminal activity is connected with the treasure.

Authorities have also warned members of the public not to enter the construction area or attempt their own treasure hunt. The site is undergoing renovation and unauthorized digging could create serious safety risks as well as legal problems.

Why the Discovery Is Trending

The Belgian gold discovery has quickly become an international viral story because it combines several elements that naturally attract public attention: a young worker, a hidden cellar, millions of euros in gold and a mystery surrounding the treasure's original owner.

The image of workers discovering what initially appeared to be ordinary coins before realizing they were dealing with a multimillion-euro treasure has made the story especially compelling on social media.

For now, however, the most important part of the story is that the gold has been secured while authorities investigate. The final outcome will depend on the evidence concerning its history and the legal claims made over the treasure.

Bottom Line

An 18-year-old Belgian student and fellow construction workers made an extraordinary discovery while working on sewer-related renovation work in Sint-Gillis-Dendermonde. Hidden inside an old cellar wall was a large collection of gold coins, nuggets and bullion bars reportedly worth about €9 million.

The treasure is now under official protection while Belgian authorities investigate its origin and ownership. Whether the workers will eventually receive a reward remains uncertain. What is certain is that an ordinary summer construction job has turned into one of the most remarkable treasure discoveries reported in Belgium in 2026.

Note: The reported €9 million valuation and details of the treasure are based on current media reports. The final valuation, ownership and legal status may change as the official investigation progresses.
