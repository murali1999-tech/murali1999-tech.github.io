Sarah Khalifa is an Egyptian television presenter and media personality who has attracted widespread attention following a major criminal case in Cairo. She has worked in television, entertainment and event production and became known to audiences through appearances on Egyptian and Arab television.

Her social media presence has contributed to her recognition as a public figure in Egypt.

According to reports, Khalifa began her media career with the ART network, where she presented a programme known as “Min Cairo,” meaning “From Cairo.” She later worked with the Iraqi Al Sharqiya television network. Her television career included entertainment programming and interviews with personalities from the Arab entertainment industry.

Her career later expanded into production and event organisation. Reports have said that she established a production company and became involved in media-related activities. She was also associated with a cosmetic surgery clinic.

The major change in Khalifa’s public life came with her arrest in April 2025 during an investigation into a large drug case. Egyptian authorities alleged that a criminal organisation was involved in obtaining materials used to manufacture synthetic narcotics.

The case involved multiple defendants. Reports said authorities seized large quantities of narcotics and materials allegedly connected with drug production. Weapons and ammunition were also reported among items seized during the investigation.

Khalifa has denied the accusations against her. Her defence has challenged the allegations, while prosecutors have presented evidence and testimony as part of the proceedings.

The case received particular attention because of Khalifa’s public profile. She had previously appeared on television discussing crime and security-related subjects, making the allegations against her especially notable to Egyptian and international audiences.

On September 5, 2026, a Cairo criminal court reportedly sentenced Khalifa and other defendants to death in connection with the drug case. Other defendants received different sentences, while some were acquitted, according to reports.

The death sentence does not necessarily mean that the legal process has ended. The verdict can be challenged through the Egyptian appeals process, and further judicial proceedings may affect what happens next.

Khalifa has continued to deny the allegations. Her legal team can use the available legal procedures to challenge the verdict. This means that developments in the appeal process are important for anyone following the case.
