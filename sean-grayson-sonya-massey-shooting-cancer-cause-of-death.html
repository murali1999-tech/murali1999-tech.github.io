Sean Grayson, the former Sangamon County sheriff's deputy convicted in the fatal shooting of Sonya Massey, has died while serving his prison sentence. Grayson's attorney confirmed his death on Sunday, August 23, 2026. He was 32 years old.

Important: Authorities and Grayson's attorney have not publicly confirmed the exact medical cause of death. Grayson had been battling advanced cancer, but it has not been officially established that cancer caused his death.

Who Was Sean Grayson?

Sean Grayson was a former Illinois law enforcement officer who served as a deputy with the Sangamon County Sheriff's Office. He became nationally known after the July 2024 shooting of Sonya Massey inside her home near Springfield, Illinois.

Massey, 36, had called 911 because she believed there was a possible prowler outside her home. Grayson and another deputy responded to the call. Body-camera footage later became central evidence in the investigation and criminal case.

Grayson was ultimately convicted of second-degree murder in connection with Massey's death. In January 2026, he received a 20-year prison sentence followed by mandatory supervised release.

What Happened to Sonya Massey?

On July 6, 2024, Sonya Massey contacted police for assistance. Officers arrived at her home and spoke with her inside the residence.

During the encounter, Massey was near a pot of hot water. A confrontation followed, and Grayson drew his firearm and shot Massey. She suffered a fatal gunshot wound to the head.

Body-camera footage from the incident was subsequently released publicly and became a major part of the investigation and prosecution. The shooting generated widespread public attention and renewed discussions about police use of force, officer training and accountability.

Grayson was initially charged with first-degree murder. At trial, however, the jury found him guilty of the lesser offense of second-degree murder.

What Happened to Sean Grayson?

Grayson died in custody on August 23, 2026, according to his attorney. At the time of his death, he was serving his 20-year sentence in the Illinois Department of Corrections.

News reports say Grayson had significant medical problems while incarcerated. His attorneys had previously sought medical release because of his serious cancer diagnosis.

Did Sean Grayson Have Cancer?

Yes. Grayson had been diagnosed with colon cancer before the Sonya Massey shooting. His attorneys later said that his disease had progressed significantly.

By 2026, reports described his condition as advanced or Stage 4 cancer, with the disease having spread to his liver and lungs. Other reporting has also described rectal involvement.

Because of his medical condition, Grayson's legal team sought early medical release from prison. The Illinois Prisoner Review Board rejected that request in July 2026, only weeks before his death.

How Did Sean Grayson Pass Away?

The exact cause of Sean Grayson's death has not been officially confirmed.

Grayson's attorney confirmed that he died in prison but did not publicly provide a specific cause of death. Several major news organizations reported that it remained unclear whether his advanced cancer caused his death.

It is therefore accurate to say that Grayson had advanced cancer and died while incarcerated, but it would be premature to state definitively that cancer was the cause of death unless officials or his family later confirm it.

Why Was Sean Grayson in Prison?

Grayson was imprisoned because of his conviction in the death of Sonya Massey. A jury found him guilty of second-degree murder after prosecutors presented evidence concerning the July 2024 shooting.

He was sentenced to 20 years in prison in January 2026. His medical condition subsequently became part of the legal proceedings when his attorneys requested compassionate or medical release.

What Was the Outcome of His Medical Release Request?

Grayson's request for early medical release was denied by the Illinois Prisoner Review Board in July 2026. The decision came despite evidence that his cancer had advanced and that he was receiving medical treatment.

The board's decision meant that Grayson remained in custody while continuing to receive treatment. He died several weeks later.

Why Is the Sonya Massey Case Still Receiving Attention?

The case became a significant national story because Massey had contacted police seeking help before she was fatally shot by one of the responding officers.

The release of body-camera footage generated widespread public reaction. The case also prompted discussions about law-enforcement hiring, officer training, crisis response and the use of deadly force.

The aftermath included calls for greater accountability and changes to policing practices. The case became particularly significant in Illinois and was followed closely throughout Grayson's prosecution.

Bottom Line

Sean Grayson, the former Illinois deputy convicted in the fatal shooting of Sonya Massey, died in prison on August 23, 2026, at age 32. His death occurred only weeks after Illinois authorities rejected his request for medical release.
