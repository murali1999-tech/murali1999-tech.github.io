Disney D23 2026: Major Announcements

Disney's D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event is taking place in Anaheim, California, from August 14–16, 2026. The 2026 event has delivered major announcements and first looks across Disney Entertainment, Marvel, Star Wars, Pixar and Disney+.

🔥 Biggest D23 Announcements

Marvel's X-Men

Marvel Studios revealed the principal cast for the upcoming X-Men movie. The announced cast includes Sadie Sink as Jean Grey, Kit Connor as Cyclops, Christopher Abbott as Professor X, Inde Navarrette as Rogue, Maya Boyd as Storm and Adam Driver as Mister Sinister. The movie is scheduled for May 2028.

Avengers: Doomsday

Marvel also showcased new material from Avengers: Doomsday, one of the most anticipated Marvel Studios releases. The project continues the expanding Marvel Cinematic Universe and generated major reactions from fans at D23.

Star Wars: Starfighter

Disney presented first footage from Star Wars: Starfighter, starring Ryan Gosling. The Star Wars project was one of the major highlights of the D23 entertainment showcase.

Frozen 3

Disney revealed new details about Frozen 3. The film is scheduled for release in November 2027 and will continue the story of Anna and Elsa.

Incredibles 3

Pixar announced Incredibles 3 for summer 2028. The new movie is expected to explore the superhero family and particularly its younger generation.

Coco 2

Pixar also announced Coco 2, scheduled for November 2029. The sequel will return to the world of Miguel and the Land of the Dead.

Kingdom Hearts Animated Series

Disney announced an animated series based on the popular Kingdom Hearts video-game franchise. The project adds another major entertainment franchise to Disney's growing animation slate.

📺 VisionQuest

Marvel confirmed an October 14 release date for the Disney+ miniseries VisionQuest, starring Paul Bettany and James Spader. The series is part of Marvel's continuing expansion of its television storytelling on Disney+.

🏰 Disney Parks Announcements

D23 2026 is also featuring major Disney Parks updates. Disney has showcased new concepts and models connected to projects including Cars Land, Avatar, Encanto, Monsters Inc. and the planned Villains Land at Magic Kingdom.

The Villains Land concept includes attractions, shopping and dining and is expected to feature iconic Disney villains including Ursula, Maleficent, Hades, Jafar and Cruella.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is D23?

D23 is Disney's official fan club and the name of Disney's major fan events. The name D23 refers to 1923, the year Walt Disney founded his first studio.
