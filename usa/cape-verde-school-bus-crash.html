Cape Verde School Bus Crash: At Least 25 Killed on Fogo Island

A devastating school bus crash in Cape Verde has left at least 25 people dead after a bus carrying many schoolchildren and teenagers plunged into a ravine on the island of Fogo. The accident happened late Saturday, September 5, 2026, on a mountainous road as the bus was returning toward São Filipe after an excursion. The tragedy has shocked communities across the island nation and prompted Cape Verdean authorities to declare two days of national mourning. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

The Cape Verde school bus accident happened on the Campanas de Cima route in northern Fogo. According to reports, the bus left the mountain road and fell approximately 30 metres into a steep and rocky ravine. The difficult terrain made the rescue and recovery operation particularly challenging for emergency workers who had to reach the wreckage in a remote area. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Many of those killed were young people. Reports from local authorities and news agencies said that primary and secondary school students were among the passengers. The bus was carrying around 32 people, according to officials cited by international news agencies. The driver was also among those who died in the crash. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

The journey had a connection to the students' school lives, but education officials stressed an important detail about the trip. The outing was not officially organized by the local schools. Instead, it was an annual excursion privately arranged by the driver, who normally transported pupils to school during the academic year. The driver had reportedly organized the trip as a special outing for the young passengers. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

The group had travelled to Chã das Caldeiras, a settlement located inside the volcanic crater area of Pico do Fogo. The location is known for its dramatic volcanic landscape and mountainous surroundings. The bus was returning toward São Filipe when the accident occurred on the difficult mountain route. Authorities have not yet established the exact reason why the vehicle left the road. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Emergency personnel worked through the night after the school bus crash. The wreckage came to rest in a rocky valley that was difficult to access, creating serious challenges for rescuers. Some passengers were trapped inside the damaged vehicle, while emergency crews worked to reach and evacuate those who could be rescued. Several injured people were taken to São Francisco de Assis Regional Hospital on Fogo. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

The number of survivors and the condition of injured passengers have been updated as authorities continue to deal with the aftermath. Reports on September 7 said that 12 people were still receiving hospital treatment, with two described as being in serious condition. The situation remains subject to further official updates as medical teams continue caring for those injured. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

The loss of so many young lives has had a profound effect on Fogo Island. Families, friends, teachers and residents gathered to mourn the victims. Funerals began shortly after the crash, with seven victims buried earlier on Sunday and additional funerals held later. The scenes reflected the scale of the tragedy in a close-knit island community. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

Cape Verde President José Maria Pereira Neves described the accident as a major catastrophe and expressed condolences to the victims' families. The president was expected to visit Fogo as authorities and local officials responded to the disaster. The government also declared two days of mourning as the country came to terms with the deaths. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

The cause of the Cape Verde bus crash remains under investigation. Authorities will need to establish why the vehicle went off the road, whether road conditions, vehicle condition, driver factors or another circumstance contributed to the accident. At this stage, officials have not announced a definitive cause, and it is important not to treat speculation as established fact. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

The tragedy has also drawn attention to the challenges of travelling through mountainous parts of Fogo. The island is dominated by volcanic terrain, and some roads pass through steep and rugged areas. A crash in such a location can make emergency response considerably more difficult because rescuers must reach accident sites that may be far below the road and difficult to access.

Although the incident is being widely described online as a school bus crash, the distinction regarding the excursion is important. The passengers included many schoolchildren and teenagers, but officials said the particular trip was privately arranged rather than an excursion organized by the schools. This detail has appeared consistently in reporting from Reuters and other international news organizations. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

The Cape Verde school bus accident is one of the country's most serious recent road tragedies. The number of victims, the young age of many passengers and the difficult circumstances surrounding the rescue have made the crash particularly devastating. Communities on Fogo Island are now focused on supporting bereaved families and those recovering in hospital.

As investigations continue, more information may emerge about the vehicle, the route, weather and road conditions at the time of the accident. Officials are expected to examine the circumstances surrounding the crash and determine what caused the bus to leave the road. Until that investigation is complete, the exact cause should be regarded as unknown.

The Cape Verde bus crash has left families across Fogo facing an enormous loss. The national mourning period is giving the country an opportunity to remember the victims and stand with those affected. For the families who lost children and relatives, the effects of the accident will continue long after the rescue operation and investigation have ended.
