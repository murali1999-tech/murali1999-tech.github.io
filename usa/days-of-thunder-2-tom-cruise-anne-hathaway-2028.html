Days of Thunder 2: Tom Cruise and Anne Hathaway Set for 2028

Days of Thunder is officially returning to the big screen. Tom Cruise has announced a sequel to the 1990 NASCAR racing film, with the new movie scheduled for theatrical release on June 2, 2028. Cruise will return as Cole Trickle, the character he played in the original film.

The announcement was made on August 29, 2026, at Daytona International Speedway in Florida, ahead of the NASCAR regular-season finale. Cruise shared the news with Anne Hathaway, who has joined the cast of the sequel. Paramount Pictures is behind the new film. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

Anne Hathaway will play a racecar team owner and engineer. The new role puts Hathaway into the NASCAR setting alongside Cruise, who returns to the franchise more than three decades after the original Days of Thunder was released. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

The new movie is currently being referred to as the Days of Thunder sequel and is also widely described as Days of Thunder 2. However, the official title may ultimately be different. The promotional material released by Cruise used the Days of Thunder name together with Summer 2028 rather than formally confirming a final title of Days of Thunder 2. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

The confirmed theatrical release date is June 2, 2028. Variety reported that the movie is planned for IMAX screens and is expected to receive theatrical presentations including Dolby Cinema and 4DX. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Jonathan Levine is set to direct the sequel. Will Staples is writing the screenplay, while Tom Cruise, Jerry Bruckheimer and Tommy Harper are producing. Bruckheimer produced the original Days of Thunder as well as the later Top Gun: Maverick legacy sequel. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Tom Cruise will reprise his role as Cole Trickle. The character was introduced in the 1990 movie as a talented stock-car driver trying to establish himself in NASCAR. The original film was directed by Tony Scott and featured Cruise alongside Robert Duvall, Nicole Kidman, Randy Quaid, Cary Elwes and John C. Reilly. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

The original Days of Thunder was released in 1990. The sequel's planned 2028 release means the two movies will arrive 38 years apart. The long gap between the films is one reason the announcement has attracted attention, particularly following the success of Top Gun: Maverick, another Cruise-led legacy sequel. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

Plot details for the new movie remain limited. What has been officially confirmed is that Cruise returns as Cole Trickle and Hathaway plays a team owner and engineer. More story details are expected as production moves forward.

The first promotional announcement was made at Daytona International Speedway, an important location in NASCAR history. Cruise and Hathaway appeared together beside a race car carrying the recognizable green-and-yellow styling associated with Cole Trickle and the original movie. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

The project is not simply a rumor anymore. Cruise's announcement and Paramount's release-date confirmation establish that the sequel is moving forward. Reports also indicate that filming will take place later rather than immediately, with the production schedule still developing. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

For Tom Cruise, the sequel represents another return to a character from an earlier stage of his career. Cruise has continued to work with producer Jerry Bruckheimer on major action projects, and the Days of Thunder sequel brings that partnership back to NASCAR racing.

For Anne Hathaway, the movie marks her first collaboration with Cruise. Her character's description as a team owner and engineer suggests that she will have a significant connection to the racing team rather than simply serving as a supporting character.

Fans looking for Days of Thunder 2, Days of Thunder 2028, Tom Cruise or Anne Hathaway can follow official announcements from Paramount and the actors for additional casting, production and trailer information. Until more details are announced, the June 2, 2028 theatrical date, Cruise's return and Hathaway's casting are the key confirmed facts.
