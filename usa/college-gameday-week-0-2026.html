College GameDay Week 0: Is College GameDay on Today?

College football is back on Saturday, August 29, 2026, but fans looking for the familiar College GameDay pregame show will have to wait one more week. ESPN is not sending College GameDay to a campus for Week 0, so there is no regular College GameDay episode today.

The 2026 college football season begins with a special Week 0 schedule featuring several games, including North Carolina against TCU in Dublin, Ireland. The game is scheduled for noon Eastern Time on ESPN. NC State and Virginia will also meet in Rio de Janeiro at 3:30 p.m. Eastern Time, while Alabama A&M faces Howard in the MEAC/SWAC Challenge at 7:30 p.m. Eastern Time.

For viewers searching for College GameDay today, ESPN instead has College Football Countdown. The two-hour pregame program begins at 10 a.m. Eastern Time on Saturday and leads into the North Carolina-TCU matchup. The program features Kevin Negandhi, Greg McElroy, Jesse Palmer, Booger McFarland and Pete Thamel.

So, is College GameDay on this weekend? The answer is no for Week 0. The regular College GameDay schedule begins in Week 1 on Saturday, September 5, 2026. ESPN has announced that the show will travel to Baton Rouge, Louisiana, for Clemson at LSU. The season premiere starts at 9 a.m. Eastern Time and will be a milestone 500th road show for College GameDay.

College GameDay then heads to Austin, Texas, on September 12 for Ohio State at Texas in Week 2. ESPN has already announced those first two destinations, giving fans a clear idea of when the regular Saturday morning program returns.

For fans in India, the Week 0 schedule falls into evening and late-night viewing hours because the games are being played in the United States and Ireland. The most important Week 0 matchup, North Carolina versus TCU, starts at 9:30 p.m. IST on August 29.

In short, there is no College GameDay today and no College GameDay campus show during Week 0. The first regular 2026 College GameDay broadcast is Saturday, September 5, from Baton Rouge ahead of Clemson versus LSU.
