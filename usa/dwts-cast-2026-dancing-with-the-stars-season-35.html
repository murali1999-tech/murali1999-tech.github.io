DWTS Cast 2026: Dancing with the Stars Season 35 Cast and Partners Revealed

Updated September 2, 2026

The Dancing with the Stars cast for 2026 has officially been revealed. The Season 35 celebrity lineup and professional dance partners were announced on Good Morning America, giving fans their first complete look at the couples who will compete for the Len Goodman Mirrorball Trophy.

The new DWTS season features 16 celebrity contestants from television, movies, music, sports, reality television, social media and the culinary world. Some of the biggest names in the lineup include Jenna Dewan, Julia Stiles, Harry Shum Jr., Tyler Cameron, Tatyana Ali, Giada De Laurentiis and Taylor Hanson.

The cast announcement had been highly anticipated because several contestants had already been confirmed before the full reveal. Ciara Miller, Maura Higgins, Jackson Olson and Guillermo Rodriguez were announced earlier, while the remaining stars and their professional partners were revealed on Good Morning America.

Jenna Dewan is one of the most talked-about names in the DWTS cast 2026 lineup. The actress and former professional dancer will compete with Val Chmerkovskiy. Dewan already has extensive dance experience, having worked as a backup dancer and later becoming widely known for her role in Step Up.

Julia Stiles is another major addition to the ballroom. The actress, known for movies including 10 Things I Hate About You, Save the Last Dance and the Bourne franchise, will compete with professional dancer Ezra Sosa. Her casting is one of the biggest surprises of the Season 35 announcement.

Harry Shum Jr. is also joining Dancing with the Stars Season 35. The actor and dancer, who became widely known as Mike Chang on Glee, will be paired with Jenna Johnson. Shum has extensive dance experience and has previously worked as a backup dancer for major music artists.

His pairing with Jenna Johnson is expected to attract considerable attention because both have strong performance backgrounds. Shum has appeared in dance-heavy productions including the Step Up films, while Johnson is one of the established professional dancers on DWTS.

The complete celebrity and professional pairings for Season 35 are Jenna Dewan with Val Chmerkovskiy, Julia Stiles with Ezra Sosa, Harry Shum Jr. with Jenna Johnson, Tatyana Ali with Jan Ravnik, Taylor Hanson with Britt Stewart, Ezra Frech with Daniella Karagach, Amber Glenn with Pasha Pashkov, Connor Wood with Rylee Arnold, Sarah Jane Nader with Hailey Bills, Tyler Cameron with Sharna Burgess, Giada De Laurentiis with Alan Bersten, Conner Leavitt with Adele Zaikman, Guillermo Rodriguez with Witney Carson, Maura Higgins with Mark Ballas, Ciara Miller with Brandon Armstrong and Jackson Olson with Emma Slater.

Tatyana Ali, best known for playing Ashley Banks on The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air, will dance with Jan Ravnik. Ali also has experience in music, television and Broadway, making her another performer with a varied entertainment background.

Taylor Hanson of the band Hanson will compete with Britt Stewart. Hanson became famous with his brothers during the 1990s after the success of the song MMMBop. His appearance brings a well-known music name into the Season 35 ballroom.

Olympic and Paralympic athletes are also strongly represented. Paralympic gold medalist Ezra Frech will dance with Daniella Karagach, while Olympic figure skating gold medalist Amber Glenn will compete with Pasha Pashkov.

Ezra Frech has become known for his achievements in adaptive athletics and his work advocating for athletes with limb differences. Amber Glenn arrives at DWTS after a major year in figure skating, including an Olympic gold medal as part of Team USA.

Social media personality Connor Wood, known online as Fibula, will dance with Rylee Arnold. Wood has built a large following through comedy and podcasting and also performed during coverage connected with the 2026 Olympic Games.

Sarah Jane Nader will compete with Hailey Bills. Nader is the sister of former DWTS contestant Brooks Nader and is part of the cast of Love Thy Nader. Her family connection gives the new season another link to previous DWTS contestants.

Tyler Cameron, who became famous through The Bachelorette and later built a career in real estate and television, will be partnered with returning professional Sharna Burgess. Burgess is making her return to the ballroom after several seasons away from the main competition.

Celebrity chef Giada De Laurentiis is also joining the competition. She will dance with Alan Bersten. De Laurentiis has spent years on television as a chef, cookbook author, restaurateur and host, but Season 35 will put her in a completely different environment.

Conner Leavitt, husband of Whitney Leavitt from The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives, will compete with Adele Zaikman. Zaikman earned her place on the professional DWTS cast after winning Dancing with the Stars: The Next Pro.

Four contestants who had already been announced before the full GMA reveal are also part of the official lineup. Ciara Miller from Summer House will dance with Brandon Armstrong, Maura Higgins will compete with Mark Ballas, Guillermo Rodriguez will dance with Witney Carson and Savannah Bananas star Jackson Olson will compete with Emma Slater.

Guillermo Rodriguez, the longtime Jimmy Kimmel Live! personality, has already generated plenty of interest because of his comedic personality. His partnership with Witney Carson is particularly notable because Carson won the previous season with Robert Irwin.

Maura Higgins arrives after appearances on Love Island and The Traitors. She will compete with Mark Ballas, one of the most experienced professionals connected with the show.

Ciara Miller, known from Summer House, will be paired with Brandon Armstrong. Jackson Olson, who became known through the Savannah Bananas, will dance with Emma Slater.

The professional lineup also includes several returning dancers and new faces. Sharna Burgess is returning to the ballroom, while Adele Zaikman, Hailey Bills and Jan Ravnik are among the newer professional dancers competing this season.

The DWTS season 35 hosts are also returning. Alfonso Ribeiro and Julianne Hough will serve as co-hosts, while Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli and Derek Hough return as judges.

Fans searching for the Good Morning America DWTS announcement were able to watch the cast reveal during the September 2 broadcast. Good Morning America normally airs in the morning, with the main program running from 7 a.m. to 9 a.m. Eastern Time. The Season 35 cast reveal was part of the Wednesday morning GMA coverage.

The new Dancing with the Stars season will not begin immediately after the cast announcement. Season 35 is scheduled to premiere with a two-night event on Tuesday, September 15, and Wednesday, September 16, at 8 p.m. Eastern Time on ABC and Disney+. Episodes will stream on Hulu the following day.

The 2026 season is already attracting attention because of the combination of established actors, athletes, musicians, reality stars and personalities with large social media audiences. Jenna Dewan, Julia Stiles and Harry Shum Jr. are among the most recognizable entertainment names, while Ezra Frech and Amber Glenn bring elite sporting backgrounds to the competition.

Harry Shum Jr. could be particularly interesting to watch because of his previous professional dance experience. Jenna Dewan also has a long history with dance, meaning both stars enter the ballroom with experience that is different from the typical celebrity contestant.

Julia Stiles will have a different challenge. Although she has performed extensively as an actress, competing in ballroom and Latin dance requires learning detailed choreography, technique and performance styles under the pressure of a live television competition.

For fans looking for the DWTS cast reveal, DWTS partners 2026, Dancing with the Stars pairings and information about when Dancing with the Stars starts, the complete lineup is now official. The next major date is September 15, when Season 35 begins its two-night premiere.

The Season 35 cast gives Dancing with the Stars another wide-ranging group of contestants, and the professional pairings are likely to become one of the main talking points as rehearsals continue. With experienced dancers, Olympic athletes, actors, musicians and reality personalities all competing, the new season has plenty of different storylines before the first scores are announced.
