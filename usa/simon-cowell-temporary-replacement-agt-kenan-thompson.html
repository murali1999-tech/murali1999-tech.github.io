Simon Cowell Temporary Replacement on America's Got Talent

Simon Cowell is temporarily stepping away from America's Got Talent as the NBC competition moves through its live Season 21 shows. Cowell is taking time away from the judging panel while he recovers from a routine medical procedure related to his back.

NBC has confirmed that comedian and actor Kenan Thompson will serve as a guest judge during Round 3 of the live quarterfinals. Thompson is scheduled to appear on America's Got Talent on Tuesday, September 1, 2026, at 8 p.m. Eastern and 7 p.m. Central. Cowell, 66, has missed several live episodes while dealing with a recurring back issue. His representative said the procedure is routine and that Cowell is expected to return in a few weeks. An exact return date has not been announced. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

The procedure is connected to Cowell's long-running back problems following his serious electric-bike accident in 2020. That accident resulted in a major back injury and surgery. His current procedure has been described by his representative as routine rather than an emergency. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

During the August 25 live episode, Terry Crews explained Cowell's absence to viewers. Crews said the operation could not be postponed and sent Cowell his best wishes for a speedy recovery. Cowell's chair was left empty during that episode while the other judges continued with the competition. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Kenan Thompson is not new to America's Got Talent. He previously filled in for Cowell during the live quarterfinals in 2020. His return therefore gives the show a guest judge who already has experience with the format and the judging panel. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

Thompson is best known as a longtime Saturday Night Live cast member and comedian. He has also worked extensively as an actor and television performer. His temporary AGT appearance adds a comedy background to a judging panel that already includes entertainment figures with different areas of experience.

The September 1 episode is part of the third round of America's Got Talent Season 21 quarterfinals. The competition is approaching its final stages, with more quarterfinal episodes scheduled before the semifinals and two-part finale. Current scheduling information lists the semifinals for September 15 and 16 and the finale for September 22 and 23. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

The temporary change does not mean that Cowell has permanently left America's Got Talent. Current reports describe his absence as a short-term break while he recovers from the procedure.
