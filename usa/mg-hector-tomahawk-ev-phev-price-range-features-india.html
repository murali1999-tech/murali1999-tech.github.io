MG Hector Tomahawk EV and PHEV: Price, Range, Features and India Details

MG Hector Tomahawk has arrived as one of the most talked-about new SUV launches in India. JSW MG Motor India has introduced the new Hector Tomahawk with electric and plug-in hybrid powertrain options, giving buyers more choices depending on how they use their vehicle. The new model combines the familiar Hector name with a completely different approach to electrification, technology and long-distance driving.

The MG Hector Tomahawk is designed as a large family SUV with three rows and seven seats. MG's official information highlights a 2,810 mm wheelbase, generous interior space and flexible seating. The company is positioning the vehicle as a premium family SUV that can be used for city driving as well as longer journeys. The model also has a distinctive exterior inspired by the Tomahawk concept, including the Hawk X grille and aerodynamic alloy wheels.

The MG Hector Tomahawk EV is the fully electric version. It uses an integrated battery and electric drivetrain and is aimed at customers who want zero-emission driving without giving up the size and practicality of a three-row SUV. The official MG website currently lists the Hector Tomahawk EV and PHEV together and provides booking information for both versions.

One of the most interesting technologies in the MG Tomahawk EV is Vehicle-to-Home, or V2H. According to MG, the Hector Tomahawk EV is the first car in India to offer this technology. The basic idea is that the vehicle's battery can provide power to a home during an outage. This makes the vehicle more than just a method of transportation and gives its large battery another possible use.

The electric version uses a 69.2 kWh battery according to launch information, while reported specifications put its range at around 517 km on a single charge. The EV produces 150 kW of power and 310 Nm of torque, with a claimed 0-100 km/h acceleration time of about 8.2 seconds. Actual range will depend on driving conditions, speed, weather, passenger load and other factors.

MG has also introduced the Hector Tomahawk PHEV. A plug-in hybrid electric vehicle combines a battery and electric motor with an internal combustion engine. This gives the driver the ability to travel using electric power for shorter trips while retaining the flexibility of a petrol-powered system for longer journeys. The Tomahawk PHEV is therefore aimed at customers who may not want to depend entirely on public charging infrastructure.

The Tomahawk PHEV uses a 20.5 kWh battery and is reported to offer approximately 115 km of pure electric driving range. MG describes the vehicle as part of its ADAPT multi-NEV platform, which supports different types of electrified powertrains. The company says the platform can support EV, PHEV, HEV and range-extender configurations.

One of the interesting aspects of the MG Tomahawk is its size. With three rows and seven seats, it is intended to provide enough room for a large family. MG says the vehicle has 85 percent usable floor space and focuses heavily on passenger comfort. The long wheelbase should also help provide useful legroom for passengers in the second and third rows.

The interior includes a 22.3 cm multi-information display, while the vehicle also features ambient lighting with 256 colours. The design is intended to feel more modern than the conventional Hector. A panoramic sunroof is another feature expected to appeal to buyers looking for a premium family SUV.

The MG Hector Tomahawk also comes with a strong technology focus. Modern connected-car functions, digital displays, driver assistance features and smartphone connectivity are part of the overall package. The exact feature combination can vary between variants and powertrains, so buyers should check the final specification of the vehicle they intend to purchase.

Safety is another important area for the new SUV. MG says its battery technology is subjected to extensive testing, including thousands of bench tests, vehicle validation and testing in extreme environmental conditions. The battery management system continuously monitors battery health. The vehicle is based on the MG ADAPT platform, while the official NCAP rating is expected to be shared once certification is completed.

Price is naturally one of the biggest search topics around the MG Tomahawk. Launch reports have mentioned introductory pricing from around ₹13.99 lakh under the Battery-as-a-Service model, while the vehicle price without the battery arrangement is higher. The official MG website currently displays pricing information for the Hector Tomahawk and asks customers to check the latest offer and variant details. Prices can change depending on variant, battery ownership model, taxes and other charges.

The Battery-as-a-Service approach is particularly interesting for the MG Tomahawk EV. Instead of paying the full battery cost upfront, customers can use a separate battery subscription arrangement. This can reduce the initial purchase price, although the customer then pays an ongoing battery usage charge. Buyers should compare the total cost over several years before deciding which ownership structure is better for them.

Bookings for the MG Hector Tomahawk have opened, and MG says deliveries will begin according to the rollout schedule. Official information indicates that EV deliveries are planned from September 2026, while PHEV deliveries are planned from November 2026. Actual delivery dates can vary according to location, variant, booking date and dealership availability.

The MG Hector Tomahawk is also significant for MG India because it expands the company's electrified product strategy. The company already sells the Hector alongside models such as the Windsor EV, Comet EV and ZS EV. The Tomahawk takes the brand into a larger three-row electrified SUV segment and gives buyers an alternative to conventional petrol and diesel family SUVs.

For buyers considering the MG Hector, the Tomahawk is a very different proposition. The regular Hector remains a petrol-powered SUV with five- and seven-seat options, while the Tomahawk focuses heavily on electrification and new technology. The conventional Hector continues to offer a 1.5-litre turbocharged petrol engine, while the Tomahawk provides EV and PHEV choices.

The MG Tomahawk EV could be particularly attractive to families who have access to home charging and mainly drive within cities or nearby areas. The PHEV could appeal to people who want electric driving for daily trips but still want the flexibility of an engine for longer journeys. The right choice will depend heavily on charging access, daily distance and ownership costs.

The new MG Hector Tomahawk also has a strong visual identity. The Hawk X front design, large proportions and aerodynamic details make it look different from the existing Hector. MG is clearly trying to create a more futuristic appearance rather than simply turning the current Hector into an electric vehicle.

Another important point is that the Tomahawk is not simply about electric range. Its combination of seven seats, connected technology, V2H capability and multiple electrified powertrain choices gives the model a broader purpose. It is aimed at buyers who want a family vehicle that can also become part of their home's energy system.

For anyone searching for MG Hector Tomahawk, MG Tomahawk EV, MG Tomahawk PHEV, MG Hector Hawk or MG India, the new SUV is one of the company's most important launches of 2026. The EV offers an all-electric driving experience, while the PHEV provides a combination of electric and engine-powered driving.

The final buying decision should be based on the exact variant price, battery arrangement, charging requirements, warranty, insurance and local on-road cost. Buyers should also take a test drive and confirm the latest specifications with an MG dealership before making a booking.

The MG Hector Tomahawk represents a major step in the expansion of electrified family SUVs in India. With a seven-seat layout, modern technology, EV and PHEV options, a large battery, V2H capability and a distinctive design, it is positioned as a vehicle for buyers who want more than a traditional petrol SUV. As deliveries begin, real-world range, charging performance and long-term ownership costs will become clearer and will ultimately determine how successful the MG Tomahawk becomes in the Indian market.
