Ciara Pregnant: Ciara and Russell Wilson Expect Baby No. 5

Ciara and Russell Wilson are preparing to welcome another member to their family. The singer has announced that she is pregnant with her fifth child, and the baby will be her fourth child with husband Russell Wilson. The pregnancy announcement was shared on September 6, 2026, giving fans a major piece of celebrity news that had been anticipated for some time.

The announcement answered a question that many fans had been asking: is Ciara pregnant? The answer is yes. Ciara publicly revealed the pregnancy herself, sharing photos and video that showed her growing baby bump. Russell Wilson was also part of the announcement, making the news a special moment for the couple and their family.

Ciara and Russell Wilson used a simple and personal idea to reveal the news. The couple showed a clothesline with shirts representing the children already in their family, followed by a blank baby outfit representing the newest addition. The announcement also included a playful five-finger gesture, pointing toward the arrival of baby number five.

Ciara and Russell Wilson have been married since 2016 and have built a blended family together. Ciara is already a mother to her oldest son, Future Zahir, from a previous relationship. With Russell Wilson, she shares daughter Sienna Princess, son Win Harrison and daughter Amora Princess. The new baby will therefore become the fifth child in Ciara's family and the fourth child she shares with Wilson.

The pregnancy news is particularly interesting because Russell Wilson had previously spoken about wanting to expand the family. After the birth of their daughter Amora in December 2023, Wilson jokingly brought up the possibility of another baby. Ciara later talked about the conversation and explained that she loved motherhood while also joking that Wilson needed to slow down after the arrival of their newest child.

Over time, however, Ciara made it clear that she was open to having another child when the timing was right. In a 2026 interview, she spoke warmly about motherhood and the possibility of welcoming another baby. The latest announcement shows that the couple has now reached that moment.

The news has attracted considerable attention because both Ciara and Russell Wilson have large fan bases. Ciara is known for her music, performances, fashion and business projects, while Wilson became one of the most recognizable quarterbacks of his generation during his NFL career. Their relationship has also been followed closely by fans because they frequently share family moments and milestones.

For Ciara, this pregnancy comes during another important stage of her career and personal life. She has continued working on music and performing while raising her children. She has previously spoken about bringing her family into her busy schedule and about how much she values spending time with her children.

Russell Wilson is also entering a new professional chapter. After a long NFL career, he announced his retirement from professional football in 2026 and is moving into a broadcasting role. That means the couple is entering a period of significant change both professionally and personally.

At this point, the couple has not publicly announced the baby's sex or a specific due date. Fans are likely to learn more as Ciara's pregnancy progresses. For now, the main news is that Ciara is expecting her fifth child and her fourth baby with Russell Wilson.

The couple's announcement also gives a little insight into how they view their growing family. Rather than using a complicated announcement, they chose a relaxed family setting and a simple visual representation of the children they already have. The blank baby outfit was a clear symbol of the newest member who will soon join them.

Ciara and Russell Wilson have previously celebrated major family moments publicly, but this announcement has a particularly emotional meaning because it represents another chapter in their family story. The new baby will join a family that already includes Future, Sienna, Win and Amora. Ciara and Russell Wilson have often spoken positively about family life, and the couple's latest announcement suggests that their journey as parents is continuing into another exciting chapter.

As more information becomes available, fans will likely be watching for updates about the baby's due date, sex, name and Ciara's pregnancy journey. Until the couple makes those details public, it is best to rely on their own announcement and confirmed reports rather than social media speculation.
