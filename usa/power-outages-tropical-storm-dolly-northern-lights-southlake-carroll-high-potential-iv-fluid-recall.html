Power Outages

Power outages can happen because of severe weather, equipment failures, accidents, wildfires or problems with the electrical grid. People experiencing an outage should first check their local utility company's outage map for current information about the affected area and estimated restoration time. During storms, residents should stay away from downed power lines and report dangerous conditions to the utility or emergency services.

Northern Lights Tonight

The northern lights, also called aurora borealis, appear when charged particles from the Sun interact with Earth's upper atmosphere. Visibility depends on solar activity, cloud cover, darkness and location. The best opportunities are generally found at higher northern latitudes, although strong geomagnetic storms can push the aurora farther south.

Anyone hoping to see the northern lights tonight should find a location away from bright city lights and look toward the northern horizon. A dark sky and clear weather are important. Aurora forecasts can change quickly, so checking a current space-weather forecast shortly before going outside is recommended.

IV Fluid Recall

The FDA recently posted several important Baxter recalls involving intravenous products. On August 26, 2026, the FDA published a voluntary nationwide recall involving two lots of Baxter 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection, USP, 500 mL VIAFLEX containers because of the potential presence of fiberglass particulate matter. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

The FDA also reported a separate recall involving one lot of Baxter 70% Dextrose Injection because stainless steel particulate matter was potentially present in the solution. The agency said intravenous administration of particulate matter can result in serious health consequences. Healthcare facilities should check the affected product information and follow the manufacturer's recall instructions. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}
