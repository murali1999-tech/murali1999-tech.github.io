NASCAR Results Today: Ryan Preece Wins Daytona, Standings and Chase Update

Ryan Preece answered the biggest question in NASCAR on Saturday night by winning the Coke Zero Sugar 400 at Daytona International Speedway. Preece earned his first career NASCAR Cup Series victory in dramatic fashion, taking the checkered flag after a late caution ended the overtime finish. The win also gave him the final place in the 2026 NASCAR Cup Series Chase.

The Daytona race was the regular-season finale, making the result particularly important for the NASCAR standings. Preece entered the night outside the Chase field and needed a major result to move ahead of Shane van Gisbergen. He delivered exactly that, winning in his 249th career Cup Series start and securing a place among the 16 drivers competing for the 2026 championship.

Ryan Preece drove the No. 60 RFK Racing Ford and led five laps during the 166-lap race. The scheduled distance was 160 laps, but the race went six laps beyond the original distance because of the overtime finish. Daniel Suarez finished second, followed by Michael McDowell, Tyler Reddick and Ricky Stenhouse Jr. in the top five.

So, who won the NASCAR race today? Ryan Preece was the winner at Daytona. His victory was officially confirmed after post-race inspection, with no issues reported on the No. 60 Ford. The result gave Preece his first Cup Series victory and moved him into the final Chase position.

Zane Smith was also involved in the decisive closing laps. Smith's No. 38 Ford led at the white flag after starting the overtime restart near the front. During the final lap, Smith attempted to block Tyler Reddick and was turned, triggering a multicar incident. Preece was ahead when NASCAR displayed the caution flag, securing the victory.

Zane Smith ultimately finished 32nd in the Daytona results. Despite the disappointing ending, Smith had been one of the drivers involved in the fight for position during the final restart. His result also demonstrated how quickly the standings can change during a Daytona superspeedway race.

Shane van Gisbergen had entered Daytona holding the final Chase position and a 31-point advantage over Preece. He won Stage 1 but was caught in the late-race incident and finished 28th. The result left van Gisbergen 17 points behind Preece in the battle for the final Chase spot, eliminating him from the postseason.

Van Gisbergen's elimination was one of the biggest stories from the NASCAR Cup Series regular-season finale. The Trackhouse Racing driver had already recorded two victories during the 2026 season, but the regular-season points battle ultimately determined his fate. His Daytona result was not enough to hold the final Chase position.

The other drivers who clinched the final three Chase positions at Daytona were Bubba Wallace and Austin Cindric along with Preece. They joined the drivers who had already secured their places, including Denny Hamlin, Ryan Blaney, Tyler Reddick, Ty Gibbs, Chase Briscoe, Christopher Bell, Kyle Larson, Chase Elliott, Joey Logano, Chris Buescher, Daniel Suarez, Carson Hocevar and William Byron.

Denny Hamlin entered the final regular-season race with the No. 1 seed already secured. Hamlin had built a substantial points advantage before Daytona, while Ryan Blaney was second in the standings following his victory at New Hampshire. The focus entering Daytona was therefore heavily concentrated on the final Chase positions.

The Daytona result sets up the 10-race NASCAR Cup Series Chase. The postseason begins at Darlington Raceway with the Southern 500 on September 6. The championship battle will continue through the fall before the final race at Homestead-Miami Speedway in November.

For fans searching for NASCAR results today, NASCAR standings, NASCAR points standings, Ryan Preece, Zane Smith or Shane van Gisbergen, the Daytona finale provided one of the most dramatic conclusions of the season. Preece went from fighting for the final Chase place to becoming a race winner, while van Gisbergen saw his championship hopes end on the same night.

The result also adds another memorable chapter to the NASCAR Cup Series history at Daytona. The combination of overtime, a late caution and a championship-position battle created an unpredictable finish. Preece took advantage of the opportunity and became one of the central stories heading into the 2026 postseason.
