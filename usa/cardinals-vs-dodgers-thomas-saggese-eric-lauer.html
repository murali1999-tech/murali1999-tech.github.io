Cardinals vs Dodgers: Thomas Saggese Stars as St. Louis Wins 13-8

Baseball action during a Major League Baseball game.

The St. Louis Cardinals defeated the Los Angeles Dodgers 13-8 on Tuesday night, September 1, 2026, at Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles. The Cardinals' offense produced five home runs, with Thomas Saggese leading the way by hitting two home runs in a major offensive performance.

The result gave St. Louis an important win at the beginning of a nine-game road trip. The Cardinals had entered the game struggling, having lost seven of their previous nine games. Their bats changed the story quickly against the Dodgers, producing runs throughout the game and building a large early advantage.

Thomas Saggese was the biggest offensive star for St. Louis. The Cardinals infielder hit two home runs, including a solo shot in the second inning and another home run later in the game. His first homer came against Dodgers starting pitcher Eric Lauer and traveled an estimated 415 feet.

Saggese's first home run gave the Cardinals a 2-0 lead after Ramón Urías had already opened the scoring with a solo homer. The back-to-back home runs immediately put pressure on Lauer and gave St. Louis momentum.

The Cardinals then broke the game open in the third inning. St. Louis scored five runs in the inning, turning a 2-0 lead into a 7-0 advantage. The rally included RBI hits and productive at-bats throughout the lineup.

One of the important moments came from rookie catcher Leo Bernal, who recorded his first major league hit with a two-run single. His contribution helped extend the Cardinals' lead and gave St. Louis another boost during an already impressive offensive inning.

Eric Lauer had a difficult night for Los Angeles. The left-hander allowed seven runs in three innings before leaving the game after throwing 63 pitches. He gave up the two early home runs and then faced a large rally in the third inning.

Lauer entered the game having been an important part of the Dodgers' pitching plans since joining the club earlier in the season. His performance against St. Louis was one of his roughest outings since becoming a member of Los Angeles.

According to the game statistics, Lauer finished with seven runs allowed over three innings and two strikeouts. The Cardinals consistently put runners on base and took advantage of opportunities when the Dodgers struggled to stop the rally.

Los Angeles did respond in the bottom of the third inning. Mookie Betts delivered a bases-clearing double that brought three runs home and reduced the Cardinals' advantage from seven runs to four. The Dodgers had opportunities to make the game even closer, but St. Louis continued producing runs.

Jordan Walker then hit a two-run home run in the fourth inning, his 28th homer of the season. That shot restored a comfortable Cardinals lead and pushed the score to 9-3.

Saggese added his second home run in the fifth inning. His second long ball was another important blow against the Dodgers and highlighted his contribution to one of St. Louis' best offensive games of the season.

The Cardinals eventually finished with five home runs. Ramón Urías, Thomas Saggese, Jordan Walker and Joshua Báez all went deep during the game. The five-homer performance gave St. Louis enough offense to withstand several Dodgers scoring opportunities.

Michael McGreevy started the game for St. Louis. He earned the victory despite allowing four runs during his five innings of work. The Cardinals bullpen then worked to protect the lead and prevent Los Angeles from completing a comeback.

The Dodgers continued to fight late in the game, but St. Louis maintained control. Los Angeles scored additional runs, but the Cardinals had already built enough of a cushion to keep the Dodgers at a distance.

Mookie Betts was one of the main bright spots for Los Angeles. He collected two doubles and drove in four runs. Will Smith also returned to the Dodgers lineup and contributed two RBIs, giving the home team some positive offensive moments despite the defeat.

For the Dodgers, the loss continued a difficult stretch. Los Angeles has dropped five of its last seven games, increasing the pressure as the regular season moves deeper into September. The Dodgers remain in a strong position in the National League West, but their recent results have raised questions about consistency.

For St. Louis, the victory was particularly valuable because the Cardinals are trying to remain in the National League wild-card race. The team entered the series needing wins during a difficult road stretch, and the offensive explosion against Los Angeles provided an important start.

Thomas Saggese's performance was especially notable because he had recently returned to the Cardinals after being recalled from Triple-A Memphis on August 28. His two-homer game immediately gave the club a major offensive contribution.

Saggese is a versatile player who has appeared at multiple positions during his professional career. The 24-year-old was originally drafted by the Texas Rangers in the fifth round of the 2020 MLB Draft before becoming part of the Cardinals organization.

The Cardinals' victory also gave the team confidence heading into the rest of the series. The Dodgers and Cardinals are scheduled to continue their series at Dodger Stadium, with Yoshinobu Yamamoto expected to start for Los Angeles in the next game.

The Dodgers will be looking for a response after allowing 13 runs and five home runs. Their pitching staff will need to limit the Cardinals' power hitters, while the Los Angeles offense will try to provide more support for its starting pitcher.

The Cardinals, meanwhile, will attempt to build on the momentum created by their biggest offensive performance of the series opener. Saggese's two home runs, Walker's 28th homer and the contributions from Urías and Báez showed how dangerous the lineup can be when several players produce at the same time.

The final score was St. Louis Cardinals 13, Los Angeles Dodgers 8. The Cardinals hit five home runs, Thomas Saggese hit two of them, and Eric Lauer was charged with seven runs in three innings. The result gave St. Louis an important road victory while extending the Dodgers' recent struggles.

As the September schedule continues, every game becomes increasingly important for teams fighting for playoff positioning. The Cardinals will continue their road trip looking for more wins, while the Dodgers will try to regain their form before the postseason race reaches its final stages.
