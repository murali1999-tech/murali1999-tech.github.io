What Does the MLB Restricted List Mean?

The MLB restricted list is a roster designation used when a player is unavailable to participate for reasons that do not fit ordinary injury or suspension categories.

Examples can include certain personal matters, extended absences, visa issues or other circumstances in which a player is unavailable to his club.

Being placed on the restricted list does not automatically mean that a player has been suspended for violating an MLB rule. The circumstances behind the designation are important.

In Marte's current situation, the Diamondbacks have cited a personal matter rather than announcing a baseball-related injury.

Why MLB Uses the Restricted List

MLB teams have to maintain accurate active-roster information. When a contracted player cannot participate for an extended or unusual reason, the restricted list provides a mechanism for removing the player from the active roster while retaining his contractual rights.

The designation is therefore different from the injured list. An injured player is unavailable because of a physical condition, while the restricted list can cover certain non-baseball circumstances.
