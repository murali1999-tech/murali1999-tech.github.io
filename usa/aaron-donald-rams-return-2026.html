Aaron Donald Returns to the Los Angeles Rams in 2026

Aaron Donald is back in the NFL. The legendary defensive tackle has come out of retirement and returned to the Los Angeles Rams, the team that he played for throughout his entire professional career. The Rams announced his return on August 30, 2026, ending a retirement that began in March 2024.

Donald is one of the most accomplished defensive players of his generation. During his first ten NFL seasons, he established himself as one of the most dominant interior defensive linemen in league history. His combination of strength, speed, technique and ability to pressure quarterbacks made him a major problem for opposing offenses.

Donald originally entered the NFL in 2014 after the Rams selected him with the 13th overall pick in the draft. At that time, the franchise was based in St. Louis. He quickly became an important part of the team's defense and eventually continued with the organization after the Rams moved back to Los Angeles.

His career numbers are impressive. According to the Rams, Donald played 154 regular-season games and made 150 starts. He finished his first NFL career with 543 tackles, 111 sacks, 176 tackles for loss, 790 pressures, 256 quarterback hits, 525 quarterback hurries, 24 forced fumbles, seven fumble recoveries and 21 passes defended.

Donald also won the NFL Defensive Player of the Year award three times. He received the honor in 2017, 2018 and 2020. He was selected as a first-team All-Pro eight times, another indication of how consistently he performed at an elite level throughout his career.

One of the biggest moments of Donald's career came during the 2021 season. The Rams reached Super Bowl LVI and defeated the Cincinnati Bengals 23-20 at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles. Donald played a major role in the final defensive stand of the game, helping the Rams secure their second Super Bowl championship.

Donald announced his retirement on March 15, 2024. At the time, he had completed ten seasons with the Rams and had already established a strong Hall of Fame case. He did not play during the 2024 or 2025 NFL seasons.

That changed in 2026. During the offseason, the Rams made a major defensive addition by acquiring star pass rusher Myles Garrett. The possibility of Donald returning began to attract attention after Rams head coach Sean McVay contacted Donald during discussions surrounding Garrett.

Donald began working toward a possible comeback during the summer. McVay said that Donald was following a strict training process and evaluating how his body responded to football activity. The decision was not announced immediately, but the possibility of a return became increasingly realistic as training continued.

On August 30, 2026, the decision became official. Donald returned to the Rams and participated in team activities as the organization prepared for the new season. His return gives Los Angeles another experienced defensive star and reunites him with the franchise where he spent his entire NFL career.

Donald is now 35 years old, so his return will naturally attract attention regarding his conditioning and how much he can contribute after two seasons away from regular-season football. He does not need to reproduce every aspect of his younger years to make an impact. Even his experience, technique and understanding of offensive blocking schemes could provide significant value to the Rams.

The Rams already have a talented defensive front. Myles Garrett is one of the league's premier pass rushers, while the team also has players such as Braden Fiske, Kobie Turner and Poona Ford on the defensive line. Donald's return adds another major name to that group.

For opposing offenses, the potential combination of Donald and Garrett is one of the most interesting storylines heading into the 2026 season. Donald has spent his career attacking the middle of offensive lines, while Garrett has traditionally created pressure from the edge. If both players are healthy and effective, opposing quarterbacks could face pressure from multiple directions.

Donald also brings championship experience to the Rams. He was part of the team that won Super Bowl LVI and has played in two Super Bowls during his career. He has also appeared in 11 postseason games, recording 34 tackles and six sacks in the playoffs.

His return is particularly notable because Donald had previously appeared comfortable with retirement. He had stepped away after ten seasons at the highest level of professional football. His decision to return suggests that his desire to compete had not completely disappeared.

The Rams have included Donald on their initial 53-man roster for the 2026 season. That makes his comeback more than just a training-camp story. Number 99 is officially back with the organization as the team begins its latest pursuit of a championship.

For Rams supporters, Donald's return is also a significant moment because of his connection with the franchise. He was drafted by the Rams, developed into one of the NFL's best defensive players with the organization and helped bring a Super Bowl championship to Los Angeles. His entire professional identity has been connected to the Rams.

His career statistics also explain why his return has generated so much attention. Donald's 111 career sacks rank among the most remarkable totals ever produced by an interior defensive lineman. He consistently generated pressure despite facing double teams and offensive game plans specifically designed to slow him down.

The 2026 comeback will now add another chapter to his career. The biggest questions will be how much he can play, how quickly he returns to game form and what kind of role the Rams give him during the season. Those answers will become clearer as he works through practices and prepares for regular-season action.

Regardless of what happens next, Aaron Donald has already built one of the strongest defensive resumes in modern NFL history. Three Defensive Player of the Year awards, eight first-team All-Pro selections, 111 career sacks and a Super Bowl championship make his career remarkable on their own.

Now the former defensive superstar has another opportunity. After announcing his retirement in 2024, Aaron Donald has returned to the Los Angeles Rams in 2026 and is preparing to compete again in the NFL.
