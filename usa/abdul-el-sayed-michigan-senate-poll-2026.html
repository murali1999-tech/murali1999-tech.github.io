Abdul El-Sayed Michigan Senate Poll: Latest 2026 Results and Analysis

Updated: August 18, 2026
Latest update: Abdul El-Sayed has emerged as the Democratic nominee for the 2026 U.S. Senate race in Michigan after winning the Democratic primary. Recent polling before the primary showed significant movement in his favor, although polls differed substantially depending on the field dates and methodology.

Abdul El-Sayed is one of the most closely watched candidates in the 2026 Michigan Senate election. The former Detroit Health Department director entered the Democratic Senate primary and built support among progressive voters. The race attracted national attention because the Democratic contest became an important test of the party's progressive and establishment wings.

Polling changed considerably during the campaign. In July, some surveys showed Haley Stevens ahead of El-Sayed, while later surveys showed El-Sayed moving into a clear lead. That late movement made the Michigan Senate primary one of the most closely watched Democratic contests of the 2026 election cycle.

Latest Abdul El-Sayed Michigan Senate Poll

One of the final pre-primary polls from Zenith Research, conducted July 26-30 among 536 likely Democratic primary voters, showed Abdul El-Sayed leading Haley Stevens 53% to 34%, with 11% undecided. The poll reported a margin of sampling error of ±4.2 percentage points. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

The same Zenith survey found El-Sayed ahead 59% to 41% when undecided voters were asked which candidate they were leaning toward. The poll also reported El-Sayed with a 69% favorable rating and Stevens with a 49% favorable rating among likely Democratic primary voters. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Emerson Poll Shows El-Sayed Ahead

An Emerson College poll conducted July 26-27 also showed a substantial advantage for El-Sayed. According to results published by his campaign, the survey put El-Sayed at 54% and Haley Stevens at 39%, with 6% undecided. With leaners included, the reported figures were 57% for El-Sayed and 41% for Stevens. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

The Emerson survey was especially notable because it came shortly after Governor Gretchen Whitmer endorsed El-Sayed. The poll also found El-Sayed performing strongly among independent voters in the Democratic primary electorate. Because the results were published on the candidate's campaign website, readers should consider the campaign source alongside independent polling coverage.

Earlier Michigan Senate Polls Were Much Closer

The Michigan Senate primary looked considerably different earlier in July. A Detroit News/WDIV poll reported in mid-July showed Haley Stevens ahead of El-Sayed by approximately seven points, with Stevens at 48% and El-Sayed at 41%. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

The contrast between that poll and the late-July surveys illustrates how quickly the Michigan Democratic primary changed during the final weeks of the campaign.

The July WDIV/Detroit News survey also found differences among demographic groups. Stevens performed better among Black voters, non-college-educated voters and Detroit-area voters, while El-Sayed had advantages among college-educated and White voters and among voters identifying with the progressive wing of the Democratic Party. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Why the polling matters: Polls are snapshots of voter preferences at a particular moment. Different results can occur because surveys use different samples, field dates, turnout assumptions, questionnaires and methodologies.

Abdul El-Sayed Wins the Democratic Nomination

El-Sayed ultimately won the Democratic primary, making history as the first Muslim nominated by the Democratic Party for the U.S. Senate in Michigan, according to reporting from The Associated Press. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

His victory transformed the polling question from "Who will win the Democratic primary?" to a broader general-election question: can El-Sayed defeat Republican nominee Mike Rogers in November?

The general election is expected to receive significant national attention because control of the U.S. Senate could depend on competitive races such as Michigan.

Abdul El-Sayed vs Mike Rogers Polling

General-election polling has produced a much closer picture than some of the late Democratic primary polls. One polling aggregation published in late July showed El-Sayed with a narrow advantage over Republican Mike Rogers, approximately 45.5% to 44.1%, or a Democratic advantage of about 1.3 percentage points. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

Other earlier surveys showed Rogers ahead or the race essentially tied. This means the Michigan Senate contest should be considered competitive rather than a guaranteed victory for either party.

Michigan's political environment will be particularly important in the general election. Both campaigns are likely to focus on voters in the Detroit metropolitan area, suburban communities, smaller cities and rural parts of the state.

Why Michigan Is Important in 2026

Michigan is one of the major battleground states in American politics. Its Senate race has attracted substantial national spending and attention from both political parties.

Recent reporting from Axios said the Senate Leadership Fund, a major Republican-aligned super PAC, was increasing its investment in Michigan with millions of dollars in television and digital advertising targeting El-Sayed while supporting Mike Rogers. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

The heavy outside spending demonstrates how important Michigan is to the national Senate battle. Advertising is likely to increase as the November election approaches.

What Could Influence the Michigan Senate Race?

Several issues could influence the final result. The economy, healthcare, immigration, abortion policy, foreign policy, taxes and public safety are likely to feature prominently in campaign messaging.

Candidate favorability will also matter. El-Sayed enters the general election with a strong progressive identity, while Republicans are expected to emphasize concerns about his policy positions and ideological background.

At the same time, Democrats are likely to emphasize issues involving healthcare, economic opportunity and opposition to Republican policies.

How to Read Michigan Senate Polls

Readers should avoid judging the entire race from a single poll. Polling organizations can produce different results because they use different voter samples and methodologies.

  • Check when the poll was conducted.
  • Check the sample size.
  • Check whether respondents are registered or likely voters.
  • Look at the margin of error.
  • Compare several polls rather than relying on one survey.
  • Pay attention to whether the poll is a primary or general-election survey.

Polling averages can sometimes provide a better overview than a single survey, although averages also depend on which polls are included and how they are weighted.

Abdul El-Sayed's Political Profile

El-Sayed previously served as executive director of the Detroit Health Department and became known nationally after running for Michigan governor in 2018. His political identity has been associated with progressive healthcare and economic policies.

His 2026 Senate campaign attracted support from progressive voters and national figures who viewed Michigan as an opportunity to elect a candidate representing the party's progressive wing.

His nomination also carries historical significance because of his Muslim background. The Associated Press reported that his nomination made him the first Muslim nominated by the Democratic Party for a U.S. Senate seat in Michigan. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

What Happens Next?

With the Democratic primary complete, attention turns to the general election. El-Sayed will face Republican Mike Rogers in the November Senate contest.

The general election is expected to be closely watched by national political organizations. Campaign spending, advertising, debates, voter turnout and the national political environment could all influence the final result.

For voters, the most useful information will come from updated polling, candidate platforms and official election information as the campaign progresses.
