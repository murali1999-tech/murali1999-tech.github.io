Aldi September 2026 New Product Releases

Aldi is filling its September 2026 shelves with a large selection of new and returning products built around the arrival of fall. The September lineup includes pumpkin-flavored foods, apple-inspired treats, comfort foods, frozen appetizers, seasonal cheeses and a major selection of German specialties. Aldi's weekly Finds are scheduled throughout the month, giving shoppers new items to look for from early September through the end of the month.

The September releases are especially focused on autumn flavors. Pumpkin, maple, caramel apple, cinnamon and other seasonal combinations appear across cookies, spreads, baking products, snacks and desserts. Aldi is also bringing back popular fall favorites while introducing new products that are designed for quick meals and entertaining.

Seasonal Aldi products shown for general visual context.

One of the biggest attractions this September is the return of pumpkin-flavored products. Shoppers can expect items such as Bake Shop Pumpkin Sandwich Cookies and Baker's Corner Pumpkin Quick Bread Mix. These products are aimed at people who want familiar fall flavors without spending a lot of time preparing desserts or baked goods from scratch.

Benton's is also bringing several fall cookie flavors to Aldi shelves. The September selection includes soft baked cookies featuring combinations such as apple crisp, pumpkin spice and maple pecan. These flavors fit into the broader autumn theme that runs through much of Aldi's September product selection.

Another popular fall flavor combination is pumpkin and maple. Berryhill is introducing seasonal spreads that include pumpkin-maple and salted-caramel apple varieties. These types of spreads can be used with toast, crackers, pastries or other snacks and give shoppers another easy way to add seasonal flavors to everyday foods.

For dinner, Aldi is introducing several comfort-food options. Specially Selected Fall Harvest Mac & Cheese combines autumn-inspired ingredients with the familiar convenience of macaroni and cheese. There are also new pizzas, flatbreads and other prepared foods aimed at shoppers looking for quick meals during the busy start of fall.

Fall food shown for general illustration.

Mama Cozzi's is another name to watch in September. The Aldi brand is adding stuffed-crust pizzas and other seasonal pizza options. These products are particularly useful for families and shoppers who want an inexpensive dinner that can be prepared quickly in the oven.

Frozen appetizers are another major part of the September releases. Aldi is adding products such as Corned Beef Reuben Bites, Chili Lime Corn Ribs, Goat Cheese Bites, Truffle Arancini Bites and Casa Mamita Stuffed Nachos. The assortment gives shoppers several options for parties, game days, casual dinners and fall gatherings.

Casa Mamita is also bringing Family Size Chicken & Cheese Enchiladas to the lineup. The ready-to-heat option is designed for shoppers who want a convenient family meal. Aldi continues to use its private-label brands to offer prepared foods at prices that are generally lower than many comparable specialty products.

September is also an important month for Aldi because of German Week. The German-themed selection is expected to arrive around the middle of the month, bringing back many of the retailer's popular Deutsche Küche products. The timing connects with Oktoberfest season and gives shoppers access to German-style foods that are not always available throughout the year.

German Week products include items such as schnitzels, Bavarian soft pretzel twists, savory strudels, white asparagus, Doppelino Cookies and Peanut Puffs. Other German-inspired products can also appear depending on the individual store and weekly Aldi Finds schedule.

For shoppers who enjoy specialty foods, German Week is one of the most anticipated Aldi events of the month. Availability can be limited because many of these products are seasonal or special purchases. That means a product listed in a weekly Aldi advertisement may not remain on shelves for the entire month.

Aldi is also preparing shoppers for Halloween later in September. Starting around September 30, seasonal Emporium Selection cheeses are expected to appear with Halloween-inspired themes. Examples include Death By Garlic Black Garlic Cheddar and Scary Pumpkin Spice Wensleydale.

Autumn and Halloween seasonal imagery for illustration.

The Halloween selection goes beyond food. Aldi's seasonal merchandise can include themed drinkware, kitchen accessories, ceramic pieces and other home products. Pumpkin, ghost and skull designs are expected to become more visible as September comes to an end and Halloween shopping begins.

Home decor is another part of the September Aldi lineup. Seasonal items can include harvest decorations, pillows and fall-themed kitchen products. Crofton has also been associated with seasonal ceramic kitchenware, including pumpkin-themed pieces that can be used for serving or decorating.

One product that may attract shoppers looking for something different is the updated Kirkwood Red Bag Chicken Sweet Heat variety. The product combines the convenience of Aldi's popular frozen chicken with a hotter flavor profile. It is another example of Aldi using familiar products while introducing new variations for shoppers.

Aldi's September releases are not all about sweets. There are also savory products designed for quick lunches and dinners, along with frozen foods that can be stored and prepared when needed. Maple, bacon, cheese, pumpkin, apple and other seasonal flavors appear throughout the selection, giving shoppers plenty of choices as summer transitions into fall.

The exact products available can vary by location. Aldi Finds are often limited-time items, and individual stores may sell out before the advertised period ends. Some products may also arrive on different dates depending on the weekly release schedule. Shoppers looking for a specific item should check their local Aldi store and the current weekly ad before making a trip.

Aldi's official schedule shows new Aldi Finds arriving during the first week of September, with additional releases continuing through the month. The September 2026 lineup therefore works less like one single product launch and more like a series of weekly drops. This approach encourages shoppers to check the store regularly because the selection changes frequently.

The September lineup is particularly strong for people who enjoy seasonal food. Pumpkin spice cookies, maple pecan treats, caramel apple products and fall baking mixes provide plenty of options for breakfasts and desserts. Meanwhile, pizzas, enchiladas, mac and cheese and frozen appetizers make it easier to put together quick meals without buying expensive restaurant food.

Overall, Aldi's September 2026 new product releases combine autumn comfort foods with seasonal German specialties and early Halloween products. The month includes new cookies, baking mixes, frozen appetizers, pizzas, prepared meals, cheeses, snacks and home products. With new Aldi Finds arriving throughout September, shoppers have several opportunities to discover limited-time items before the fall selection changes again.

Anyone planning an Aldi trip in September should pay particular attention to the weekly release dates. Popular seasonal products can disappear quickly, especially German Week specialties and Halloween-themed items. Checking the latest Aldi Finds information before visiting can help shoppers decide which products are worth looking for first.
