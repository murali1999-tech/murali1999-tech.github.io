Dover Delaware Tornado and Arsenal vs Coventry: Latest News and Updates

Updated: August 21, 2026
BREAKING WEATHER: A confirmed tornado moved through Dover, Delaware, on Thursday, August 20, causing significant damage across parts of the city. Cleanup and damage assessment are continuing.

Dover Delaware Tornado

Dover, Delaware, is recovering after a powerful tornado moved through the city on Thursday evening. The National Weather Service reported a confirmed, radar-detected tornado near Dover at approximately 6:18 p.m. EDT, with the storm moving northeast at around 30 mph.

The tornado caused damage to buildings, trees, power lines and other infrastructure. Several locations along North DuPont Highway were affected, while debris was scattered across roads and properties.

The City of Dover declared a state of emergency after the storm moved through the area. Emergency crews, police, utility workers and public works teams began responding to the damage as conditions improved.

Latest situation: Cleanup crews are working across Dover, while officials assess the extent of the damage. Residents have been urged to stay away from dangerous areas and allow emergency and utility crews to work safely.

National Weather Service Confirmed Tornado

The National Weather Service issued a severe weather statement during the storm. At 6:18 p.m. EDT, the agency reported a confirmed large and extremely dangerous tornado over the Dover Speedway area.

The warning described a life-threatening situation and warned residents about flying debris. The storm was moving northeast at approximately 30 mph.

Earlier in the afternoon, another tornado warning had been issued for parts of central Kent County. The severe weather system produced multiple warnings across the region as storms moved through Delaware and surrounding areas.

Dover Damage and Power Outages

Reports from Dover indicate that the storm damaged several buildings and knocked down numerous trees and power lines. Some roads were affected by debris and flooding, while power outages disrupted parts of the city.

The Super Lodge and Days Inn on North DuPont Highway were among the locations reported to have suffered significant damage. Images and videos from the area showed debris around buildings and roads.

Officials also identified the Division Street and Lockerman Street corridor and portions of North DuPont Highway among the areas experiencing significant impacts.

Cleanup Underway

Cleanup operations continued Friday morning as crews worked to remove debris and inspect damaged infrastructure. Utility workers are also working to restore electricity where service was disrupted.

Residents are being encouraged to avoid unnecessary travel through damaged areas. Debris, damaged structures and downed power lines can remain dangerous even after the tornado has passed.

The National Weather Service is also expected to assess the damage as part of the process of determining the tornado's characteristics and impact.
