Karen Attiah Latest News

Journalist Karen Attiah is back in the spotlight after an independent arbitrator ordered The Washington Post to reinstate her and provide back pay. Attiah had been dismissed by the newspaper in September 2025 following social-media posts that became the subject of a dispute over journalistic expression and workplace discipline.

The ruling announced on August 24, 2026, represents a significant development in the dispute. Reuters reported that the arbitrator ordered the newspaper to reinstate Attiah with back pay. Attiah is an award-winning journalist who spent more than a decade at The Washington Post and became a founding editor of the newspaper's Global Opinions section. Her work has frequently addressed race, gender, international affairs and human rights.

2027 Social Security COLA Forecast

Social Security recipients are also closely following early estimates for the 2027 Social Security cost-of-living adjustment, commonly known as COLA. The official figure will not be finalized until October because the calculation depends on third-quarter inflation data.

Current estimates suggest that the 2027 increase could be around the mid-3% range. AARP's August 2026 analysis projects a 3.5% COLA, while the Senior Citizens League's estimate cited by CBS News is slightly higher at 3.6%. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

The final number is calculated using changes in the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers, or CPI-W, during the third quarter. Because September's inflation data is part of the calculation, estimates can change before the official announcement.

For an average retired worker receiving approximately $2,092 per month, a 3.5% adjustment would represent roughly $73 more per month before considering other changes that could affect a person's net payment. AARP estimates that a 3.5% increase would produce approximately that amount for the average retired worker. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

Beneficiaries should therefore treat current figures as forecasts rather than final numbers. The official 2027 COLA will be announced after the relevant inflation data becomes available in October.
