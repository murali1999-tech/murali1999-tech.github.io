LA Galaxy vs San Diego FC: Live Score, Facts and Figures

LA Galaxy traveled to Snapdragon Stadium to face San Diego FC in a Major League Soccer regular-season match on Saturday, August 29, 2026. The match brought together two Southern California clubs in one of the most interesting regional matchups on the MLS calendar.

San Diego FC entered the match looking to continue a strong run of home form, while LA Galaxy were trying to build momentum after collecting four points from their previous two league matches.
