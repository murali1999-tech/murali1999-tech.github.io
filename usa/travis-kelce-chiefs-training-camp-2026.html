Travis Kelce Chiefs Training Camp 2026: Latest News and Practice Updates

Updated: August 21, 2026
Latest training camp update: The Kansas City Chiefs completed their final full-team training camp practice in St. Joseph, Missouri, on August 20. Travis Kelce was among the veteran players preparing for the upcoming NFL season.

Travis Kelce is back with the Kansas City Chiefs and preparing for his 14th NFL season. The veteran tight end has been one of the central figures at Kansas City's 2026 training camp as the team works toward the regular season.

The Chiefs held their training camp at Missouri Western State University in St. Joseph, Missouri. The camp provided coaches and players with an opportunity to develop chemistry, evaluate younger players and prepare for the regular-season schedule.

Travis Kelce Returns for Another Chiefs Season

Kelce reported to training camp on July 29 and immediately returned to football work. The 36-year-old tight end is entering his 14th season with Kansas City and remains one of quarterback Patrick Mahomes' most familiar targets.

His return attracted considerable attention following a highly publicized offseason. However, once training camp began, Kelce's focus quickly shifted toward football and preparing for another demanding NFL season.

Kelce has described Chiefs training camp as a place where he can focus on football and the team environment. His experience has also made him an important veteran presence for younger players competing for roster positions.

Kelce and Patrick Mahomes Build Chemistry

One of the biggest storylines surrounding Chiefs training camp is the continued connection between Kelce and Patrick Mahomes. The quarterback and tight end have developed one of the NFL's most productive partnerships since Mahomes became Kansas City's starting quarterback.

During a training camp practice on August 11, the Chiefs reported that Mahomes and Kelce connected multiple times during seven-on-seven drills. Their continued chemistry will be important as Kansas City develops its offensive plans for the 2026 season.

Why it matters: Kelce's experience and established connection with Mahomes give the Chiefs an important veteran combination as the team prepares for the regular season.

Chiefs Training Camp Comes to an End

Kansas City completed its final full-team training camp practice on Thursday, August 20, at Missouri Western State University. The Chiefs then shifted their attention toward the next stage of preseason preparation.

The final camp period included several important evaluations as coaches continued to assess the roster. Younger players were competing for opportunities, while veterans such as Kelce and Mahomes continued to prepare for the season ahead.

The Chiefs' official training camp coverage also highlighted observations from practices throughout August, including developments involving the offense, defense and special teams.

Jason Kelce Visits Chiefs Training Camp

Another memorable moment during camp came when Jason Kelce, Travis Kelce's older brother, made a surprise appearance at Chiefs training camp on August 19.

Jason's appearance attracted attention from fans at the Missouri Western State University practice facility. He also interacted with Chiefs personnel and supporters during his visit.

The visit came during one of the final practices of Kansas City's St. Joseph training camp and added another family moment to an already closely followed preseason period for Travis Kelce.

Is This Travis Kelce's Final NFL Season?

Another major storyline surrounding Kelce is speculation about his future. There has been discussion about whether the 2026 season could be his final NFL campaign, although Kelce has not publicly confirmed that he will retire after the season.

For now, the veteran tight end remains focused on football. Training camp has given him the opportunity to work with teammates, build timing with Mahomes and prepare for the physical demands of another NFL season.

Kelce's experience will be particularly valuable as Kansas City develops its younger players. His leadership and understanding of the Chiefs' offensive system could also help the team navigate the long NFL season.

Chiefs Prepare for the 2026 Season

Kansas City enters the 2026 season looking to improve after a difficult 2025 campaign. The Chiefs finished last season with a 6-11 record, making the upcoming season an important opportunity for the franchise.

The team will continue preseason preparations after training camp. Coaches will use preseason games and practices to make roster decisions and establish the roles of players heading into the regular season.

For Kelce, the objective is straightforward: remain healthy, continue developing chemistry with Mahomes and contribute to the Chiefs' offense when the regular season begins.

What to Watch Next

NFL fans will continue to monitor Travis Kelce's practice workload, his connection with Patrick Mahomes and his role in the Chiefs' offensive plans. His performances during preseason action could provide additional clues about how Kansas City intends to use its veteran tight end.

The Chiefs' training camp may be over, but preparation for the regular season is continuing. With Kelce returning for another campaign, his performances will remain one of the most closely watched stories surrounding Kansas City.
