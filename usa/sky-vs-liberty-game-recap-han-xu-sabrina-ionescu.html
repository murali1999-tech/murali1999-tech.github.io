Sky vs Liberty Game Recap: New York Liberty Win 85-66

The New York Liberty defeated the Chicago Sky 85-66 on Saturday, August 29, 2026, at Barclays Center in Brooklyn. The victory gave New York a 24-16 record, while Chicago fell to 15-25. The game was the Liberty's final contest before the WNBA's FIBA World Cup break.

Han Xu was the leading scorer for New York and produced a career-high 19 points. She shot 6-for-8 from the field, made 2 of 3 three-point attempts and went 5-for-5 from the free-throw line. Her 19 points were the highest scoring total of her WNBA career. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

The Liberty controlled the game through their defense and rebounding. Chicago struggled to create consistent offense, while New York maintained its advantage after halftime. The final margin was 19 points.
