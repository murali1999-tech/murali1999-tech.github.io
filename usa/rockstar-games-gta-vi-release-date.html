Rockstar Games GTA VI: Release Date, Story, Platforms and Latest Updates

Grand Theft Auto VI is one of the most anticipated video games from Rockstar Games, and the project is now moving closer to its official launch. Rockstar currently lists Grand Theft Auto VI for release on November 19, 2026. The game is being developed for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with Rockstar's official information confirming those platforms. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

GTA VI returns players to Vice City, the fictional neon-filled location that has been an important part of the Grand Theft Auto series. This time, the wider setting is the state of Leonida, giving Rockstar a much larger environment for the story and exploration. The official game description says that Jason and Lucia find themselves caught in a criminal conspiracy stretching across Leonida after an easy score goes wrong. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Jason Duval and Lucia are the two central characters revealed by Rockstar. Jason grew up around criminals and eventually worked for local drug runners in the Keys. Lucia's background and her relationship with Jason are central to the game's story. Rockstar describes the pair as being forced to rely on each other as they attempt to survive a dangerous situation. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

The world of GTA VI extends beyond the streets of Vice City. Rockstar's official material introduces locations and characters across Leonida, including the Florida Keys-inspired area where Jason has been working. The game is designed around a modern version of the Vice City setting, with beaches, highways, businesses, waterways and rural areas forming part of the larger world.

Rockstar Games released an extended look at Grand Theft Auto VI on August 27, 2026. The extended presentation was captured entirely from in-game footage on PlayStation 5 and gives players a closer look at the game's characters, locations and visual presentation. Rockstar also made the extended look available through its official channels, including YouTube and Netflix. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

The new footage has added to interest surrounding the game's graphics, environments and characters. Rockstar says the extended look showcases the biggest and most immersive evolution of the series. The company has also continued to publish official screenshots, videos and information through the Grand Theft Auto VI website.

GTA VI is currently available for pre-order. Rockstar announced that pre-orders began globally on June 25, 2026. Players can choose between the Standard Edition and Ultimate Edition. The Ultimate Edition includes additional digital content connected with Jason and Lucia's story, along with various vehicles, clothing, weapons and other items. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Players who purchase the game before November 20 can receive the Vintage Vice City Pack at no additional cost while supplies last. The bonus includes the '55 Vapid Stanier Sedan and Garage, outfits and hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern. Digital pre-orders also include one month of GTA+ according to Rockstar's official store information. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

Pre-loading is scheduled to begin on November 12, 2026. Rockstar says physical versions will contain a download code rather than a game disc, allowing players to begin the pre-load process before launch. The game itself is scheduled to become playable on November 19. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

The current official information lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as the launch platforms. Rockstar has not listed a PC version on its current GTA VI platform information page, so players should rely on official Rockstar announcements for any future platform news rather than unofficial release-date claims. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

Interest in GTA VI has remained high because Rockstar has kept much of the game's details under wraps while gradually releasing trailers and information. The first trailer arrived in December 2023, followed by Trailer 2 in May 2025. The latest extended look in August 2026 provides another substantial official preview before the November launch. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

Rockstar Games is continuing to promote GTA VI through its official website and Newswire. The latest announcements include the extended look, pre-order information and details about the game's editions. With the November 19 release date approaching, more official information is expected as Rockstar continues its marketing campaign.
