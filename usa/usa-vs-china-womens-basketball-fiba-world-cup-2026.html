USA vs China Women's Basketball: FIBA Women's World Cup 2026

USA vs China is one of the major matchups on the opening schedule of the FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup 2026 in Berlin, Germany. The United States and China are meeting in Group D, with both teams entering the tournament carrying significant international basketball history. The 2026 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup runs from September 4 through September 13 in Berlin and features 16 national teams.

The USA vs China women's basketball game is part of the first group phase of the competition. Group D includes USA, China, Czechia and Italy. The opening group phase is played in a round-robin format, meaning each team has three games. The group winner advances directly to the quarter-finals, while the teams finishing second and third move into the qualification round for the quarter-finals.

FIBA lists USA vs China as a Group D game at Max-Schmeling-Halle in Berlin. The official FIBA schedule lists the matchup for September 4, 2026, with the game scheduled for 12:15 UTC. Fans following the USA women's basketball team are also watching the tournament closely because the American team is seeking another world title after winning the previous four editions.

The United States has been the dominant nation in FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup history. Before the 2026 tournament, USA had won 11 Women's World Cup titles, including the 2022 edition in Sydney. The American team is also attempting to continue a remarkable unbeaten run in World Cup and Olympic competition. FIBA reported that the USA had not lost in the Women's World Cup or Olympics since a semifinal defeat to Russia at the 2006 World Championship.

China enters the USA vs China matchup with its own strong World Cup record. China finished as runner-up at the 2022 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup after reaching the final against the United States. China also has a long history in the competition, with its best finishes including silver medals in 1994 and 2022 and a bronze medal in 1983.

The recent head-to-head record heavily favors the United States. FIBA's official matchup information lists USA with 15 wins against China and China with no wins in the recorded head-to-head series. At the 2022 Women's World Cup, the two teams met in the group phase and the United States won 77-63. They met again in the championship game, where USA defeated China 83-61 to claim the gold medal.

Those 2022 results add extra interest to the 2026 meeting. China is again facing the reigning champion, while USA begins another World Cup campaign with a roster featuring a mixture of experienced international players and players making their senior World Cup debut.

Chelsea Gray is one of the experienced names associated with Team USA. Gray was part of the American squad that won the 2022 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup and was also part of the USA team that won Olympic gold in Paris in 2024. Her experience and ability to organize an offense make her an important veteran presence for the American team.

Kara Lawson is coaching the USA women's basketball team at the 2026 FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup. Lawson is the head coach at Duke University and previously represented the United States as a player. FIBA notes that Lawson was part of the USA team that won Olympic gold in 2008. Her role in Berlin puts her in charge of a team with substantial international experience and high expectations.

The 2026 USA roster also includes Aliyah Boston, Paige Bueckers, Sonia Citron, Caitlin Clark, Napheesa Collier, Kahleah Copper, Chelsea Gray, Rhyne Howard, Kiki Iriafen, Angel Reese, Breanna Stewart and Jackie Young. Several of these players have already won major international titles, while Caitlin Clark, Paige Bueckers and Angel Reese are among the players attracting major attention around the tournament.

The USA vs China stats are especially interesting because of the teams' previous meetings. The 2022 World Cup group game finished USA 77, China 63, while the final ended USA 83, China 61. Earlier meetings have also produced wins for the United States, including a 100-88 victory in the 2018 Women's World Cup and a 105-62 Olympic group-stage victory in 2016.

The USA's Group D schedule continues after the China game. FIBA lists USA against Italy on September 6 and USA against Czechia on September 7. China also continues its group campaign against Czechia and Italy. The results of all three group games will determine the final positions in Group D and the route each team takes toward the knockout stages.

The wider FIBA Women's World Cup 2026 schedule includes 36 games across 10 days. The quarter-finals are scheduled for September 10, the semi-finals for September 12, and the third-place game and final for September 13. The tournament is being played in Berlin, making Germany the host nation for the twentieth edition of the Women's Basketball World Cup.

For Team USA women's basketball, the objective is clear: continue the championship tradition and compete for another gold medal. For China, the challenge is to compete against the tournament favorite and build on the progress shown by the team that reached the 2022 World Cup final. That history makes USA vs China one of the most closely watched games of the group stage.
