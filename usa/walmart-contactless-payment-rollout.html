Walmart Contactless Payment Rollout Begins at Select Stores

Walmart is finally expanding contactless payment options at its stores in the United States. The retailer began introducing Tap to Pay at select Walmart stores and Sam's Club locations on August 24, giving shoppers another way to pay without inserting a card or using Walmart's traditional QR code payment system. Walmart says the rollout is expected to reach all U.S. stores and clubs by the end of 2026.

The change is significant because Walmart has been one of the largest retailers in the country without widespread support for popular contactless wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay. For years, customers were encouraged to use Walmart Pay through the company's mobile app. That system allowed shoppers to pay by scanning a QR code displayed at the checkout register.

With the new Tap to Pay system, customers can use an eligible contactless credit or debit card, smartphone or smartwatch. Apple Pay and Google Pay are among the payment services being supported. Eligible Walmart, Sam's Club and OnePay cards can also be added to digital wallets for contactless purchases.

Walmart says the main goal is to give customers more choice and make checkout easier. Shoppers who already use a digital wallet on their phone will not have to switch to another payment system simply because they are shopping at Walmart.

The rollout is being introduced in stages rather than arriving at every store at the same time. Walmart has not provided a complete public list of the first stores receiving the feature, so customers may find that Tap to Pay works at one location while another store has not yet received the updated checkout equipment.

The company plans to continue supporting existing payment methods. Cash, regular credit and debit cards and Walmart Pay will remain available. At Sam's Club, members can also continue using Scan & Go, which allows shoppers to scan products with the Walmart app and complete their purchase without using a traditional checkout process.

Fuel stations are on a different schedule. Walmart expects Tap to Pay to reach its fuel stations by the middle of 2027. That means shoppers looking for contactless payment at a Walmart fuel pump may have to wait longer than customers using the checkout lanes inside stores.

The move also reflects how quickly contactless payments have become part of everyday shopping. Many consumers now carry a smartphone or smartwatch that can securely store payment cards. Being able to simply tap a device at a payment terminal can save time, especially during busy shopping periods.

For Walmart customers, the biggest difference is convenience. Instead of opening the Walmart app and scanning a QR code, shoppers with a supported digital wallet can tap their phone or watch at the payment terminal. Customers who prefer physical cards can also use eligible contactless cards.

Walmart's contactless payment rollout is expected to continue throughout the rest of 2026. As more stores receive the technology, the company will move closer to making Tap to Pay a standard checkout option across its U.S. locations. For shoppers who have been asking for Apple Pay, Google Pay and other contactless choices at Walmart, the long wait is finally coming to an end.
