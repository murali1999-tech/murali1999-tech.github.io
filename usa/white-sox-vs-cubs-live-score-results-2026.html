Why White Sox vs Cubs Is Important

The White Sox and Cubs represent Chicago's two Major League Baseball franchises. Their rivalry is commonly known as the Crosstown Classic.

The Cubs play their home games at historic Wrigley Field, while the White Sox play at Rate Field on Chicago's South Side.

Games between the clubs attract attention beyond normal divisional competition because the teams represent different sides of the same major American sports market.
