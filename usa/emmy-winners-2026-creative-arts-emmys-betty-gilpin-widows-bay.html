2026 Emmy Winners: Widow's Bay and Betty Gilpin Lead the Creative Arts Emmys

The 2026 Emmy Awards season has already produced some major winners, with the Creative Arts Emmy Awards taking place over September 5 and September 6 at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles. The ceremonies recognized performers, writers, directors, designers, editors and other television professionals before the main Primetime Emmy ceremony on September 14.

One of the biggest stories from the 2026 Emmy winners list is the success of Apple's Widow's Bay. The horror-comedy series had a particularly strong Creative Arts Emmy weekend and finished the second night with eight awards. The show, starring Matthew Rhys, became one of the early standouts of this year's Emmy race.

Widow's Bay is one of the major winners from the 2026 Creative Arts Emmy Awards.

Betty Gilpin was one of the most talked-about individual winners. Gilpin won Outstanding Guest Actress in a Comedy Series for her performance in Widow's Bay. Her win is a major moment in her career and marks her first Emmy victory after previous nominations.

Gilpin plays Sarah Westcott Warren in Widow's Bay, appearing in a flashback episode connected to the history and folklore of the fictional town. Her performance was recognized during the second night of the Creative Arts Emmys, putting her among the notable acting winners of the 2026 awards season.

Widow's Bay collected awards in several technical and creative categories as well. The show's wins included recognition for production design, sound and other elements that helped create the unusual atmosphere of the series. Its eight victories made it one of the biggest winners heading into the main Emmy ceremony.

The Pitt was another major winner during the Creative Arts ceremonies. The medical drama entered the 2026 Emmy season with 25 nominations, the highest total of any program. It picked up four Creative Arts Emmy wins, giving the series an early boost before the Primetime Emmy awards are presented.

DTF St. Louis also had a strong weekend, collecting six awards. Among its winners were David Harbour and Linda Cardellini. Cardellini won her first Emmy for Outstanding Supporting Actress in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie, while Harbour was recognized in the acting categories for his work on the HBO series.

Another notable result involved Spider-Noir. Although the series had been canceled by Prime Video before the Creative Arts ceremonies, it still received significant recognition and won five Emmys. Its success demonstrates that cancellation does not prevent an already completed television production from receiving awards for its work.

There were also emotional moments during the ceremonies. Rob Reiner received a posthumous Emmy for his guest performance in The Bear. The award was one of the most poignant moments of the weekend and brought attention to his long career in television and film.

Shailene Woodley also won her first Emmy, receiving the Outstanding Guest Actress in a Drama Series award for her performance in Paradise. Ernest Harden Jr. won Outstanding Guest Actor in a Drama Series for The Pitt, giving the medical drama another acting victory.

An Emmy Award statuette. The 2026 ceremonies recognize achievements across television.

Bad Bunny was another major name associated with the 2026 Creative Arts Emmy results. The Apple Music Super Bowl LX Halftime Show earned seven awards, including Outstanding Variety Special (Live). The success gave Bad Bunny his first Emmy and made the halftime show one of the most decorated productions of the weekend.

Mariska Hargitay also had a notable Creative Arts Emmy weekend. She won two awards connected with the HBO documentary My Mom Jayne. Hargitay will also have an important role in the main Emmy ceremony because she is scheduled to host the 78th Primetime Emmy Awards.

For viewers asking when the Emmys are in 2026, the main 78th Primetime Emmy Awards will take place on Monday, September 14, 2026, at the Peacock Theater in downtown Los Angeles. The ceremony will be broadcast live on NBC and will also stream on Peacock. The live broadcast is scheduled for 8 p.m. Eastern Time and 5 p.m. Pacific Time.

The Creative Arts Emmy ceremonies took place earlier, on September 5 and 6. An edited television presentation of the Creative Arts Emmys is scheduled to air on FXX on September 12. This means viewers can see many of the early 2026 Emmy winners before the main Primetime Emmy ceremony takes place.

For viewers following the Emmy race, the Creative Arts results provide an early indication of which programs have momentum. Widow's Bay is particularly interesting because its eight wins put it among the strongest performers before the main ceremony. The Pitt, DTF St. Louis and other highly nominated programs will also be closely watched when the major comedy, drama and limited-series categories are announced.

The main Primetime ceremony will determine winners in some of the most widely followed categories, including Outstanding Comedy Series, Outstanding Drama Series and Outstanding Limited or Anthology Series. Acting awards for lead and supporting performers will also attract major attention from television audiences around the world.

Widow's Bay entered the 2026 Emmy season with 19 nominations, while The Pitt led all programs with 25 nominations. Hacks followed with 24 nominations and Pluribus had 18. Those nomination totals made the competition particularly strong, with several established programs and newer shows competing across major categories.

For Betty Gilpin, however, the Creative Arts Emmy victory already represents a significant personal milestone. Her win for Widow's Bay gives the actress her first Emmy and adds another important achievement to a career that has included television, film and stage work. Her performance has become one of the memorable acting stories from the first part of the 2026 Emmy weekend.
