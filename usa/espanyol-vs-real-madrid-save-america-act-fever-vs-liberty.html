Espanyol vs Real Madrid

Real Madrid began the 2026-27 LaLiga campaign with a dramatic 2-1 victory over Espanyol. The match was Jose Mourinho's first competitive game back as Real Madrid manager after more than 12 years away from the club.

Espanyol 1-2 Real Madrid
LaLiga 2026-27

Real Madrid took the lead in the ninth minute when Jude Bellingham headed in a cross from Arda Guler. Espanyol responded in the 30th minute when Alex Calatrava scored to bring the home side level.

The decisive moment arrived in the 90th minute. Substitute Carlos Espí scored the winning goal after Real Madrid continued to push for a second goal. The late strike gave Mourinho a winning start on his return to the Santiago Bernabeu dugout.

Real Madrid's New Season

The result gives Real Madrid three points from its opening league fixture. The team has significant expectations this season after finishing behind Barcelona in the previous title race. Mourinho's return has added another major storyline to the new campaign.

Jude Bellingham made an immediate contribution with his early goal, while Carlos Espí became the hero after coming off the bench to score the late winner. The match also highlighted areas where Madrid can improve as the season progresses.

Despite the victory, Real Madrid did not completely dominate the match. Espanyol created problems and managed to equalize before the visitors eventually found the decisive goal. The performance therefore provides both encouragement and areas for Mourinho to address.

SAVE America Act

The SAVE America Act is another major topic in U.S. politics. The legislation seeks to establish stricter requirements relating to voter identification and proof of citizenship for federal elections.

The White House has continued to promote the legislation. President Donald Trump recently discussed the SAVE America Act and its election-security goals.

The proposal has become part of a broader political debate over election security, voter identification and access to voting. Supporters argue that stronger identification and citizenship requirements can increase confidence in elections. Critics argue that additional documentation requirements could create barriers for eligible voters.

Why SAVE America Is Trending

The legislation is receiving increased attention as the United States approaches the 2026 midterm elections. The debate involves questions about federal election standards and the role of documentary proof when people register to vote.

Trump has also pointed to India's election system while making the case for stronger voter identification requirements in the United States. Recent comments about India's use of voter identification have become part of the discussion surrounding the SAVE America Act.

The legislation remains politically controversial, with supporters and opponents offering sharply different views about its potential effect on election security and voting access.

Fever vs Liberty

The Indiana Fever and New York Liberty have developed into one of the most closely followed matchups in the WNBA, helped by the popularity of Caitlin Clark and the Liberty's championship ambitions.

In their latest meeting on August 22, the New York Liberty defeated the Indiana Fever 109-102 at Barclays Center. The result was important for New York's position in the playoff race.

New York Liberty 109-102 Indiana Fever
WNBA — August 22, 2026

The Fever entered the game with a 24-13 record but could not complete a late comeback. Indiana managed to tie the game with 1:45 remaining, but New York held its composure and finished the contest with a victory.

Caitlin Clark and Indiana Fever

Caitlin Clark remains one of the biggest attractions in the WNBA. Along with Aliyah Boston and Kelsey Mitchell, she helped Indiana remain competitive against New York. The Fever's strong offensive stretches kept the game close until the final minutes.

New York, however, produced the stronger finish. The Liberty's victory was particularly significant because Indiana had already defeated New York earlier in the season. The August 22 game therefore provided an important response from the Liberty.

Fever's Next Game

After playing New York on Saturday, Indiana is scheduled to continue its road trip against the Chicago Sky on Sunday evening. The Fever's schedule lists the game for 7 p.m. ET.

The back-to-back schedule presents another challenge for Indiana. Managing player minutes and maintaining energy will be important as the team continues its push toward the postseason.
