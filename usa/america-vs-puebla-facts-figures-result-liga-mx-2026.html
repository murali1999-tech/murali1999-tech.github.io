América vs Puebla: Facts, Figures, Result and Liga MX Standings

América defeated Puebla 2-0 in the Liga MX Apertura 2026 on Saturday, August 29, 2026. The match was played at Estadio Banorte in Mexico City, with América continuing its unbeaten start to the tournament. The result gave the Águilas another three points and kept them at the top of the early Liga MX standings.
