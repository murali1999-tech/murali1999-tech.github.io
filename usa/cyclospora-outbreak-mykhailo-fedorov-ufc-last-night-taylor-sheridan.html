Cyclospora Outbreak 2026: What We Know

A major multistate outbreak of Cyclospora infections is currently being investigated by U.S. public health authorities. The U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention says that thousands of laboratory-confirmed cases have been reported since May 1, 2026.

The investigation has focused on iceberg lettuce. CDC, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and state and local health authorities have been investigating the outbreak, with recalled iceberg lettuce sourced from central Mexico identified as the implicated food.

According to the CDC's latest surveillance information, 13,895 laboratory-confirmed cases of cyclosporiasis acquired in the United States had been reported since May 1, although the agency notes that the true number of infections is likely higher because not every infection is diagnosed or reported.

Important outbreak update:
Taylor Farms initiated a recall on July 17, 2026, involving iceberg lettuce sourced from central Mexico. CDC says the best-by dates for the recalled products have passed and the recalled lettuce should no longer be available in stores or restaurants.

Cyclospora is a microscopic parasite known scientifically as Cyclospora cayetanensis. It causes an intestinal infection called cyclosporiasis. The infection is usually associated with contaminated food or water and can cause prolonged gastrointestinal symptoms.

Symptoms of Cyclospora Infection

The most common symptom is watery diarrhea. Other symptoms can include loss of appetite, weight loss, stomach cramps, bloating, increased gas, nausea and fatigue. Symptoms commonly begin about a week after infection, although the incubation period can vary.

The CDC advises people experiencing symptoms consistent with cyclosporiasis to contact a healthcare provider. Testing and treatment decisions should be made by a medical professional rather than based solely on information from an online news article.

Health reminder: If you believe you consumed recalled food and subsequently develop persistent diarrhea or other concerning symptoms, contact a healthcare professional. Do not self-diagnose an infection based only on an outbreak report.

Readers should also remember that a food recall does not mean every item of the same food is contaminated. The safest approach is to follow current instructions from the FDA, CDC and local health authorities concerning recalled products.

Mykhailo Fedorov: Why He Is Trending

Mykhailo Fedorov has become a major topic in Ukrainian political news following his removal as Ukraine's defence minister during a government reshuffle in July 2026.

Fedorov had served as defence minister for approximately six months. Before taking the defence portfolio, he was widely associated with Ukraine's digital transformation efforts and technology-focused government initiatives. His appointment to the defence ministry was seen as part of an effort to bring technology and modernization into military administration.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy dismissed Fedorov in July as part of a wider government restructuring. Fedorov subsequently confirmed his departure and said that serving the Ukrainian people had been a great honour.

His departure attracted considerable attention because Fedorov had developed a reputation for technology-driven approaches, including efforts related to digital systems and military innovation. Supporters argued that his managerial and technological background made him an important figure in Ukraine's wartime modernization efforts.

Why Fedorov matters:
His dismissal has been discussed not only as a cabinet change but also as part of a broader debate over Ukraine's military leadership, technology strategy, mobilization reform and political decision-making during wartime.

In late July, Fedorov discussed plans for a mobilisation overhaul that had been expected to be presented and launched during August. He said the reforms were intended to improve staffing levels in the Ukrainian armed forces.

The political reaction has continued beyond parliament and government circles. Reports in early August described demonstrations in Kyiv calling for Fedorov's return. The controversy also became linked to wider disagreements concerning military command and the direction of Ukraine's defence policy.

UFC Last Night: UFC 330 Results

UFC fans are looking back at UFC 330, which took place on Saturday, August 15, 2026, at Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia.

The headline fight featured UFC welterweight champion Islam Makhachev against No. 1 contender Ian Machado Garry. Makhachev successfully defended his welterweight championship by unanimous decision after five rounds.

Makhachev's victory extended his remarkable winning run and reinforced his position among the UFC's elite fighters. Garry showed resilience throughout the contest, but Makhachev's wrestling, grappling and positional control proved decisive over the five-round championship fight.

Mackenzie Dern Also Retains Her Title

The UFC 330 co-main event featured women's strawweight champion Mackenzie Dern against Gillian Robertson. Dern successfully defended her championship with a unanimous decision.

The card also featured several notable finishes. Esteban Ribovics defeated veteran Edson Barboza by second-round stoppage, while Jalin Turner produced one of the night's quickest finishes with a 39-second knockout of Kaue Fernandes.

Other fighters who earned attention included Neil Magny, who stopped Ramiz Brahimaj in the second round, and Charles Johnson, who submitted Eduardo Chapolin with a rare Scottish Twister during the preliminary card.
