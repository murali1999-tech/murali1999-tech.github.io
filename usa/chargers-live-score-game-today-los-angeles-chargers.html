Los Angeles Chargers

The Los Angeles Chargers compete in the National Football League as a member of the AFC West. The franchise has a long history in professional football and has developed a strong following in Southern California and across the United States.

The Chargers play their home games at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. The stadium is also home to the Los Angeles Rams and is one of the NFL's most prominent modern venues.

Chargers games attract attention because of the team's offensive talent, quarterback play and competition within the AFC West.
