Mortgage refinance rates in the U.S. are currently staying in the mid-6% range, with daily fluctuations depending on the lender, loan type, credit score, and market conditions.

Current average refinance rates include:

  • 30-year fixed refinance: around 6.7% to 6.8%
  • 15-year fixed refinance: around 6.0% to 6.3%
  • 5/1 ARM refinance: around 6.0%

Freddie Mac reported the national average 30-year mortgage rate at 6.37% for the latest weekly survey, while the 15-year average stood near 5.72%.

Recent refinance trends show rates moving slightly higher again after briefly dropping close to 6% earlier in 2026. Analysts say inflation concerns and global economic uncertainty are keeping mortgage rates elevated.

Recent refinance averages from major financial trackers:

  • Bankrate 30-year refinance APR: about 6.82%
  • Bankrate 15-year refinance APR: about 6.28%
  • Conventional 30-year refinance: around 6.45%
  • FHA refinance loans: near 5.88%

Experts currently expect mortgage refinance rates to remain above 6% for much of 2026, although forecasts suggest gradual declines may happen later in the year if inflation slows.
