Multiple People Shot at Virginia State University

Multiple people were shot early Saturday morning, August 15, 2026, near the Quad Annexes at Virginia State University (VSU) in Ettrick, Virginia.

Virginia State University said the shooting involved multiple suspects. The university campus was placed under lockdown while law enforcement investigated the incident.

The shooting was reported at approximately 1:30 a.m. near the 3300 block of Boisseau Street, according to Chesterfield County police.

What Happened at Virginia State University?

Police responded to a report of a shooting at about 1:30 a.m. Saturday near the university's Quad Annexes in the area of Boisseau Street and Third Avenue.

When officers arrived, they found several people with gunshot wounds. According to the latest available local reporting, five people were injured and transported to area hospitals.

One victim was initially reported to have life-threatening injuries and was later described as being in critical condition. The other four victims were reported to have non-life-threatening injuries.

The university and Chesterfield County authorities have continued their investigation. A significant law-enforcement presence remained on campus as investigators processed the scene.

Why the Campus Was Locked Down

A campus lockdown is used by universities and law enforcement when there is a potential threat to people inside or near campus. During a lockdown, students and staff may be instructed to remain inside secure locations and avoid specific areas.

The lockdown at Virginia State University followed the early-morning shooting near the Quad Annexes. The university instructed members of the campus community to avoid the affected area and follow law-enforcement instructions.

Virginia State University Community

The shooting occurred shortly after Virginia State University held a welcome event for approximately 1,500 incoming freshmen. Former NFL MVP Cam Newton was among the featured guests at the event.

Classes were scheduled to begin Monday, making the incident particularly significant for students returning to campus for the new academic year.
