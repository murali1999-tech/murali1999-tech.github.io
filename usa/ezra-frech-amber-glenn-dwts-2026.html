Ezra Frech and Amber Glenn Join Dancing With the Stars 2026

Updated September 2, 2026

Ezra Frech and Amber Glenn are two of the biggest sporting names joining Dancing with the Stars for Season 35 in 2026. The Paralympic champion and Olympic figure skating champion were officially revealed as part of the new DWTS cast during the Good Morning America announcement on September 2.

Frech will compete with professional dancer Daniella Karagach, while Glenn will be paired with professional dancer Pasha Pashkov. Their inclusion gives the new season two elite athletes with very different sporting backgrounds and plenty of experience performing under pressure.

Ezra Frech enters the ballroom as a two-time Paralympic gold medalist. The American track and field athlete competes in the T63 classification and has established himself as one of the most recognizable young athletes in the Paralympic movement.

At the Paris 2024 Paralympic Games, Frech won gold in both the men's 100-meter T63 and high jump T63. He won the 100-meter title on September 2, 2024, before winning the high jump the following day with a Paralympic record of 1.94 meters.

Frech's sporting career began at a young age. He competed at the Tokyo 2020 Paralympic Games and reached the finals in both the high jump and long jump. He returned to the Paralympic stage in Paris and produced the breakthrough performances that made him a two-time champion.

His success extends beyond the track. Frech has worked as an advocate for adaptive sports and is a co-founder of Angel City Sports, an organization that provides sports opportunities for people with disabilities. He has also used his public platform to encourage young athletes with disabilities to participate in sport.

Now Frech is moving from athletics to ballroom dancing. That change will require an entirely different kind of training. Track and field relies heavily on explosive movement and technical precision, while DWTS competitors have to learn choreography, rhythm, ballroom technique and performance skills.

Amber Glenn brings another major sporting résumé to Season 35. The American figure skater is an Olympic gold medalist and three-time U.S. national champion. She has also competed successfully at the international level and is known for her powerful skating style.

Glenn helped Team USA win Olympic gold in the figure skating team event at the 2026 Winter Olympics. She also became the first U.S. woman to land three triple Axels at a single Olympic Games, adding another major accomplishment to her career.

Her transition to Dancing with the Stars could be especially interesting because figure skating and ballroom dancing share some important characteristics. Both require balance, body control, musical timing, choreography and the ability to perform in front of a large audience.

However, the two sports are still very different. Glenn will have to learn ballroom and Latin dance technique while performing without ice skates. She will also have to adjust to the judging system used on DWTS, where judges evaluate technique, performance and choreography.

Glenn will compete alongside professional dancer Pasha Pashkov. Pashkov has become a familiar face to DWTS viewers and will be responsible for creating routines that allow Glenn to use her athletic ability while helping her develop the specific skills required for ballroom competition.

Frech will work with Daniella Karagach, another established professional dancer on the show. Karagach has extensive DWTS experience and has previously won the Mirrorball Trophy. Her experience could be valuable as Frech learns the fundamentals of competitive ballroom dancing.

The inclusion of Frech and Glenn is part of a particularly diverse Season 35 cast. The new lineup includes actors, television personalities, musicians, reality stars, social media personalities, a celebrity chef and athletes.

Other major names announced for the season include Jenna Dewan, Julia Stiles, Harry Shum Jr., Tatyana Ali, Tyler Cameron, Giada De Laurentiis, Taylor Hanson, Maura Higgins, Ciara Miller, Guillermo Rodriguez, Jackson Olson, Sarah Jane Nader, Conner Leavitt and Connor Wood.

Jenna Dewan will compete with Val Chmerkovskiy, while Julia Stiles will dance with Ezra Sosa. Harry Shum Jr. will be partnered with Jenna Johnson. Tatyana Ali will compete with Jan Ravnik, and Taylor Hanson will dance with Britt Stewart.

Tyler Cameron will compete with Sharna Burgess, who is returning to the DWTS ballroom. Giada De Laurentiis will dance with Alan Bersten, while Maura Higgins will compete with Mark Ballas.

The four contestants announced before the full Good Morning America reveal are also part of the Season 35 lineup. Ciara Miller will dance with Brandon Armstrong, Jackson Olson will compete with Emma Slater, Guillermo Rodriguez will be paired with Witney Carson and Maura Higgins will dance with Mark Ballas.

For Frech and Glenn, the opportunity comes at an interesting point in their careers. Both athletes have already competed on some of the biggest sporting stages in the world. The DWTS ballroom will test them in a completely different way.

Frech's background could give him an advantage in areas such as explosive movement, body awareness and competitive focus. At the same time, he will have to adapt choreography to his individual movement style and learn how to communicate through dance.

Glenn's figure skating background could also prove useful. Years of skating have required her to develop balance, flexibility, musical interpretation and precise choreography. The challenge will be transferring those skills to a floor without the assistance of blades and ice.

Both athletes also understand the pressure of major competitions. Frech has competed at multiple Paralympic Games, while Glenn recently competed at the 2026 Winter Olympics. That experience could help them handle live television, judges' scores and audience reactions.

The Season 35 cast announcement took place on Good Morning America on September 2, giving fans the complete list of celebrities and professional pairings. The announcement ended weeks of speculation about which stars would join the new season.

Dancing with the Stars Season 35 is scheduled to premiere with a two-night event on Tuesday, September 15, and Wednesday, September 16, at 8 p.m. Eastern Time on ABC and Disney+. Episodes will also stream on Hulu the following day.

The season will be hosted by Alfonso Ribeiro and Julianne Hough. Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli and Derek Hough return as judges. The combination of returning professionals, new professionals and a large group of celebrity contestants gives the new season a different mix of personalities and performance styles.

Frech and Glenn will have to wait until the premiere to make their first competitive performances. Their professional partners will be helping them prepare routines during rehearsals, and viewers will get their first opportunity to see how quickly the two athletes can adapt to ballroom dancing.

For Ezra Frech, Dancing with the Stars is another opportunity to introduce a wider television audience to adaptive sport. His Paralympic achievements have already made him an important figure in athletics, and his DWTS appearance could bring his story to people who may not have followed the Paralympic Games.

For Amber Glenn, the show represents a new challenge immediately after a historic Olympic season. She has already demonstrated her ability to perform under enormous pressure on the ice, but the ballroom will require a different form of precision and creativity.

The pairing of two Olympic and Paralympic champions also gives Season 35 a strong sports storyline from the beginning. Fans will be able to compare how Frech and Glenn approach dance training and whether either athlete can turn their competitive experience into high scores from the DWTS judges.

As the September 15 premiere approaches, attention will remain focused on the first routines, scores and reactions from the judges. Ezra Frech and Amber Glenn will be among the contestants most closely watched because both arrive with major international sporting achievements but limited experience in competitive ballroom dancing.

For now, their DWTS pairings are confirmed: Ezra Frech with Daniella Karagach and Amber Glenn with Pasha Pashkov. Their next challenge is to take years of athletic training and translate that discipline into the ballroom.
