Matthew Mayich: What Happened to the Arizona State Hockey Player?

Matthew Mayich, a 21-year-old hockey defenseman and St. Louis Blues prospect, remains hospitalized after collapsing during an outdoor workout with the Arizona State University hockey team on August 20, 2026. Mayich was taking part in an offseason workout in Tempe, Arizona, when he collapsed. The incident happened on the first day that NCAA hockey teams were permitted to begin formal activities for the new season. Emergency responders were called after reports of a medical emergency, and Mayich was taken to a hospital for treatment.

According to reports, paramedics initially described the situation as a heat-related illness. Mayich was later transferred to the Barrow Neurological Institute in Phoenix, where he remains under medical care.

Other reports quoted the attorney as saying that there is effectively no chance of recovery. The available credible reports therefore indicate that Mayich is alive and receiving medical treatment.
