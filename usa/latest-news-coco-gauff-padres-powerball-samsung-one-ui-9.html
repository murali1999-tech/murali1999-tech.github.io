Lunar Eclipses

Lunar eclipses are among the most visually interesting astronomical events. They occur when Earth moves between the Sun and Moon and Earth's shadow falls across the lunar surface.

A total lunar eclipse can turn the Moon a reddish or copper color. This is sometimes called a "blood moon." The color occurs because sunlight passing through Earth's atmosphere is filtered before reaching the Moon.

Unlike solar eclipses, lunar eclipses can generally be viewed safely with the unaided eye. Visibility depends on the location of the observer and the timing of the event.
