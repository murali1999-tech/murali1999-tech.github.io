Colorado vs Georgia Tech: CU Buffs Win 14-13 as Micah Welch and Julian Lewis Lead Comeback

Colorado Buffaloes football opened the 2026 season with a dramatic 14-13 victory over Georgia Tech at Bobby Dodd Stadium in Atlanta. The game was decided in the final seconds after Colorado scored a late touchdown and Georgia Tech had a field-goal attempt blocked as time expired.

The result gave Colorado a 1-0 start to the season and handed Georgia Tech a 0-1 record. The matchup was highly anticipated because it was a rematch of the 2025 season opener, when Georgia Tech defeated Colorado 27-20 in Boulder.

Colorado came into the game with a new-look coaching staff and roster. Deion Sanders entered his fourth season as Colorado's head coach, while Brennan Marion began his role as offensive coordinator and Chris Marve took over as defensive coordinator.

The game was played at Bobby Dodd Stadium at Hyundai Field in Atlanta. It was scheduled for 8 p.m. Eastern Time and was broadcast nationally on ESPN. Colorado's official schedule listed the game as the opening contest of the 2026 season.

Colorado quarterback Julian Lewis completed 14 of 27 passes for 124 yards and one touchdown. He did not throw an interception and made the biggest offensive play of the game when Colorado needed a score late in the fourth quarter.

With less than a minute remaining, Lewis connected with Charlie Williams for a 20-yard touchdown. The touchdown brought Colorado level at 13-13, and the extra point put the Buffaloes ahead 14-13 with only seconds remaining.

Georgia Tech immediately moved into position to answer. Alberto Mendoza connected with Dalen Penson for a 42-yard completion that moved the Yellow Jackets deep into Colorado territory. But the Buffaloes defense and special teams produced one final decisive play.

Georgia Tech attempted a 45-yard field goal with three seconds remaining. The kick was blocked by Colorado's B.J. Carter, ending the game and preserving the 14-13 victory.

Micah Welch was one of Colorado's important offensive contributors. He carried the ball 13 times for 58 yards and scored Colorado's first touchdown of the game on a five-yard rushing play during the second quarter.

Damian Henderson II also provided production on the ground with 65 yards on 12 carries. JaQuail Smith added 42 rushing yards on 10 attempts. Colorado finished with 307 total yards and ran 76 offensive plays.

Colorado's passing attack was not especially productive statistically, but the offense remained patient and eventually found the decisive touchdown. DeAndre Moore Jr. caught four passes for 37 yards, while Charlie Williams finished with two receptions for 28 yards and the game-tying touchdown.

DeKalon Taylor added two receptions for 24 yards. Colorado's offense relied heavily on the running game, finishing with 49 rushing attempts compared with 27 passing attempts.

Georgia Tech quarterback Alberto Mendoza had a strong statistical passing performance despite the loss. He completed 17 of 23 attempts for 237 yards and did not throw an interception.

Justice Haynes was Georgia Tech's leading rusher. He carried 19 times for 64 yards, averaging 3.37 yards per carry. He also had an important role during Georgia Tech's final possession as the Yellow Jackets tried to move into field-goal range.

Jordan Allen was Georgia Tech's leading receiver with eight catches for 113 yards. Dalen Penson added three receptions for 73 yards, including the crucial 42-yard reception during the final drive.

Georgia Tech finished the game with 288 total yards compared with Colorado's 307. The Yellow Jackets were more efficient on a per-play basis, averaging 5.3 yards per play compared with Colorado's four yards per play.
