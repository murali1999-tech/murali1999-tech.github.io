NAM vs SA: Namibia vs South Africa, Gerhard Erasmus and South Africa National Cricket Team Details

Published: August 28, 2026 | Cricket News

NAM vs SA is the big cricket fixture attracting attention on August 28, 2026, as Namibia take on South Africa in the opening match of the Namibia T20I Tri-Series. The match is being played at the Namibia Cricket Ground in Windhoek. Zimbabwe are the third team in the competition, with the three teams scheduled to play each other twice before the final.

The Namibia vs South Africa match is particularly interesting because both teams are preparing for a busy period of international cricket. Namibia are led by experienced all-rounder Gerhard Erasmus, while South Africa have named a young side for the Namibia tour under captain Bjorn Fortuin. The T20I tri-series runs from August 28 to September 6, with the final scheduled for September 6.

The experienced all-rounder has been a central figure in Namibia's rise in international cricket and has captained the country in major tournaments. The previous completed T20I between Namibia and South Africa produced a memorable result. Namibia defeated South Africa by four wickets in Windhoek in October 2025. Ruben Trumpelmann played an important role with the ball in that match, while Namibia's batting chased down the target successfully.

That previous result means the Namibia vs South Africa rivalry has already produced a major upset. Although South Africa have a much deeper cricketing system and are traditionally one of the strongest international teams, Namibia's home advantage and experienced core make the contest interesting.
