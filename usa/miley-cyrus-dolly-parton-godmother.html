Is Dolly Parton Miley Cyrus's Godmother? Their Special Relationship Explained

Yes. Dolly Parton is Miley Cyrus's godmother, and the relationship between the two singers goes back to before Miley became a television and music superstar. Their connection has lasted for decades and has become one of the most recognizable relationships between two generations of entertainers.

Although Dolly Parton and Miley Cyrus are known as godmother and goddaughter, they are also connected through the Cyrus family's long friendship with Parton. Dolly knew Miley's father, Billy Ray Cyrus, before Miley was born, and that friendship eventually became a close family connection.

How Did Dolly Parton Become Miley Cyrus's Godmother?

Dolly Parton has explained that she became Miley Cyrus's godmother because of her friendship with Billy Ray Cyrus. The two entertainers became friends around the time Billy Ray was building his country music career in the early 1990s.

When Miley was born in 1992, Billy Ray asked Dolly to become her godmother. Parton has recalled the story in interviews and has described herself as Miley's “fairy godmother.”

Parton later said she had known Miley since she was a baby. Their relationship was therefore not something that developed after Miley became famous. Dolly was part of her life long before the world knew her as Hannah Montana.

Is Dolly Parton Related to Miley Cyrus?

Dolly Parton and Miley Cyrus are not traditionally considered close relatives, but their family connection is unusually strong. The two have described each other as family for years because of their godmother-goddaughter relationship.

There is also a distant biological connection between the two. In 2024, reporting revealed that Parton and Cyrus are seventh cousins once removed. That means they share a distant ancestor, although the family connection is much more remote than their famous godmother relationship.

For most fans, however, the important connection has always been Dolly's role as Miley's godmother and mentor.

Dolly Parton and Billy Ray Cyrus

The friendship between Dolly and Billy Ray played a major role in bringing the two families together. Billy Ray became well known after the success of his song Achy Breaky Heart, and he and Parton developed a friendship through the country music world.

Dolly has spoken warmly about the Cyrus family over the years. Her friendship with Billy Ray gave her an opportunity to watch Miley grow up and eventually enter the entertainment business herself.

Parton has said that she was proud of Miley and wanted to support her rather than tell her how to live her life.

Dolly Parton on Miley Cyrus

Dolly has frequently praised Miley's talent. She has described her goddaughter as a talented singer and songwriter and has defended her when the younger star faced criticism during different stages of her career.

Parton has also acknowledged that Miley is strong-minded. Rather than trying to control her career, Dolly has generally presented herself as someone Miley could turn to for support and advice.

The relationship became especially important when Miley was transitioning from a child television star into an adult recording artist. Dolly understood that Miley needed to establish her own identity rather than remain associated with her Hannah Montana character forever.

Dolly Parton on Hannah Montana

One of the most memorable examples of their relationship came through Disney Channel's Hannah Montana.

Dolly Parton appeared on the series as Aunt Dolly. Although the character was called “Aunt Dolly,” she was actually Miley Stewart's godmother within the show's story, closely reflecting the real-life relationship between Parton and Miley Cyrus.

Parton appeared in several episodes of the series. Her appearances gave fans a chance to see the real-life godmother and goddaughter together on screen.

The connection was particularly entertaining because Dolly's larger-than-life personality fit naturally into the world of Hannah Montana.

Why Did Dolly Parton Play Aunt Dolly?

Miley Cyrus was reportedly instrumental in bringing Dolly Parton onto Hannah Montana. Because Parton was already an important person in Miley's life, having her appear on the show made sense both personally and creatively.

The role allowed Parton to connect with a younger audience while giving fans a fictional version of the relationship they already knew about in real life.

Parton's appearances became some of the most remembered celebrity guest appearances from the series.

Miley Cyrus and Dolly Parton Music

The two singers have also performed together numerous times. One of their best-known collaborations is Jolene, Dolly's classic song that Miley has performed with her godmother on several occasions.

Their performances show the contrast between Parton's traditional country style and Miley's modern pop and rock influences.

Miley has repeatedly demonstrated how much Parton's music influenced her. At the same time, Dolly has praised Miley's voice and songwriting ability.

Rainbowland and Other Collaborations

Dolly and Miley have worked together on several musical projects. Parton appeared on Miley's music and television projects, while Miley has participated in projects connected to Dolly.

The two have also shared the stage at major events. Their collaborations have become popular because they combine two generations of artists who have a genuine personal connection.

The relationship has therefore never been simply a publicity partnership. Dolly has been involved in Miley's life since childhood.

Dolly Parton's Influence on Miley

Dolly's influence on Miley extends beyond music. Miley has often spoken about Dolly with affection and respect, describing her as an important role model.

Parton's approach to fame has also provided an example for Miley. Dolly built an enormous entertainment career while maintaining her sense of humor and her connection to her roots.

For Miley, having someone with that experience available for advice has been valuable throughout her career.

Are Miley Cyrus and Dolly Parton Really Close?

Yes. Their public history shows a relationship that has lasted for more than three decades. Dolly knew Miley as a child, supported her career and appeared alongside her in television and music projects.

Miley has also consistently spoken warmly about Dolly and has celebrated her work. Their relationship continued even as Miley developed her own independent career.

That makes their connection different from many celebrity friendships. They have a long personal history that began before either of them became part of the relationship as entertainers.

Dolly Parton's Legacy With Miley Cyrus

Dolly Parton's influence on Miley Cyrus is likely to remain one of the most interesting parts of both singers' stories. Dolly helped introduce Miley to an older generation of music fans, while Miley helped bring Dolly's personality and music to younger audiences through Hannah Montana and their collaborations.

The two artists represented different generations but shared a strong appreciation for music and performance.

For fans searching “is Dolly Parton Miley Cyrus's godmother,” the answer is straightforward: yes. Dolly has personally described herself as Miley's godmother and has referred to herself as her fairy godmother.

Their relationship began with Dolly's friendship with Billy Ray Cyrus and grew into a lifelong bond. From childhood memories to Hannah Montana, live performances and musical collaborations, Dolly Parton has remained an important figure in Miley Cyrus's life.
