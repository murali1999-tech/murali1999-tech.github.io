How Boxing Results Are Decided

A boxing contest can end in several ways. A knockout occurs when a fighter is unable to continue within the referee's count. A technical knockout can be declared when the referee, doctor or corner determines that continuing would be unsafe.

If both fighters complete the scheduled rounds, judges score the contest. Depending on the scorecards, the result can be a unanimous decision, split decision or majority decision. Draws are also possible.
