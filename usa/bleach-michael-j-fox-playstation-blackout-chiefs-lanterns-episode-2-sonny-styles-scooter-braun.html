Allen Iverson's Michael Wright Tribute

NBA legend Allen Iverson has paid tribute to actor Michael Wright following Wright's death at age 70. Wright was best known for his role as Eddie King Jr. in the 1991 musical drama The Five Heartbeats.

Iverson's tribute attracted attention because Wright's work had a lasting cultural impact and because the former NBA star publicly expressed his admiration for the actor. The tribute has helped bring renewed attention to Wright's career and his memorable performances.

The Five Heartbeats remains particularly popular among audiences who remember its story of a fictional vocal group and its rise through the music industry. Wright's performance as Eddie King Jr. became one of the most recognizable parts of the film.

Following Wright's death, fans and entertainment figures have shared memories of his work. Iverson's message was among the tributes that connected the entertainment and sports communities.

Michael J. Fox

Michael J. Fox is entering another significant chapter of his career after the Television Academy announced that he will receive the Bob Hope Humanitarian Award at the 78th Emmy Awards.

The award recognizes Fox's long-running humanitarian work and his advocacy for Parkinson's research. The honor is scheduled to be presented during the Emmy Awards on September 14, 2026.

Fox has had a remarkable entertainment career, becoming internationally famous through Family Ties, the Back to the Future trilogy and Spin City. He has also received multiple Emmy Awards and other major honors.

His advocacy became a central part of his public work after he disclosed his Parkinson's diagnosis in 1998. In 2000, he founded The Michael J. Fox Foundation for Parkinson's Research, which has since supported billions of dollars in research programs.

Fox has also received an Emmy nomination this year for his guest appearance in Shrinking, adding another career milestone to an already extraordinary year.

PlayStation Blackout

A player-led PlayStation blackout campaign is scheduled to begin on August 23, 2026. The campaign is being organized by players and game-preservation advocates who want to protest Sony's planned move away from physical game discs.

The #PSBlackout campaign calls on PlayStation users to stop playing or logging into their consoles for a week. Reports say the campaign is connected to concerns over Sony's reported plan to stop producing discs for new games in 2028.

Supporters argue that physical media provides players with greater ownership and helps preserve games for future generations. The debate has become part of a larger discussion about the industry's transition toward digital distribution.

PlayStation blackout: The player-organized campaign begins August 23 and is planned as a week-long protest over concerns about the future of physical games and game preservation.
