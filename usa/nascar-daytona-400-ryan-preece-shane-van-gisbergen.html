Ryan Preece Wins Daytona 400: NASCAR Results Today, Standings and Shane Van Gisbergen

Ryan Preece captured his first NASCAR Cup Series victory at Daytona International Speedway, winning the regular-season finale and securing the final playoff position in a dramatic finish. The Daytona 400 was decided after a late caution during overtime, giving Preece one of the biggest wins of his NASCAR career and changing the final shape of the Cup Series playoff field.

Preece entered the Daytona race needing a strong performance to move into the postseason. He was outside the playoff field before the race and faced a significant points deficit to Shane van Gisbergen. Because Daytona is a superspeedway where a single late incident can completely change the standings, Preece still had a path to the playoffs, but he needed to capitalize on his opportunity.

That opportunity arrived during the closing laps. Preece drove the No. 60 RFK Racing Ford toward the front and was in position when the final caution appeared. The caution froze the running order and gave Preece the race win. The victory was the first Cup Series win of his career and came in his 249th career Cup Series start.

For fans asking who won the NASCAR race today, the answer is Ryan Preece. His victory at Daytona was particularly important because it was not simply a race win. It also moved him into the 2026 NASCAR Cup Series playoffs. Preece became the final driver to earn a spot in the 16-driver championship field.

Daniel Suarez finished second, followed by Michael McDowell, Tyler Reddick and Ricky Stenhouse Jr. The Daytona 400 produced a competitive final stage, with several drivers moving through the field during the overtime restart. The race was scheduled for 160 laps but continued beyond the scheduled distance because of the overtime finish.

Shane van Gisbergen entered Daytona holding the final playoff position. He had a points advantage over Preece before the race, but the late-race result changed everything. Van Gisbergen was caught in the closing incident and finished outside the position he needed to remain in the postseason.

Van Gisbergen's elimination was one of the most significant developments in the NASCAR results today. The Trackhouse Racing driver had already won two races during the regular season, but NASCAR's playoff format combines race victories with the regular-season points picture. His Daytona result left him outside the 16-driver playoff field.

The late incident also involved Zane Smith, who had been running near the front during the final restart. Smith attempted to protect his position as the field raced toward the checkered flag, but contact sent cars spinning and brought out the caution. The incident prevented several drivers from completing the final lap at racing speed.

Ryan Preece's victory gives RFK Racing another major moment at Daytona. The team has developed a strong superspeedway presence, and Preece was able to take advantage of the draft and positioning during the closing stages. The result also provides him with momentum heading into the most important part of the season.

The NASCAR standings entering the playoffs are led by drivers who performed consistently throughout the regular season and accumulated victories. Denny Hamlin secured the No. 1 seed before the Daytona finale, while Ryan Blaney was also among the leading drivers. The playoff field includes a mixture of established Cup Series champions, race winners and drivers competing for their first championship.

The 2026 NASCAR Cup Series playoffs begin at Darlington Raceway. The postseason consists of 10 races and progressively eliminates drivers as the championship battle moves through the Round of 16, Round of 12 and Round of 8 before the Championship 4 finale.

For Preece, the objective now changes completely. Instead of simply trying to qualify for the playoffs, he will attempt to use the momentum of his Daytona victory to advance through the championship rounds. A driver who performs well at superspeedways can gain confidence from a victory like this, but the playoff tracks will require strong performance across different racing styles.

Shane van Gisbergen will now have to focus on the remaining opportunities outside the championship fight. His road-course ability has made him an important driver in the Cup Series, and his two regular-season victories demonstrate the speed of the Trackhouse Racing program. However, the Daytona finale ended his hopes of competing for the 2026 Cup Series championship.

The Daytona 400 also demonstrated why the final race of the regular season is one of the most closely followed events on the NASCAR calendar. The combination of a superspeedway, tight drafting and a playoff cutoff creates enormous pressure for drivers near the points bubble.

Fans following NASCAR results, NASCAR standings and NASCAR points standings will now turn their attention to the playoff opener. Every position becomes important during the postseason, and stage points, race finishes and victories can dramatically change the championship picture.

Ryan Preece's breakthrough victory will remain one of the defining stories of the 2026 regular season. After years of competing for his first Cup Series win, he finally reached Victory Lane at one of NASCAR's most famous tracks. More importantly, he did it on the exact night he needed a breakthrough to keep his championship hopes alive.

The result makes the opening playoff race even more interesting. Preece enters the postseason with confidence, while the drivers already near the top of the standings will try to protect their advantage. The NASCAR Cup Series now moves from regular-season survival to championship racing, with every race carrying much greater importance.
