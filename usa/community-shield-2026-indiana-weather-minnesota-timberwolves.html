Indiana Weather: Severe Storms and Widespread Flooding

Indiana is experiencing a serious weather emergency as heavy rain and severe storms have produced widespread flooding across the state. At least five people have died, while authorities have carried out numerous rescues as rivers and streams rose rapidly.

The flooding is particularly significant because some areas have received extremely heavy rainfall over a short period. Reports indicate that parts of Indiana received approximately 11 inches of rain in two days, causing rivers including the White River to reach exceptionally high levels.

Indiana weather safety:

Flash flooding can become dangerous very quickly. People in affected areas should follow evacuation orders, avoid driving through flooded roads and rely on current instructions from local emergency officials and the National Weather Service.

Flooding Across Central Indiana

Communities from Hamilton County toward northern Indianapolis have experienced significant flooding. Indianapolis residents have faced rising water levels, while emergency crews have conducted dozens of rescues. More than 350 evacuations were reported in Delaware County alone.

The flooding has damaged homes, roads and other infrastructure. Emergency officials have described the event as unprecedented in some affected communities. Flash-flood warnings have continued in parts of central Indiana as authorities monitor river levels and additional rainfall.

The weather situation is especially dangerous because flooding can continue even after rainfall decreases. Water moving downstream can cause rivers to rise in areas that did not receive the heaviest rainfall themselves.

Indiana Weather Forecast and Safety

Residents should monitor location-specific forecasts because conditions can vary considerably from one part of Indiana to another. The most important information during an active flood event comes from the National Weather Service, county emergency-management agencies and local authorities.

People should never attempt to walk or drive through moving floodwater. Even relatively shallow water can move a vehicle or hide damaged roads and debris. Anyone who receives an evacuation warning should leave the affected area using the route provided by local officials.

Minnesota Timberwolves Latest News

The Minnesota Timberwolves are preparing for the next NBA season while also celebrating one of the most important players in franchise history: Kevin Garnett.

The Timberwolves have announced that Garnett's No. 21 jersey will be retired on February 28, 2027. The ceremony is scheduled around Minnesota's game against the Boston Celtics, another team with which Garnett has a historic connection.

Kevin Garnett jersey retirement:

Player: Kevin Garnett
Number: 21
Team: Minnesota Timberwolves
Ceremony: February 28, 2027
Opponent: Boston Celtics

Garnett was drafted by Minnesota in 1995 directly out of high school. He quickly became the centerpiece of the franchise and developed into one of the greatest players in Timberwolves history.

During his Minnesota career, Garnett became the franchise's all-time leader in several major statistical categories, including points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks and games played. He also won the NBA Most Valuable Player award during the 2003-04 season.

Kevin Garnett's Minnesota Legacy

Garnett's greatest period with Minnesota came during the early 2000s. He led the Timberwolves to eight consecutive playoff appearances and became the face of the organization.

Although Garnett later won an NBA championship with Boston, his connection with Minnesota remains central to his legacy. The Timberwolves' decision to retire No. 21 recognizes his enormous contribution to the franchise.

The retirement ceremony will make Garnett only the second Timberwolves player to have his number retired, following Malik Sealy.

Anthony Edwards and the Timberwolves

Current Timberwolves superstar Anthony Edwards also continues to attract attention during the NBA offseason. Edwards recently returned to the Philippines, where he met fans during the team's "Believe That" activities. His growing international profile reflects his importance to Minnesota's current generation.

The Timberwolves are entering another important phase as they look to remain competitive in the Western Conference. The development of Edwards and the team's broader roster will be central to their ambitions for the upcoming season.
