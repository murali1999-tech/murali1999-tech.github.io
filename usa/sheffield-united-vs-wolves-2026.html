Sheffield United vs Wolves: Match Preview, Form, Table and Latest Details

Read more updates

Sheffield United and Wolverhampton Wanderers meet at Bramall Lane in a Championship fixture on Sunday, September 13, 2026. The match brings together two clubs looking to build momentum in the early part of the Championship season. Sheffield United come into the game after a 2-1 away victory against Blackburn Rovers, while Wolves drew 2-2 away against Birmingham City in their most recent league match.

The fixture is scheduled for a 12:00pm kick-off at Bramall Lane in Sheffield. The match is an important opportunity for both sides because the Championship table is still tightly packed after the opening rounds. Sheffield United entered the weekend in 10th place with nine points from six league matches, while Wolves were 11th with eight points from five league games.

Sheffield United have made a mixed but competitive start to their Championship campaign. Their recent 2-1 win over Blackburn Rovers gave the team three valuable points on the road. Before that result, the Blades lost 3-1 at home to Norwich City, defeated Bolton Wanderers 3-2 and drew 2-2 with Cardiff City. Their results show a side capable of scoring goals but also capable of conceding when games become open.

Wolves have also experienced an eventful opening to the season. They defeated Preston North End 3-1 away and Stoke City 4-1 at home, while they also recorded a Carabao Cup victory against Sheffield Wednesday. However, a 4-2 defeat against West Ham United and a 2-2 draw with Birmingham City have left the team looking for greater consistency.

The head-to-head record adds another interesting element to the match. In their most recent meetings, Wolves have generally had the better results. Wolves won 1-0 against Sheffield United in February 2024, while Sheffield United won the previous Premier League meeting at Bramall Lane 2-1 in November 2023. Wolves also won the FA Cup meeting in January 2022 and recorded a 1-0 Premier League victory in April 2021.

The current Championship standings make the fixture particularly interesting. After six matches, Sheffield United had two wins, three draws and one defeat for nine points. Wolves had two wins, two draws and one defeat from five league games for eight points. The difference is small, meaning a win could move either club several places depending on other results.

For Sheffield United, home advantage could be important. Bramall Lane is expected to provide a strong atmosphere, with the club's supporters hoping the team can continue the momentum from the away victory over Blackburn. The Blades will need to stay organised because Wolves have shown that they can produce attacking football and score several goals in a match.

Wolves arrive in Sheffield for their third successive away game. The club has confirmed that around 2,800 visiting supporters are expected to attend the fixture. Wolves manager Cesar Peixoto has also received some positive squad news, with Kieran Trippier returning to full training and Jean-Ricner Bellegarde back in contention after recovering from injury. New signing Bertrand Traore could also feature.

The match is expected to be competitive because neither side has created a large gap from the other in the early Championship table. Sheffield United have the advantage of playing at home, while Wolves have recent head-to-head success and attacking confidence from victories over Stoke City and Preston North End.

For viewers in the United Kingdom, Wolves have confirmed that the match will be broadcast live on Sky Sports, with coverage beginning on Sky Sports Football at 11:30am. Wolves supporters in selected international territories may also have access to video streaming through Wolves TV. Matchday coverage and updates are also available through the clubs' official channels.

The weather around South Yorkshire is expected to be cloudy with a small chance of rain, with temperatures around 18 degrees Celsius at kick-off. Supporters travelling to Bramall Lane have also been advised to allow extra time because the Sheffield Hallam University moving-in weekend is expected to make roads around the city centre busier than usual.

Sheffield United vs Wolves therefore arrives as a closely balanced Championship contest. Both clubs have shown positive moments during the opening weeks, but both have also dropped points. A victory at Bramall Lane would give either side an important boost and could significantly improve its position in the early league table.
