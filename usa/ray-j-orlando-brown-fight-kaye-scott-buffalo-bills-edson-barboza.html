Edson Barboza at UFC 330

Edson Barboza was one of the major names on the UFC 330 fight card on August 15, 2026. The veteran Brazilian lightweight faced Esteban Ribovics at Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia.

Barboza entered the contest as one of the UFC's most experienced and recognizable strikers. He became particularly famous for his devastating kicks and highlight-reel finishes throughout his long MMA career.

At UFC 330, Ribovics stopped Barboza by second-round TKO at 1:32. After the fight, Barboza became emotional and placed his gloves in the center of the Octagon, strongly signaling the end of his career.

Reports subsequently described the 40-year-old Brazilian as retiring from professional mixed martial arts following the defeat.

Edson Barboza's UFC Legacy

Barboza made his UFC debut in 2010 and became known as one of the most dangerous kickers in the organization. His spinning wheel kick knockout of Terry Etim remains one of the most memorable finishes in UFC history.

He competed against numerous elite fighters during his career, including Khabib Nurmagomedov, Tony Ferguson, Justin Gaethje, Anthony Pettis and other top-level lightweight contenders.

Although Barboza never won a UFC championship, his striking ability, longevity and willingness to compete against elite opposition established him as a fan favorite.

At UFC 330, Ribovics paid tribute to Barboza after the victory, describing him as an inspiration and recognizing the significance of defeating such an experienced opponent.

UFC 330 and the Wider Fight Card

UFC 330 featured a major welterweight championship main event between Islam Makhachev and Ian Machado Garry. The card also included Mackenzie Dern vs Gillian Robertson and several other important matchups.

Barboza's fight therefore formed part of a major UFC event rather than an isolated contest. His retirement announcement became one of the biggest stories from the card because of his long career and reputation as an exciting striker.
