What Makes UFC Events Popular?

UFC fighters commonly have backgrounds in wrestling, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, boxing, kickboxing, Muay Thai, judo and other combat sports. Modern MMA requires athletes to combine several disciplines, making preparation highly demanding.

A typical UFC fight can involve striking exchanges, takedowns, wrestling, ground control and submission attempts. Fighters must therefore develop both offensive and defensive skills in multiple areas.

The organization has also expanded its international presence substantially. UFC events are staged in different countries and broadcast to audiences around the world.
