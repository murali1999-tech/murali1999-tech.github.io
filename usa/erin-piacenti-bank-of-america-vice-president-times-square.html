Erin Piacenti, Bank of America Vice President, Killed in Times Square Attack

Times Square, New York City. The area was the scene of a fatal stabbing on August 31, 2026.

Erin Piacenti, a vice president at Bank of America, was fatally stabbed in Times Square in New York City on Monday, August 31, 2026. The incident happened in one of Manhattan's busiest areas and involved two victims before police confronted the woman suspected of carrying out the attacks.

According to reports from Reuters and other news organizations, Piacenti was a vice president responsible for business selection and conflicts at Bank of America. She died at a hospital after suffering stabbing injuries. Bank of America confirmed the loss of its colleague and expressed condolences to Piacenti's family and loved ones.

Piacenti was 32 years old and worked in New York City. Public professional information identifies her as a Bank of America vice president in the business selection and conflicts area. Her professional background also included legal work before she joined the bank.

The attack took place in Times Square during the afternoon of August 31. Police said a woman carrying multiple knives attacked two people in the area. The first victim was a 68-year-old man who was stabbed in the abdomen. Piacenti was then attacked nearby.

Emergency responders transported the victims to a hospital. Piacenti later died from her injuries. Police said the other victim was in stable condition.

The suspect was identified by police as Pamela Cisneros, 49. Police officers confronted Cisneros after the attacks. Authorities said officers attempted to stop her using a Taser and repeatedly ordered her to drop the knives.

According to police statements reported by news organizations, Cisneros continued moving toward officers while holding knives. Officers then fired their weapons. Cisneros was taken to Bellevue Hospital, where she was pronounced dead.

New York Police Commissioner Jessica Tisch described the attack as random and unprovoked. Authorities said there was no indication that the incident was an act of terrorism. The NYPD Force Investigation Division is investigating the police shooting.

The death of Piacenti has received widespread attention because of her position at Bank of America and because the attack happened in Times Square, a heavily visited part of New York City. The location normally has large numbers of tourists, commuters, workers and residents moving through the area.

Bank of America released a statement expressing shock and sadness over Piacenti's death. The company described her as a valued teammate who would be greatly missed and extended its sympathy to her family and loved ones.

Professional information available about Piacenti shows a background combining law and business. Reports say she graduated from the University of Pennsylvania in 2016 and later earned a law degree from Fordham University School of Law in 2021.

Before joining Bank of America, Piacenti worked as an associate at the law firm Davis Polk & Wardwell. Her professional experience also included work connected with legal and compliance functions at Morgan Stanley. She subsequently joined Bank of America, where she held the position of vice president of business selection and conflicts.

The role of business selection and conflicts is part of the legal, compliance and risk-management functions within a large financial institution. Such work can involve reviewing business activities, identifying potential conflicts and helping ensure that business decisions follow applicable policies and regulations.

Reports also said Piacenti had recently celebrated her second wedding anniversary. She had been married for two years before her death, according to information reported by news outlets.

The investigation into the Times Square incident remains ongoing. Authorities are examining the circumstances surrounding the two stabbings, the suspect's actions and the police response. Officials have not indicated that Piacenti knew the attacker.

The incident has also renewed attention on safety in crowded public places. Times Square is among New York City's most recognizable locations and normally has a significant police presence because of the large number of people passing through it each day.

For Bank of America, Piacenti's death represents the loss of a young professional who worked in a specialized corporate and legal function. The company's statement made clear that colleagues were deeply affected by the tragedy.
