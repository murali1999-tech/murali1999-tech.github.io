Tacko Fall Returns to NBA as 76ers Add 7-Foot-6 Center for Training Camp

Updated September 2, 2026

Tacko Fall is back in the NBA picture after the Philadelphia 76ers announced that they have added the 7-foot-6 center to their training camp roster. The move is one of the latest developments around the Sixers as they prepare for the 2026-27 NBA season.

The 76ers announced on September 1 that they had signed Tacko Fall, Jameer Nelson Jr. and Saint Thomas. The three players are expected to compete for opportunities during training camp. Reports indicate that the deals are Exhibit 10 contracts, which are generally non-guaranteed agreements used to bring players into training camp and can provide a path toward a two-way contract or the team's G League affiliate.

Fall immediately became the biggest name among the three additions because of his unusual height and previous NBA experience. Listed at 7 feet 6 inches, or 2.29 meters, Fall is one of the tallest players to have appeared in the NBA. He is a native of Senegal and played college basketball at UCF before beginning his professional career.

Fall previously played for the Boston Celtics and Cleveland Cavaliers. According to NBA career statistics, he appeared in 37 regular-season games across three seasons. He averaged 2.2 points and 2.4 rebounds in about 6.1 minutes per game during his NBA career.

His last NBA appearance came with Cleveland during the 2021-22 season. Since then, Fall has continued his professional career outside the NBA, including time playing in international leagues. His return to an NBA training camp gives him another opportunity to compete for a place in the league.

Tacko Fall during the 2026 NBA All-Star Celebrity Game.

The timing of the signing has attracted attention because Philadelphia is looking for additional depth in the frontcourt. Joel Embiid remains the team's established center, while the roster also includes younger frontcourt players. Bringing Fall into training camp gives the coaching staff another large-bodied option to evaluate.

Fall's size has always been one of the most recognizable parts of his basketball career. At 7-foot-6 and around 311 pounds, he provides a physical presence that is difficult to replicate. His height can be useful around the basket, particularly for rebounding, contesting shots and finishing close to the rim.

At the same time, a training camp invitation does not mean that Fall has already secured a regular-season roster position. An Exhibit 10 contract is not the same as a guaranteed NBA contract. Fall will have to compete during camp and preseason opportunities if he wants to earn a more permanent role with Philadelphia.

Jameer Nelson Jr. is another important part of the latest Sixers move. The 25-year-old guard is the son of former NBA player Jameer Nelson, who is now Philadelphia's executive vice president of basketball operations. The elder Nelson played 14 seasons in the NBA and was a prominent point guard during his playing career.

Jameer Nelson Jr. has not yet appeared in an NBA regular-season game, but he has built professional experience in the G League and overseas. During the 2025-26 season with the Stockton Kings, he averaged 19.9 points, 3.7 assists and 3.2 rebounds per game, according to reports on the signing.

Nelson Jr. also has previous training camp experience. He spent time with the San Antonio Spurs organization before joining Sacramento's system. His latest opportunity with Philadelphia gives him another chance to compete for a professional roster position.

The Sixers also added Saint Thomas. The 6-foot-7 forward spent the previous season with the Delaware Blue Coats, Philadelphia's G League affiliate. He averaged 12.5 points, 5.0 rebounds and 3.7 assists in 36 games for the Blue Coats.

Philadelphia's decision to bring in three players provides the organization with additional options before training camp begins. The team can evaluate players based on their performances, physical condition, defensive ability, shooting and overall fit with the roster.

For Tacko Fall, however, the story is particularly notable because it represents a return to the NBA after several seasons away. His enormous size made him a fan favorite during his earlier NBA appearances, and his name has remained popular among basketball fans even while he played internationally.

Fall also appeared in the 2026 NBA All-Star Celebrity Game, giving fans an opportunity to see him back on an NBA court. His appearance generated renewed interest in his career and was followed by the latest opportunity with Philadelphia.

The 76ers are entering the new season with significant expectations. The organization made major moves during the offseason, including adding LeBron James and trading for Jaylen Brown. The team is therefore expected to compete at a high level, making the training camp competition important for players trying to earn opportunities around the main roster.

For now, the most accurate description is that Tacko Fall has joined the 76ers for training camp rather than that he has guaranteed a place on the regular-season NBA roster. The coming weeks should provide a clearer picture of how Philadelphia plans to use him and whether he can turn the opportunity into a longer-term position.

Basketball fans searching for Tacko Fall NBA news may also see his name written incorrectly as Taco Fall. The correct spelling is Tacko Fall. His height, previous NBA experience and return to the 76ers have made him one of the more unusual stories of the current NBA offseason.

Jameer Nelson Jr. will also be closely watched during training camp. His scoring production in the G League gives him a legitimate opportunity to show that he can contribute at a higher level. His connection to the Philadelphia organization through his father is also likely to attract additional attention, although his on-court performance will ultimately determine his chances.

The latest 76ers additions show how NBA teams continue to use training camp to evaluate experienced players, young prospects and potential G League contributors. For Fall, it is another chance to continue a professional career that has already taken him from Senegal to college basketball, the NBA and international leagues.

As the 2026-27 NBA season approaches, the main question is whether Tacko Fall can turn his training camp opportunity into a regular role. His height gives him a unique physical advantage, while his previous NBA experience means he already understands the demands of professional basketball. The Sixers will have the opportunity to evaluate him before making their final roster decisions.
