What Is the Pima County Sheriff's Department?

The Pima County Sheriff's Department is the primary sheriff's law-enforcement agency serving Pima County, Arizona. The department provides a wide range of public-safety services, including patrol operations, investigations, corrections, court security and community policing.

Pima County includes the Tucson metropolitan area as well as surrounding communities and rural areas. Because of the county's size and diverse geography, the Sheriff's Department handles a broad range of calls for service and public-safety responsibilities.

The department also works with other law-enforcement agencies and public-safety organizations when incidents cross jurisdictional boundaries or require additional resources.

Understanding Pima County Calls for Service

A call for service does not necessarily mean that a crime occurred. Police and sheriff's departments receive calls for many different reasons, including traffic concerns, welfare checks, suspicious activity, accidents, disputes and requests for assistance.
