Are Post Malone and Christy Lee Engaged?

Who Is Christy Lee?

When Did Post Malone and Christy Lee Start Dating?

Post Malone and Christy Lee were first publicly linked in early 2025. They were reportedly spotted together in Rome in January, and later appearances and social media footage increased speculation that they were romantically involved.

By March 2025, photographs and videos showed the pair displaying affection in public. Entertainment reports subsequently identified Lee as Malone's girlfriend.

Post Malone and Christy Lee Breakup

The relationship did not appear to continue uninterrupted. In June 2025, TMZ reported that Malone and Lee had broken up after dating for several months. The outlet said sources with direct knowledge indicated that the couple had ended their relationship shortly before the report was published.

The reason for the reported breakup was not publicly disclosed. For much of the following period, fans had limited information about whether Malone and Lee remained in contact or whether they had resumed their relationship.

Did Post Malone and Christy Lee Get Back Together?

Later appearances reportedly suggested that the relationship had been rekindled. The pair were photographed together at the Khaite fashion show in February 2026, adding to speculation that they had resumed their romance.

The reported engagement now provides a much stronger indication that the two have reconciled. Still, because the couple is known for keeping personal matters private, details about when they officially got back together and when the proposal occurred remain limited.

The Engagement Ring

Where Did Post Malone Propose?

Reports indicate that the proposal took place in Utah around August 18, 2026. Friends reportedly traveled to join the couple for a celebration, with photographs showing the couple wearing bride-to-be and groom-to-be accessories.

The exact location and circumstances of the proposal have not been extensively detailed by Malone or Lee. More information could emerge if either of them chooses to discuss the engagement publicly.

Post Malone's Previous Engagement

Before his relationship with Christy Lee, Post Malone was engaged to Hee Sung “Jamie” Park, the mother of his daughter. Malone had previously discussed his engagement and fatherhood publicly, while details about the relationship remained largely private.

Post Malone and Christy Lee Wedding Plans
