Missouri vs Kansas: Mizzou Beats KU 38-21 in 2026 Border War

Missouri football came away with another important victory over Kansas football on Friday night, beating the Jayhawks 38-21 in the latest edition of the Border War. The No. 23 Missouri Tigers used a strong second half to pull away from Kansas after the two teams were tied 14-14 early in the second half.

The Missouri vs Kansas game was played at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium in Lawrence, giving the Jayhawks home-field advantage. It was the first time Missouri had traveled to Lawrence for the rivalry since 2005, adding another layer of history to an already intense college football matchup.

Austin Simmons was one of the biggest stars of the Mizzou vs KU game. The Missouri quarterback threw for 292 yards and three touchdowns, helping the Tigers move the ball effectively against the Kansas defense. Simmons had already impressed in his Missouri debut against Arkansas-Pine Bluff, and his performance against Kansas showed why expectations around the transfer quarterback are so high.

Simmons transferred to Missouri from Ole Miss and quickly became the starting quarterback for head coach Eli Drinkwitz. In Missouri's opening game of the season, he threw four touchdown passes in a 54-14 victory. Against Kansas, Simmons again delivered when the Tigers needed him most.

Missouri entered the Kansas game after a dominant opening-week performance, while Kansas had also started the season strongly. The Jayhawks defeated LIU 51-6 in their first game, creating confidence around the program before the rivalry matchup.

Kansas quarterback Isaiah Marshall had an active night but faced increasing pressure as the game progressed. Marshall finished with 168 passing yards, two touchdown passes and two interceptions. His fumble early in the second half also became one of the turning points of the game.

Robert Woodyard Jr. produced two crucial turnovers for Missouri in the second half. He recovered Marshall's fumble and later intercepted a pass, giving the Tigers excellent field position and helping Missouri turn defensive plays into points.

Those turnovers changed the momentum. The game was tied 14-14, but Missouri quickly turned the defensive takeaways into touchdowns. Simmons connected with Donovan Olugbode for a go-ahead score, and the Tigers continued to build their advantage during the second half.

Jamal Roberts also played a major role for Missouri. The running back rushed for 125 yards and scored a touchdown. His performance helped Missouri maintain balance and prevented Kansas from focusing completely on Simmons and the passing game.

Donovan Olugbode and Cayden Lee both finished with more than 100 receiving yards and each caught a touchdown pass. Their performances gave Simmons reliable targets and helped Missouri consistently create explosive plays.

The final Missouri score of 38 points continued a strong offensive start for the Tigers. Missouri scored 54 points in its opening game and followed that performance with 38 against a much stronger Kansas opponent.

For Kansas, the 21-point performance showed flashes of offensive potential but also exposed areas that need improvement. The Jayhawks moved the ball at times, but turnovers and second-half defensive problems allowed Missouri to take control.

Head coach Eli Drinkwitz has now guided Missouri through another successful Border War victory. The Tigers have won five straight games against Kansas, extending their recent dominance in one of college football's oldest rivalries.

The Missouri-Kansas rivalry has a history that stretches back more than a century. The football series began in 1891 and is connected to the wider historical rivalry between the two states. The matchup is commonly known as the Border War or Border Showdown.

The rivalry became less frequent after Missouri left the Big 12 for the SEC in 2012. The return of the football series has generated considerable attention because fans from both schools have strong memories of the rivalry and its history.

Missouri won the previous meeting 42-31 in 2025. The 2026 matchup gave Kansas an opportunity to stop Missouri's recent winning streak, but the Tigers once again finished on top.

The game also had significance for Missouri because of the team's upcoming SEC schedule. After beating Kansas, the Tigers will return home to face Troy before beginning a demanding conference schedule that includes Mississippi State, Florida, Texas A&M, Ole Miss, Arkansas, Texas, Georgia, Kentucky and Oklahoma.

For Kansas, the loss drops the Jayhawks to 1-1. The team will now turn its attention to Arizona State, with the coaching staff looking to correct the mistakes from the Missouri game.

One of the biggest questions surrounding Missouri entering the game was the running back position. Ahmad Hardy was unavailable after suffering a gunshot wound in May, leaving other players to take on more responsibility. Roberts responded with an important performance against Kansas.

The Tigers also had to overcome mistakes of their own. Missouri had special-teams problems and committed penalties, but the defense created enough big plays to overcome those issues. Drinkwitz said after the game that Missouri had to overcome its mistakes while playing on the road.

The atmosphere in Lawrence was another major part of the night. Kansas fans had been waiting for Missouri to return to David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium, and the rivalry created an intense environment from the opening kickoff.

The result will be particularly disappointing for Kansas because the Jayhawks had started the season with a convincing 51-6 victory. The team showed promise in Week 1, but Missouri provided a much stronger test and exposed some weaknesses.

For Missouri football, the victory provides an early statement. The Tigers are now 2-0 and have already defeated two different opponents with very different styles. Simmons has looked comfortable at quarterback, while the receiving group has shown the ability to make big plays.

Missouri's defense may ultimately be the most important part of the team's season. The ability of players such as Robert Woodyard Jr. to create turnovers could become especially valuable once the Tigers enter the SEC portion of their schedule.

The Mizzou vs Kansas game also produced plenty of discussion among college football fans about the future of both programs. Missouri has the advantage of playing in the SEC, while Kansas continues to build its program in the Big 12. The rivalry remains important despite the teams playing in different conferences.
