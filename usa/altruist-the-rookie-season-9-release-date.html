What Does Altruist Mean?

An altruist is a person who acts out of concern for the well-being of other people. Altruism generally refers to behavior motivated by helping others rather than gaining a personal benefit.

The word is often used to describe people who donate money, volunteer their time, help strangers or make sacrifices for someone else. An altruistic person may choose to help another person even when there is no obvious reward.

Altruism is an important concept in psychology, philosophy and everyday life. People can demonstrate altruistic behavior in many different ways, from small acts of kindness to major charitable contributions.

Altruist Meaning in Everyday Life

In everyday conversation, calling someone an altruist is generally a compliment. It suggests that the person cares about others and is willing to help without expecting something in return.

A teacher who spends extra time helping a student, a person who volunteers at a food bank or someone who regularly supports a charitable organization could all be described as altruistic.

The term can also be used when discussing characters in movies and television shows. A character who repeatedly risks their own safety to protect others might be described as an altruist.

What Is The Rookie?

The Rookie is an American police procedural drama created by Alexi Hawley. The series premiered on ABC in 2018 and stars Nathan Fillion as John Nolan.

Nolan is unusual compared with most rookie police officers because he enters the profession later in life. After a major personal event, he decides to pursue a career with the LAPD.

The show follows Nolan and his fellow officers as they deal with criminal investigations, emergencies and the pressure of police work. At the same time, the series spends considerable time on their friendships, romances and family lives.
