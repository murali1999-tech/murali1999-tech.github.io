Diego Pavia is a former Vanderbilt quarterback who became one of the most recognizable players in college football. After a strong 2025 season, Pavia finished as the Heisman Trophy runner-up and entered the 2026 NFL Draft.

Diego Pavia at Vanderbilt

Pavia played his final two college seasons at Vanderbilt after previously playing at New Mexico State. In his final Vanderbilt season, he threw for 3,539 yards and 29 touchdowns while completing 70.6 percent of his passes. He also rushed for 862 yards and 10 touchdowns.

His performance helped Vanderbilt reach its first 10-win season and made Pavia a major name in college football. Vanderbilt lists him as a 2025-26 graduate quarterback on its official athletics website.

Diego Pavia and the 2026 NFL Draft

Pavia went undrafted in the 2026 NFL Draft despite his impressive college production. He later agreed to join the Baltimore Ravens as an undrafted free-agent quarterback.

The Ravens gave Pavia an opportunity to compete for a place on the quarterback depth chart behind Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley. His mobility and experience made him an interesting player to watch during the offseason.

Latest Diego Pavia News

The Baltimore Ravens waived Pavia on July 23, 2026, shortly before the start of training camp. The move came as Baltimore adjusted its 90-man roster and narrowed its competition for the third quarterback position.

Pavia's next opportunity in professional football remains a major point of interest for fans who followed his Vanderbilt career. His college production and experience could give him another chance to compete for an NFL opportunity.
