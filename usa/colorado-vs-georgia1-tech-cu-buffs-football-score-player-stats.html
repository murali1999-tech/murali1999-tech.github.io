Colorado vs Georgia Tech: Julian Lewis Leads Buffaloes to 14-13 Win

Colorado vs Georgia Tech turned into one of the most dramatic college football games of the night on September 3, 2026. The Colorado Buffaloes defeated the Georgia Tech Yellow Jackets 14-13 at Bobby Dodd Stadium in Atlanta after a late touchdown from freshman quarterback Julian Lewis and a game-ending blocked field goal by Boo Carter.

Georgia Tech appeared to have control of the game late in the fourth quarter. The Yellow Jackets led 13-7 with less than two minutes remaining, but Colorado stayed alive and eventually produced the decisive scoring drive. Julian Lewis found Charlie Williams for a 20-yard touchdown with 42 seconds remaining, giving the Buffaloes a 14-13 advantage after Daniel Gerlach's extra point.

The ending was just as dramatic for Georgia Tech. Quarterback Alberto Mendoza moved the Yellow Jackets into Colorado territory with a 42-yard completion to Dalen Penson. Georgia Tech reached the Colorado 27-yard line and called on Aidan Birr for a 45-yard field goal attempt with only three seconds remaining. Boo Carter got through and blocked the kick, allowing Colorado to escape with the victory.

Julian Lewis had a difficult night for much of the game, but his performance became much more important when the game was on the line. The Colorado quarterback completed 14 of 27 passes for 124 yards and one touchdown without an interception. His biggest completion was the 20-yard touchdown to Charlie Williams that changed the outcome.

Lewis also benefited from a Colorado rushing attack that helped keep the Buffaloes competitive. Micah Welch rushed for 58 yards on 13 carries and scored Colorado's first touchdown on a 5-yard run in the second quarter. Damian Henderson II was another important contributor, finishing with 65 rushing yards on 12 carries.

DeAndre Moore Jr. led Colorado in receiving with four catches for 37 yards. Charlie Williams had two receptions for 28 yards, including the game-winning touchdown. DeKalon Taylor added two catches for 24 yards.

Colorado's defense also produced the play that ultimately decided the game. Boo Carter's blocked field goal came at the perfect time. With Georgia Tech already in range for a potential winning kick, Carter's pressure prevented Aidan Birr from giving the Yellow Jackets the victory.

Georgia Tech quarterback Alberto Mendoza had strong numbers despite the loss. He completed 17 of 23 passes for 237 yards and did not throw an interception. His 42-yard completion to Dalen Penson during the final drive appeared to put Georgia Tech in position to win, but the Yellow Jackets could not finish the possession.

Justice Haynes was Georgia Tech's leading rusher. He carried the ball 19 times for 64 yards. Jordan Allen was the leading receiver, catching eight passes for 113 yards. Dalen Penson finished with three receptions for 73 yards, including the crucial 42-yard play during the final Georgia Tech drive.

Georgia Tech also got a spectacular special teams play earlier in the game. Rahkeem Smith returned a kickoff 100 yards for a touchdown in the second quarter. It was his first touch as a Yellow Jacket and gave Georgia Tech an immediate response after Colorado had taken a 7-0 lead.

The first quarter was scoreless. Both teams had early opportunities but failed to convert them into touchdowns. Georgia Tech's Aidan Birr missed a 35-yard field goal, while Colorado was stopped on fourth down at the Georgia Tech 1-yard line.

Colorado finally opened the scoring when Micah Welch rushed five yards for a touchdown early in the second quarter. Georgia Tech answered immediately with Rahkeem Smith's 100-yard kickoff return. Birr then added a 22-yard field goal to put Georgia Tech ahead 10-7 at halftime.

Neither team scored in the third quarter. Colorado later had another opportunity to tie the game, but Georgia Tech's Shymeik Jones blocked Daniel Gerlach's 38-yard field goal attempt with 7:35 remaining in the fourth quarter.

Georgia Tech then moved down the field and extended its advantage to 13-7 when Aidan Birr made a 29-yard field goal with 1:43 remaining. At that point, the Yellow Jackets were extremely close to securing a season-opening win against Deion Sanders and the Colorado Buffaloes.

Instead, Colorado produced its biggest drive of the night. Lewis stayed composed and moved the Buffaloes down the field before connecting with Williams for the decisive touchdown. The play came with 42 seconds left, giving Georgia Tech just enough time for one final possession.

The final statistics showed how close the game was. Colorado finished with 307 total yards compared with 288 for Georgia Tech. Colorado had 76 offensive plays while Georgia Tech ran 54. The Buffaloes also controlled the ball for 34 minutes and nine seconds, while Georgia Tech had possession for 25 minutes and 51 seconds.

Colorado finished the game without losing a turnover. Georgia Tech had one lost fumble. The Yellow Jackets had three fumbles overall, while Colorado had two fumbles but did not lose either one.

For Deion Sanders, the victory gives Colorado a 1-0 start to the 2026 season. It was another memorable late-game finish for the Buffaloes under their head coach. The result also gives Julian Lewis an important first victory as Colorado's quarterback.

The offense showed promise through Mendoza, Allen, Penson and Justice Haynes, but the team could not close the game.
