Katie Ledecky Latest News

American swimming superstar Katie Ledecky is making headlines at the 2026 Pan Pacific Swimming Championships in Irvine, California. Ledecky won the women's 1500m freestyle earlier in the competition, continuing her remarkable record in distance swimming.

However, August 16 brought a major surprise. Australia's Lani Pallister defeated Ledecky in the women's 800m freestyle final. Pallister finished in 8:06.10, while Ledecky took silver in 8:07.26.

The result was particularly significant because Ledecky has dominated the 800m freestyle internationally for many years. Pallister's victory represents one of the most notable challenges to Ledecky's long-standing position in the event.

Ledecky had also been beaten by Pallister in the 400m freestyle during the Pan Pacific Championships. The Australian swimmer's performances have therefore become one of the biggest swimming stories of the weekend.
