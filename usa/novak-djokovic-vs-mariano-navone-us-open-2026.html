Novak Djokovic vs Mariano Navone: US Open 2026 Result

Novak Djokovic is out of the 2026 US Open after a stunning first-round defeat to Argentina's Mariano Navone. Navone won a dramatic five-set match at Arthur Ashe Stadium on Sunday, August 30, ending Djokovic's latest attempt to win a record 25th Grand Slam singles title.

The final score was Mariano Navone 7-6(5), 5-7, 4-6, 6-2, 6-1 Novak Djokovic. The match lasted four hours and 36 minutes and became one of the biggest surprises of the opening round in New York.

So, did Djokovic win? No. Mariano Navone won the match and advanced to the second round of the 2026 US Open. Djokovic was eliminated from the men's singles draw after one of the longest and most difficult first-round matches of his Grand Slam career.

The match started with Navone taking the opening set in a tiebreak. Djokovic responded strongly and won the second set 7-5. He then took the third set 6-4 and appeared to have turned the match around after going two sets to one ahead.

Navone refused to let the match slip away. He raised his level in the fourth set and won it 6-2. The Argentine then dominated the deciding set, taking it 6-1 to complete the biggest upset of his career.
