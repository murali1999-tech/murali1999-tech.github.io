Cardinals vs Giants: Shay Whitcomb Walk-Off Gives SF Giants 5-4 Win

Latest baseball updates

The San Francisco Giants defeated the St. Louis Cardinals 5-4 in an exciting 11-inning game at Oracle Park on Monday night. Shay Whitcomb delivered the winning hit in the bottom of the 11th inning, giving the SF Giants a dramatic walk-off victory.

Logan Webb started for San Francisco and put together a strong performance. He worked six innings, allowing one run on three hits while striking out eight. The Giants gave Webb a lead, but the Cardinals continued to fight and eventually forced extra innings.

Alec Burleson provided the Cardinals with one of the game's biggest moments when he launched a solo home run off Webb in the sixth inning. The homer traveled 431 feet into McCovey Cove and was Burleson's 22nd home run of the season.

The Giants took a 3-1 lead into the later innings, but St. Louis rallied. José Fermín's sacrifice fly tied the game in the ninth inning, and Leo Bernal put the Cardinals ahead 4-3 in the 10th.

San Francisco answered in the bottom of the 10th when Bryce Eldridge delivered a game-tying single. That sent the game to the 11th inning with both teams still fighting for the win.

Jason Foley kept the Cardinals off the scoreboard in the top of the 11th. San Francisco then had its chance, and Whitcomb finished the game with an infield single that scored automatic runner Nate Furman.

The 5-4 victory was an important moment for the Giants, while the loss was costly for a Cardinals team trying to remain in the National League wild-card race. San Francisco finished the night with 10 hits, while St. Louis had nine.

Rafael Devers also had a productive game for the Giants, collecting two doubles, two walks and an RBI. Bryce Eldridge drove in three runs and continued to show why the young first baseman is attracting attention.

For the Cardinals, Burleson was a major offensive contributor. His home run accounted for one of St. Louis' runs, while the team repeatedly pressured the Giants bullpen before ultimately falling in extra innings.

The Giants and Cardinals continue their three-game series in San Francisco. Fans searching for Giants game information, SF Giants scores, Logan Webb updates, Shay Whitcomb news or Cardinals results will have plenty to follow as the series continues.

The latest game also highlighted the importance of extra-inning baseball. With the automatic runner starting at second base, one successful bunt, hit or defensive play can quickly decide the result. San Francisco executed that situation better when Whitcomb came through in the 11th.

For the Giants, the biggest positives were Webb's bounce-back outing, Eldridge's production and Whitcomb's game-winning hit. Webb had struggled in his previous start but responded with six effective innings against St. Louis.

The Cardinals will now look to respond in the next game of the series. San Francisco will try to carry the momentum from its walk-off win into the following matchup.
