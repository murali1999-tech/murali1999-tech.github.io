Ben Shelton vs Frances Tiafoe: US Open 2026

Ben Shelton and Frances Tiafoe delivered one of the biggest all-American matches of the 2026 US Open when they met in the men's singles semifinal at Arthur Ashe Stadium. The match attracted huge attention because both players were trying to reach their first Grand Slam final and keep American hopes alive at the US Open.

Ben Shelton entered the semifinal after producing one of the most important victories of his young career. The American defeated defending champion Carlos Alcaraz in a dramatic five-set quarterfinal that finished at 3:33 a.m. The victory sent Shelton into his third Grand Slam semifinal and created another major chapter in his growing tennis career.

Frances Tiafoe also arrived in the semifinal after an extraordinary comeback. Tiafoe defeated Alex Michelsen in five sets after falling two sets behind. He eventually won 5-7, 3-6, 7-5, 6-3, 7-6, completing a remarkable turnaround in Arthur Ashe Stadium.

The Shelton vs Tiafoe rivalry has become one of the most interesting American matchups in modern tennis. Before their 2026 US Open meeting, Shelton led their overall head-to-head record 3-1. Their previous US Open meetings had produced one victory each, with Shelton winning in 2023 and Tiafoe winning in 2024.

There was much more than a place in the final at stake. American men's tennis has been waiting for a Grand Slam singles champion since Andy Roddick won the 2003 US Open. The winner of Shelton vs Tiafoe would move one step closer to ending that long drought.

Shelton maintained his composure late in the match, while Tiafoe struggled to close out the final points. A double fault on match point ended the contest and sent Shelton into the final.
