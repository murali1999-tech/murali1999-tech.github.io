Buckcherry Members and Crew Safe After Fatal South Carolina Accident

Latest update: Rock band Buckcherry's tour bus was involved in a fatal multi-vehicle accident on Interstate 26 in Spartanburg County, South Carolina, on August 21, 2026. Rock Band Tour Bus Crash: What Happened?

A serious highway accident involving the tour bus carrying rock band Buckcherry has drawn widespread attention. The incident happened during the band's ongoing tour in South Carolina. According to reports, the bus was traveling on Interstate 26 in Spartanburg County during the early morning hours of Friday, August 21, 2026, when a multi-vehicle collision occurred ahead of the band's vehicle.

The accident was fatal, with authorities confirming that one person died. However, Buckcherry confirmed that its musicians and touring crew were safe and unharmed. The band expressed condolences to the family and loved ones of the person who lost their life.

Buckcherry Tour Bus Accident

The Buckcherry tour bus was involved in the aftermath of a multi-vehicle collision on I-26. Reports indicate that the crash happened at approximately 2 a.m. in Spartanburg County. Authorities investigated a sequence of events involving multiple vehicles and a person who had exited a vehicle following an earlier crash.

The incident caused significant disruption on the interstate, with eastbound lanes closed for several hours while authorities investigated the crash and cleared the roadway. Reports said the lanes later reopened.

Are Buckcherry Members Safe?

Yes. Buckcherry reported that all members of the band and its touring crew were safe and unharmed following the incident. The band shared an update with fans after the crash and acknowledged the tragic loss of life connected with the collision.

The band's current lineup includes vocalist Josh Todd, guitarists Stevie D and Billy Rowe, bassist Kelly LeMieux and drummer Francis Ruiz. Reports following the crash stated that none of the musicians or touring personnel were injured.

Who Is Buckcherry?

Buckcherry is an American hard rock band known for songs including "Crazy Bitch" and "Lit Up." The group has maintained a touring presence over many years and continues to perform for rock audiences.

The band was traveling as part of its Roar Like Thunder Tour when the South Carolina accident occurred. The tour supports the group's 2025 album of the same name.

What Happened on Interstate 26?

According to reports citing authorities, the fatal sequence began after an earlier single-vehicle crash. A person exited a disabled vehicle and was later struck by another vehicle. The collision occurred ahead of Buckcherry's tour bus, which became involved in the resulting multi-vehicle incident.

The circumstances surrounding the crash were being investigated. Initial reports emphasized that the fatal collision occurred ahead of the band's bus, rather than reporting injuries to Buckcherry members or their crew.
