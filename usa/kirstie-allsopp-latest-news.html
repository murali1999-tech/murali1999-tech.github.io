Kirstie Allsopp airlifted to hospital after Devon beach accident

Kirstie Allsopp has been receiving messages of support after a serious accident during a family birthday trip in Devon. The television presenter revealed that she was taken to hospital by air ambulance after slipping on a muddy section of a steep coastal path near Welcombe Mouth.

Allsopp, who turned 55 on August 31, 2026, had been spending time with her family as part of a birthday tradition. She explained that the family normally enjoys a picnic at the beach, a swim and a walk back to their home at Meadowgate for cake. This year, however, the outing ended with an emergency response after she lost her footing.

The presenter said she slipped on a muddy patch on the steep path while returning from the beach. Her son Oscar ran to get help, and emergency teams were eventually able to locate her. The rescue involved the RNLI and an air ambulance service before she was taken to hospital for treatment.

Allsopp later shared photographs from the incident and her hospital stay with followers. The images showed her in an ambulance and later receiving treatment in hospital. She also shared an image of her injured leg and explained that doctors had operated on it.

The television presenter praised the people who came to her assistance and the hospital staff who cared for her. She described the medical team as kind and caring and said she felt extremely fortunate that help was able to reach her quickly.

She has also indicated that her recovery will involve a medical boot and crutches for some time. Despite the accident, Allsopp remained positive about getting back to work. She pointed out that much of her work can be carried out while using crutches, suggesting that she is already thinking about returning to her normal routine.

The accident happened during a particularly busy period for Allsopp professionally. She has been a familiar face on British television for many years, particularly through Location, Location, Location, the Channel 4 property programme she has presented with Phil Spencer since the programme began in 2000.

Her television work has extended beyond property programmes. Allsopp has also become closely associated with crafting and home-related programmes, where her interest in handmade projects, interiors and traditional skills has become a major part of her public profile.

Channel 4 has already confirmed that Kirstie's Handmade Christmas will return for the 2026 festive season. The broadcaster announced in July that two new series had been commissioned for the 2026 and 2027 festive periods. The 2026 series is planned as a five-part daytime competition featuring different craft challenges.

The programme is expected to feature Christmas wreath making, homemade Christmas cakes, handcrafted toys and other creative projects. Allsopp is due to host the series and demonstrate practical craft ideas that viewers can try at home. Her latest accident could therefore become relevant to her schedule as she recovers, although there has been no indication that the programme has been cancelled.

Allsopp's career has often combined property, interiors, family life and crafts. That mix has helped make her one of the recognisable presenters associated with British lifestyle television. Her straightforward approach and enthusiasm for handmade projects have also given her a substantial following beyond her property programmes.

This is not the first serious fall Allsopp has experienced in recent years. In 2023, she was forced to step away from filming Location, Location, Location after falling down stairs at a pub during filming. She later described that accident as an extremely frightening and painful experience, although she did not suffer broken bones on that occasion.

The latest accident appears to have been more serious because her leg required an operation. However, Allsopp's own comments have remained reassuring. Rather than concentrating only on the difficulties ahead, she has repeatedly expressed gratitude toward the people who helped her and the medical professionals who treated her.

Her family also played an important part in the emergency response. According to her account, Oscar ran for help after the accident. The difficult coastal location meant that finding and reaching her required coordination between several people and emergency organisations.

The incident has prompted many people to send messages wishing the presenter a quick recovery. Her decision to share photographs and details of what happened has also given followers an update about her condition rather than leaving people to speculate about the accident.

For now, the main focus is expected to be recovery. Allsopp has said that she will be using a boot and crutches while her leg heals. She has also made clear that she feels lucky to have received emergency assistance so quickly.

Her latest health update is therefore less about cancelling her professional plans and more about adapting temporarily while she recovers. With several television and crafting commitments associated with her name, fans will likely be watching for further updates about when she expects to return to filming and other public appearances.
