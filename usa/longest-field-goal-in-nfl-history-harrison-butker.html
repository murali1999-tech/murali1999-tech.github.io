Longest Field Goal in NFL History

Harrison Butker, NFL Kicking Records & Memorable Long-Range Field Goals

The longest field goal in NFL history is one of the most impressive achievements in professional football. Kicking a football through the uprights from more than 60 yards requires exceptional leg strength, accuracy, timing, technique and confidence. Harrison Butker is among the NFL's most respected long-distance kickers and has produced several memorable kicks during his career.

What Is the Longest Field Goal in NFL History?

The NFL has witnessed a number of extraordinary long-distance field goals. The official record has changed over the decades as kickers have benefited from improved training, equipment, kicking techniques and playing conditions.

The record-breaking kick currently associated with the NFL's longest field goal is a 66-yard field goal by Justin Tucker of the Baltimore Ravens, made against the Detroit Lions in 2021. It surpassed the previous 64-yard mark.

NFL Record:
Justin Tucker — 66-yard field goal, 2021.

That kick became one of the most famous moments in NFL kicking history. The field goal was made as time expired and gave Baltimore a dramatic victory. The kick also demonstrated how modern NFL kickers have pushed the limits of what is considered possible from long range.

Harrison Butker and Long Field Goals

Harrison Butker is one of the NFL's elite kickers and has built a reputation for accuracy and range. As the Kansas City Chiefs' kicker, Butker has played an important role in many high-pressure games, including postseason contests.

Butker entered the NFL after playing college football at Georgia Tech. He was selected by Kansas City in the seventh round of the 2017 NFL Draft and quickly became an important part of the Chiefs' special-teams unit.

Although Butker does not hold the official NFL record for the longest field goal, his ability to convert long attempts makes him one of the most dangerous kickers in the league.

Harrison Butker's Longest Field Goal

Butker has successfully converted field goals from well beyond 60 yards during his NFL career. His powerful kicking leg allows Kansas City to consider long-range attempts in situations where many teams would choose to punt or attempt a fourth-down play.

A long field goal is not simply about leg strength. The kicker must strike the ball cleanly, maintain balance and generate the correct trajectory. The snap, hold and protection must also be executed perfectly.

How Far Can an NFL Kicker Kick?

Modern NFL kickers are capable of attempting field goals from 60 yards or more. However, distance alone does not determine whether a kick is successful. Wind, temperature, altitude, field conditions and pressure can all influence the result.

A 50-yard field goal is already considered a significant kick, while attempts from 60 yards or longer are generally regarded as extremely difficult. A successful 65-yard-plus field goal requires a combination of exceptional power and accuracy.

Justin Tucker's 66-Yard Record

Justin Tucker's 66-yard field goal remains one of the defining long-range kicking moments in NFL history. The kick came against Detroit in a game that was decided by a field goal at the end of regulation.

The kick was particularly memorable because it came under significant game pressure. The ball traveled through the uprights from a distance that would have been considered extraordinary in earlier eras of professional football.

Matt Prater's 64-Yard Field Goal

Before Tucker broke the record, Matt Prater held the NFL record with a 64-yard field goal for the Denver Broncos in 2013. The kick was made at Denver's high-altitude stadium, where thinner air can help a football travel farther.

Prater's kick became an important benchmark for NFL kickers. For several years, 64 yards represented the maximum distance achieved in an official NFL game.

Why Stadium Conditions Matter

Stadium conditions can make a significant difference to a long field goal. Altitude, wind direction, temperature and humidity can affect the flight of the football.

High-altitude stadiums can provide an advantage for kickers because the air is less dense. Strong winds can either help or hurt a kick depending on their direction and speed.

Harrison Butker's Kicking Technique

Successful NFL kickers develop highly repeatable techniques. The approach to the football, plant foot, hip rotation, contact point and follow-through all need to work together.

Butker is known for his powerful leg and consistent approach. His ability to remain calm during high-pressure situations has helped him become an important part of Kansas City's success.

Field Goals in Important NFL Games

A field goal can have a much greater impact when the game is close. A kicker may be asked to attempt a long kick late in the fourth quarter, sometimes with the result of the game depending entirely on the outcome.

For this reason, kickers often receive significant attention during playoff games. A successful kick can become one of the defining moments of a season.

Longest Field Goal vs Longest Successful Kick

The longest field goal record refers to an official successful field goal in an NFL game. Kickers may sometimes attempt even longer kicks during practice, warmups or unofficial competitions, but those attempts do not count toward the official NFL record.

This distinction is important when comparing different kicking achievements. Only qualifying official game attempts are included in the NFL's historical records.

Could the NFL Record Be Broken Again?

It is possible that the NFL's 66-yard record could eventually be broken. Kickers continue to improve their physical conditioning and technique, while teams are increasingly willing to attempt long field goals when the situation allows.

A future record attempt could depend on the kicker's range as well as weather conditions. A strong kicker playing in favorable wind or at altitude could have an opportunity to challenge the record.

Harrison Butker and the Chiefs

Harrison Butker has been an important part of the Kansas City Chiefs' special teams for years. His kicking has contributed to regular-season victories and major postseason moments.

For the Chiefs, having a kicker with long-range capability can influence fourth-down decisions. Coaches may consider attempting a field goal from a distance that would be outside the comfortable range of many other kickers.

Why Long Field Goals Are Difficult

A long field goal requires the football to travel a considerable distance while still reaching enough height to clear the defensive line and pass between the uprights. A kick that is slightly inaccurate can miss even when it has enough power.

The pressure is also significant. The snapper, holder and kicker must work as a single unit. A small mistake can turn a potential game-winning kick into a miss.

Final Thoughts

The longest field goal in NFL history is currently the 66-yard kick by Justin Tucker. Harrison Butker, meanwhile, remains one of the league's most talented and reliable long-distance kickers.

As kicking techniques continue to develop, NFL fans can expect more attempts from extraordinary distances. Every successful 60-plus-yard field goal adds another memorable chapter to the history of professional football.
