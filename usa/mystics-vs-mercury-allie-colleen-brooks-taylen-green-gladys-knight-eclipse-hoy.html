Eclipse Hoy

"Eclipse hoy" means "eclipse today" in Spanish and is a common search phrase when people want to know whether an eclipse is happening on the current date. Eclipse visibility depends heavily on location, date and local time. A solar eclipse occurs when the Moon passes between Earth and the Sun, while a lunar eclipse happens when Earth moves between the Sun and Moon.

For August 2026, astronomy observers are particularly interested in the lunar eclipse occurring around August 27-28. The event is a deep partial lunar eclipse rather than a total lunar eclipse. The exact viewing circumstances differ by location, so people searching for eclipse today, eclipse tonight or lunar eclipse tonight should check local eclipse times.

A lunar eclipse is safe to observe with the unaided eye. During a deep lunar eclipse, part of the Moon can become noticeably darker or reddish as it moves through Earth's shadow. The red appearance comes from sunlight filtered through Earth's atmosphere.
