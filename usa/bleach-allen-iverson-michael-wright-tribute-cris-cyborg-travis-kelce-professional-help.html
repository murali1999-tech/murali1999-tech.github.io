Cris Cyborg Latest Fight

MMA star Cris Cyborg remains one of the biggest names in women's combat sports after another major victory in the Professional Fighters League. Cyborg defeated Ketlen Vieira by unanimous decision at PFL Tampa on August 22, 2026.

Cris Cyborg defeated Ketlen Vieira
Unanimous Decision — PFL Tampa

The five-round fight was competitive, with Vieira making Cyborg work throughout the contest. The judges ultimately scored the fight 49-46, 48-47 and 49-46 in Cyborg's favor.

The fight had an unusual championship situation because Vieira missed the featherweight limit by a significant margin and was therefore ineligible to win the title. Cyborg nevertheless remained committed to competing and successfully defended her standing in the division.

Cyborg's Future

Cyborg has indicated that retirement is not necessarily immediate. Following the fight, she continued discussing major potential matchups and expressed interest in high-profile combat sports challenges.

She has also continued to pursue a potential boxing matchup with Claressa Shields. Cyborg's career has already included championships across major MMA organizations, making her one of the most accomplished female fighters in the sport's history.

Mental Health and Professional Athletes

Athletes can experience many of the same emotional difficulties as anyone else, while also dealing with unique pressures. Injuries, performance expectations, career uncertainty, travel schedules and constant public attention can all affect wellbeing.

Professional athletes increasingly speak about therapy and mental health in order to reduce stigma and encourage people to seek appropriate help when needed. Kelce's comments fit into a wider trend of sports figures discussing emotional wellbeing more openly.

Professional mental health support can include talking with a licensed therapist, counselor or other qualified professional. Anyone experiencing persistent emotional distress should consider speaking with an appropriate healthcare professional.
