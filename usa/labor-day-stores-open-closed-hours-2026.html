What’s Open on Labor Day 2026? Store Hours for Costco, Walmart, Target, Sam’s Club, USPS, UPS and More

Labor Day is one of the most popular holidays in the United States, marking the unofficial end of summer and giving millions of people a chance to relax, travel, shop and spend time with family. Many Americans also use the holiday to take advantage of major sales offered by retailers across the country. Because of this, one of the most searched questions every year is simple: what stores are open on Labor Day?

Most major retailers remain open on Labor Day, although some operate with reduced holiday hours. Walmart is generally open and many locations continue regular schedules, making it a convenient option for groceries, household items and last-minute purchases. Target stores are also typically open, while Walgreens remains available in many locations for pharmacy and convenience needs.

Costco is one of the few major retailers that traditionally closes on Labor Day. Customers planning warehouse shopping are advised to check local schedules before visiting. Sam’s Club stores often remain open but may follow holiday timings, so checking official hours can save a trip.

Kroger, Whole Foods, Trader Joe’s, Aldi, Marshalls, TJ Maxx and many other grocery and department stores generally operate during the holiday, though individual store hours may vary by location. Restaurants also remain open in most cities, making Labor Day a popular day for dining out.

Many people also ask whether mail services operate on Labor Day. USPS generally observes the federal holiday, meaning regular mail delivery and post office operations are suspended. UPS and FedEx may offer limited services depending on package type and region, but standard deliveries are often affected.

Banks are usually closed because Labor Day is a federal holiday. However, online banking, ATMs and digital services remain available. Liquor store openings depend on state and local regulations, so hours vary across the country.

Labor Day itself honors the contributions and achievements of American workers. The holiday became a federal observance in 1894 and has since become associated with family gatherings, parades, sports events and shopping sales. Major retailers often offer discounts on electronics, furniture, clothing, appliances and seasonal products.

Before heading out, it is always a good idea to check local store websites or call ahead because hours can differ from one location to another. While most stores welcome customers during Labor Day, some may reduce operating hours.

The holiday also marks the transition into autumn for many Americans, with schools reopening, football season beginning and communities celebrating the final days of summer. Whether shopping, traveling or relaxing at home, Labor Day remains one of the most recognized holidays in the United States.
