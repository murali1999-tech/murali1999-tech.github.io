Sam Darnold Injury Update: Seahawks Beat Patriots 13-10 as Drew Lock Steps In

Latest NFL updates

The Seattle Seahawks opened the 2026 NFL season with a dramatic 13-10 comeback victory over the New England Patriots at Lumen Field, but the biggest concern for Seattle was the early exit of starting quarterback Sam Darnold.

Darnold suffered a hip injury during Seattle's opening drive after he was sacked by former Seahawks defensive lineman Dre'Mont Jones. He was able to get up after the play but was clearly limping and eventually went to the medical tent before heading to the locker room.

Sam Darnold was initially listed as questionable to return, but the Seahawks later ruled him out for the remainder of the game. He completed one of two passes for 13 yards before leaving the contest.

The early injury immediately raised questions from Seahawks fans about what happened to Sam Darnold, whether he is seriously injured and whether he will be available for Seattle's next game.

The first medical news was encouraging. Initial X-rays and a CT scan did not show a hip fracture, according to reports following the game. Seahawks coach Mike Macdonald said the team received good news, although further testing was still needed.

Darnold is scheduled for an MRI to provide a clearer picture of the injury. That means it is too early to say exactly how long he will be out or whether he will start Seattle's next game.

For now, the Seahawks have reason to be hopeful because a fracture was ruled out by the initial testing. The MRI should give the team a much better idea about the seriousness of the hip problem and his recovery timeline.

When Darnold left, Drew Lock took over at quarterback. Lock had a difficult start as Seattle's offense struggled to generate points, but he became much more effective in the fourth quarter when the Seahawks needed him most.

Lock finished the game completing 16 of 22 passes for 187 yards and a touchdown. His biggest moment came in the fourth quarter when he connected with Jaxon Smith-Njigba for a 45-yard touchdown that tied the game at 10-10.

Jason Myers then kicked the winning field goal from 26 yards, giving Seattle a 13-10 lead. The Seahawks defense protected that advantage and finished the comeback.

The Seattle defense played a major role in the victory. Patriots quarterback Drake Maye threw three interceptions in the fourth quarter, allowing the Seahawks to turn a game they had trailed for most of the night into a win.

Josh Jobe and Nehemiah Pritchett were among the Seahawks defensive backs involved in the big plays. Pritchett recorded an interception of Maye, while Jobe made the game-sealing interception in the end zone.

The Patriots had led 7-0 at halftime after Drake Maye connected with rookie Eli Raridon for a two-yard touchdown. New England extended the lead to 10-0 with a 50-yard field goal from Andy Borregales before Seattle finally began its comeback.

The Patriots' offense struggled badly in the fourth quarter. Maye, who was one of the most important players in the matchup, could not avoid Seattle's pass rush and secondary when the game was on the line.

The final score was Seattle Seahawks 13, New England Patriots 10. The result was especially meaningful because the game was a rematch of the previous season's Super Bowl, which Seattle won to become NFL champions.

The Seahawks entered the new season with high expectations, and the injury to Darnold immediately became the main story of the night. Darnold had been one of the most important players in Seattle's championship season and was expected to lead the offense again in 2026.

Last season Darnold threw for 4,048 yards, 25 touchdowns and 14 interceptions during the regular season. His performance helped Seattle win a franchise-record 14 games and reach the Super Bowl.

That is why Seahawks fans are watching the latest Sam Darnold injury update closely. The initial testing has provided some relief, but the MRI remains important for determining whether Darnold can play next week.

If Darnold cannot play, Drew Lock is the obvious option to start at quarterback. Lock has significant NFL experience and showed during the fourth quarter against New England that he can manage the offense when the team needs him.

Seattle also has Jalen Milroe available as the emergency third quarterback. However, the Seahawks would clearly prefer to have Darnold healthy and available rather than make an immediate change at the most important position.

There were also several important individual performances in the Seahawks vs Patriots game. Jaxon Smith-Njigba's 45-yard touchdown catch changed the momentum, while the Seattle defense repeatedly pressured Maye during the decisive final quarter.

The game also featured a difficult night for New England's offense. Maye had opportunities to extend the Patriots' lead, but Seattle's defense made the plays that mattered most. Three interceptions in one quarter ultimately decided the game.

For Seattle coach Mike Macdonald, the comeback was an important early test. The Seahawks trailed 10-0 and lost their starting quarterback, yet they still found a way to win in front of their home fans at Lumen Field.

The Seahawks now turn their attention to their next game against the Arizona Cardinals. Darnold's MRI and his ability to participate in practice will be closely watched during the week.

The Patriots will return home to prepare for their next game against the Pittsburgh Steelers. New England will have to improve its offensive execution after failing to score in the fourth quarter against Seattle.

For fans searching for answers about why Sam Darnold is not playing, the reason is the hip injury suffered on the sack by Dre'Mont Jones. He was ruled out for the game, but the initial tests did not show a fracture.

It is also too early to say that Sam Darnold is out for the season. There has been no indication from the Seahawks that the injury will automatically end his season. The MRI results should provide more information about the actual damage and expected recovery time.

The Seahawks will therefore wait for the additional medical evaluation before making decisions about their quarterback. Until those results are available, Drew Lock remains an important insurance option for Seattle.

The 13-10 win gives Seattle a positive start to its title defense, but the health of Sam Darnold will remain the biggest storyline surrounding the team. Seahawks fans will be hoping that the encouraging initial tests are followed by an equally positive MRI.
