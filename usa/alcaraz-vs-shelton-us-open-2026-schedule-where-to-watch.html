Alcaraz vs Shelton: US Open 2026 Quarterfinal, Schedule and Where to Watch

Watch the latest US Open tennis updates

Alcaraz vs Shelton is one of the biggest men's quarterfinal matches at the US Open 2026. Carlos Alcaraz and Ben Shelton meet at Arthur Ashe Stadium with a place in the semifinals at stake. The match brings together the defending champion and one of the leading American players in the tournament, creating a matchup that has attracted major attention from tennis fans around the world.

Carlos Alcaraz entered the US Open as the defending champion and the No. 2 seed. His return to New York has been closely followed after his injury absence, but the Spaniard has quickly shown why he remains one of the biggest names in men's tennis. He reached the quarterfinals by defeating Tommy Paul in straight sets in the fourth round.

Ben Shelton also arrived in the quarterfinal with plenty of momentum. The American defeated Stefanos Tsitsipas 6-2, 6-3, 6-4 in the fourth round. Shelton's performance was especially impressive because he did not face a break point and hit 11 aces. He also won 83 percent of his first-serve points during the match.

The Alcaraz vs Shelton matchup has an interesting history. Their most recent meeting before this US Open came at the 2025 French Open, where Shelton won the first set but Alcaraz came back to win the match in four sets. Alcaraz has also beaten Shelton at the 2024 Laver Cup. Shelton is therefore looking for his first victory over Alcaraz at this level, while Alcaraz wants to continue his strong record against American opponents at Grand Slam tournaments.

The contrast in styles makes Shelton vs Alcaraz particularly interesting. Shelton possesses one of the biggest serves in men's tennis and can produce enormous pace from the baseline. His left-handed forehand can create difficult angles, especially when he gets control of a rally early. Alcaraz, meanwhile, combines speed, variety, explosive forehands, drop shots and exceptional movement around the court.

For Shelton, serving well will be one of the most important parts of the match. Against a player such as Alcaraz, giving away easy break opportunities can quickly change the momentum. Shelton's ability to win short points with his serve and first forehand could be a major factor, especially on the fast hard court at the US Open.

Alcaraz will likely try to make the match more physical. His movement allows him to defend from difficult positions and turn defense into attack almost immediately. His drop shot is another weapon that can force Shelton forward and disrupt the American's preferred rhythm from behind the baseline.
