Ray LaMontagne Concert Stopped in Nashville After Onstage Collapse

Latest music and entertainment updates

Ray LaMontagne's concert at Nashville's Ryman Auditorium ended early on September 5 after the singer collapsed on stage during his performance. The incident happened while he was performing “Jolene” during his Trouble 20th Anniversary Tour.

According to reports from his team, LaMontagne was treated by local emergency medical personnel for heat-related dehydration and was doing well afterward. The unexpected moment worried fans who had gathered at the historic Nashville venue.

Earlier in the performance, LaMontagne had stopped during “Hannah” after appearing to lose his place. He apologized to the audience and continued with the show. Later, he briefly left the stage before returning to perform “Jolene.”

During the song, he suddenly went down on stage. Members of his team quickly came to his assistance and helped him away from the stage. The concert then ended before the planned set was completed.

LaMontagne's management thanked everyone who attended the Nashville show and said the singer was in good hands. He was scheduled to continue his tour in Louisville the following night.

The Nashville concert is part of LaMontagne's Trouble 20th Anniversary Tour, celebrating his acclaimed 2004 debut album. The album helped establish him as one of the best-known singer-songwriters in contemporary folk music.

Fans have since expressed concern and support for the musician online. The latest reports indicate that he was doing well after receiving treatment for heat-related dehydration.
