How to Apply for UGC NET 2026

Candidates can apply for UGC NET through the official NTA website once the registration portal becomes active. Applicants should carefully read the official notification before filling out the application form.

  1. Visit the official website.
  2. Click on the UGC NET registration link.
  3. Create a new account using valid details.
  4. Fill in personal and academic information.
  5. Upload required documents.
  6. Pay the application fee online.
  7. Submit and download confirmation page.

How to Download UGC NET Admit Card

The UGC NET admit card will be released online by NTA before the examination. Candidates must carry a printed copy of the hall ticket along with valid identification proof to the examination center.

  • Go to the official NTA website.
  • Open the UGC NET admit card link.
  • Enter application number and date of birth.
  • Click on submit button.
  • Download and print the admit card.

UGC NET Exam Pattern

The examination consists of two papers conducted in computer-based mode. Paper 1 tests teaching and research aptitude while Paper 2 focuses on the candidate’s selected subject.

Both papers are conducted in a single session. Candidates should prepare according to the latest syllabus and previous year question patterns for better performance.
