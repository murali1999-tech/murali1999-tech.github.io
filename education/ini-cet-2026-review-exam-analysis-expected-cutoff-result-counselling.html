INI CET 2026 Exam Review

According to student reactions and early analysis, the INI CET May 2026 paper was moderate to difficult in overall difficulty level. Many aspirants felt the clinical subjects carried a higher weightage than expected. Questions tested conceptual understanding instead of direct factual memory, making the examination slightly challenging for average candidates.

Subjects like Medicine, Surgery, Pathology, Pharmacology, and Microbiology had several integrated and application-based questions. Some candidates found the paper lengthy, especially due to complex clinical scenarios and image-based interpretations.

Difficulty Level Analysis

  • Medicine – Moderate to Difficult
  • Surgery – Moderate
  • Pathology – Moderate
  • Pharmacology – Moderate to Difficult
  • Anatomy – Easy to Moderate
  • Physiology – Moderate
  • Microbiology – Difficult for some candidates

Overall, toppers stated that strong conceptual clarity and revision of previous year questions helped significantly in attempting the examination effectively.

Expected Cutoff for INI CET 2026

Experts believe the expected cutoff for top AIIMS branches may remain high due to intense competition. Candidates scoring well above the average are expected to secure better ranks in clinical specialties.

Expected Percentile Range:
General Category: 96–99 Percentile
OBC Category: 92–96 Percentile
SC/ST Category: 85–92 Percentile

The actual cutoff may vary depending on paper difficulty level, number of candidates, and overall performance trends.

INI CET 2026 Result Date

AIIMS is expected to release the INI CET May 2026 result online through the official portal. Candidates can check their rank, percentile, and qualifying status using their login credentials.

  1. Visit the official AIIMS examination website.
  2. Open the INI CET 2026 result link.
  3. Enter your registration details.
  4. Submit the login information.
  5. Download the scorecard PDF.
  6. Take a printout for counselling use.

INI CET 2026 Counselling Process

After the declaration of results, AIIMS will begin the counselling and seat allocation process. Qualified candidates must register online and select their preferred institutions and branches during choice filling.

Seat allotment will be based on rank, reservation category, seat availability, and candidate preferences. Multiple rounds of counselling are expected, including open rounds if seats remain vacant.

Live Updates for Aspirants

Students are advised to regularly monitor the official AIIMS portal for the latest notices regarding result announcements, counselling schedules, document verification, and seat allotment updates.

Several candidates also discussed that revision of mock tests and previous year AIIMS questions played a major role in improving performance. Time management during the examination was another key factor highlighted by successful aspirants.

Preparation Tips for Future INI CET Aspirants

  • Focus on conceptual understanding instead of rote learning.
  • Practice integrated clinical case-based questions.
  • Solve previous year AIIMS and INI CET papers.
  • Revise short notes and important images regularly.
  • Attempt grand tests to improve speed and accuracy.
