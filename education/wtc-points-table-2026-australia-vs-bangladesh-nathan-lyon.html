Nathan Lyon in Australia vs Bangladesh

Nathan Lyon returned to Australia's Test XI for the Bangladesh series after missing the latter stages of the Ashes because of a hamstring injury. His return reunited Australia's experienced bowling group of Lyon, Pat Cummins, Mitchell Starc and Josh Hazlewood.

However, Australia's defeat in Darwin has generated discussion around the future composition of the Test side. Lyon's position has come under scrutiny after a difficult match, with Australia's selectors also having emerging spin options available. Reports have highlighted competition from younger spinners including Corey Rocchiccioli and Todd Murphy.

Despite the criticism following the defeat, Lyon remains one of Australia's most experienced Test bowlers and has played a major role in Australia's success in the World Test Championship era.

Why the WTC Points Table Matters

The WTC points table is different from conventional cricket league tables because teams are ranked by their percentage of available points. This means a team that plays fewer matches can remain above another team despite having fewer total points.

The top two teams at the end of the WTC league cycle qualify for the World Test Championship final. Therefore, every Test result can have an impact on the race for the final.

Australia's WTC Position After Bangladesh Win

Australia remain at or near the top of the WTC standings despite the defeat because they had built a substantial lead earlier in the cycle. Their previous record of seven wins in eight Tests gave them 84 points and an 87.50 PCT before the Darwin match.

The defeat reduces Australia's percentage of points won and gives Bangladesh a major boost in the WTC race. Bangladesh had already been performing strongly in the current cycle, sitting fourth with 28 points and a 58.33 PCT before the Australia series.

World Test Championship Points System

  • Win: 12 points
  • Draw: 4 points per team
  • Tie: 6 points per team
  • Loss: 0 points
  • Teams are ranked according to percentage of points won.
  • Slow over-rate penalties can affect a team's points.
