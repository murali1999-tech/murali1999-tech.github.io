How to Check CBSE Class 12 Results

Students can follow simple steps to access their board examination results online quickly and safely.

  • Visit the official CBSE results website
  • Click on Class 12 Result link
  • Enter roll number and required details
  • Submit information carefully
  • Download and print the scorecard

Students are advised to keep admit cards ready before checking results online to avoid delays.
