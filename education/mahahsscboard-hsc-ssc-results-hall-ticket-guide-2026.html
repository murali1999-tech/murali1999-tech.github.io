About mahahsscboard.in

The Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (MSBSHSE) is one of the largest education boards in India. The official website, mahahsscboard.in, provides important information and online services for SSC and HSC students across Maharashtra.

Lakhs of students visit the portal every year to check examination notifications, download hall tickets, view exam timetables, access syllabus documents, and check board examination results. The website is especially important during result season when students search for direct links to access their marks and scorecards.

The board conducts SSC examinations for Class 10 students and HSC examinations for Class 12 students. Apart from results, the portal also offers circulars, updates for schools and colleges, migration certificate procedures, duplicate mark sheet requests, and rechecking application information.

How to Check SSC and HSC Results

Maharashtra Board results are generally announced online through the official website and partner result portals. Students need their roll number and mother’s first name to access the scorecard.

Follow these steps:

  • Visit the official Maharashtra Board website.
  • Click on SSC Result or HSC Result link.
  • Enter your roll number correctly.
  • Type your mother’s first name.
  • Submit the details.
  • Download or print the marksheet.

Students should verify all details carefully after downloading the provisional marksheet. Any mistakes in name, marks, or subject details should be reported immediately to the respective school authorities.

Hall Tickets and Exam Timetable

Before board examinations, students can access hall ticket details and official timetables through the board website. Schools usually download and distribute hall tickets to students, but the official portal provides important updates and instructions.

The exam timetable section contains:

  • Subject-wise exam dates
  • Morning and afternoon session timings
  • Exam day instructions
  • Practical examination schedules
  • Supplementary exam dates

Students should regularly check the official website for revised schedules or important notices related to examinations.

Syllabus and Study Material

Maharashtra Board periodically updates syllabus patterns and examination structures. Students preparing for SSC and HSC board exams should download the latest syllabus only from official sources.

The board website helps students understand:

  • Updated syllabus chapters
  • Deleted topics
  • Question paper patterns
  • Internal assessment rules
  • Passing criteria and grading system

Students preparing for competitive exams often use Maharashtra Board textbooks as a strong foundation for state-level entrance examinations.
