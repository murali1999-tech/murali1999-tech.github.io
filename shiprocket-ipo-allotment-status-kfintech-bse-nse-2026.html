How to Check Shiprocket IPO Allotment Status on KFintech

KFin Technologies is the registrar handling the IPO allotment process. Investors can use the KFintech IPO status portal to find out whether their application has been successful.

  1. Open the official KFintech IPO allotment status website.
  2. Select Shiprocket from the IPO selection field, if displayed.
  3. Choose the appropriate search method.
  4. You can generally use your PAN, application number or Demat account details.
  5. Enter the requested information carefully.
  6. Submit the form to view your Shiprocket IPO allotment status.

If shares have been allotted, the status should show the number of equity shares allocated to the applicant. If no shares have been allotted, the result will indicate that the application was not successful.

How to Check Shiprocket IPO Status Using PAN

Checking an IPO result using PAN is one of the simplest methods for investors. Visit the KFintech IPO status portal and select the PAN option. Enter the same PAN that was used while submitting the Shiprocket IPO application.

After submitting the information, investors can view the allotment result. Make sure the PAN details are entered correctly. Investors should avoid sharing their PAN, bank account information, UPI PIN or other confidential financial information with unofficial websites or individuals claiming to provide IPO allotment services.

Shiprocket IPO Allotment Status Through BSE

Investors may also check IPO application and allotment-related information through the BSE website. The exchange provides an IPO application status facility where investors can enter the required application information.

When using an exchange website, investors should make sure they are visiting the official BSE website and not a third-party page that imitates an exchange portal. Always verify the website address before entering personal information.

Shiprocket IPO Allotment Status Through NSE

The National Stock Exchange also provides facilities for investors to verify IPO bids and application-related information. Investors can use the NSE's official investor services and IPO-related pages to verify their application details.

If the allotment result is not immediately visible on one platform, investors can also check the registrar's portal. During periods of heavy traffic, IPO websites may temporarily load slowly or show errors. Waiting for a few minutes and trying again can help.

Shiprocket IPO Price and Issue Size

The Shiprocket IPO was offered at a price band of ₹92 to ₹97 per equity share. The total issue size was approximately ₹1,617.48 crore, consisting of a fresh issue along with an offer for sale.

The strong subscription response made the allotment process highly competitive. When an IPO receives significantly more applications than the number of shares available, many applicants may receive no shares even though they applied at the issue price.

What Happens After Shiprocket IPO Allotment?

After the basis of allotment is finalized, successful applicants generally receive the allotted shares in their Demat accounts. For applicants who do not receive shares, the amount blocked through the application process is released or unblocked according to the applicable process.

Investors who receive an allotment should monitor their Demat account and bank account. The expected Shiprocket listing date is August 19, 2026, when the shares are scheduled to begin trading on the stock exchanges.

Shiprocket IPO GMP and Listing Expectations

Grey market premium, commonly known as GMP, is often watched by investors before an IPO listing. Recent market reports indicated a positive grey market premium for Shiprocket, suggesting expectations of a premium listing. However, GMP is unofficial and can change quickly.

Investors should not treat GMP as a guaranteed listing price or guaranteed profit. The actual opening and trading price is determined by market demand and supply once the shares begin trading on the exchange.

Why Shiprocket IPO Allotment May Not Be Received

Not receiving an IPO allotment does not necessarily mean that there was an error with the application. When an IPO is heavily oversubscribed, the available shares are distributed according to the applicable category-wise allotment process.

Retail investors in particular may find that their application receives no shares when demand is substantially higher than the number of shares reserved for the category. In such cases, the blocked amount is generally released according to the IPO settlement process.
